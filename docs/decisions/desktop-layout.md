# Desktop layout (Melvin, 2026-11-12: "change the UI for the web, kinda like how Hevy's is just a regular web app")

Part of the decision record. [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) has the rules that apply to every change and the list of topics. Record decisions about this topic here, in the same change as the code.

- **Why.** People lift with their phone and plan at their desk. On a laptop, `/app/` was a phone: one 512 px column, a bottom tab bar and bottom sheets. Hevy's website is a normal sidebar web app for routines, history and progress, while its phone app is the whole product. Analysis and options: [A desktop web UI for Liftsmith](https://claude.ai/artifact/Kpv18JpGyw3yEUeHs6bETZ).
- **Option A, one app with a desktop layout** (Melvin chose it, 2026-11-12), not a separate web shell or a separate web app, because a second UI would drift from the phone apps and double every feature, test and translation. Every screen is built once; desktop is a different shape of the same screens.
- **When it's on** (`src/lib/layout.ts`): on the web only (never inside the Android or iOS app), on a window at least 1,024 px wide, with a mouse or trackpad as the main pointer (`(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`). It follows the window live. **Tablets keep the phone layout for now** (Melvin, 2026-11-12), in the apps and in a touch browser; an iPad with a trackpad in a browser counts as a desktop. The layout is one attribute, `data-layout="desktop"` on `<html>`, and screens style for it with the `desk:` Tailwind variant (`globals.css`); never check the width in a component.
- **What changes on desktop** (stage 1, the shell):
  - A left sidebar (`Sidebar` in `App.tsx`, 15rem) replaces the bottom tab bar. It holds the tab bar's tabs with the app's name on top, then **Programs** and **Tools** (Melvin picked them, 2026-11-13: desks are where people plan and look things up; top-level there, so they show no back link), then **Coaching** for accounts that coach or are coached (a role picked, an invite out, a client or a coach; `coaching` in `useCoachNotes`, kept on the device, with a dot for unread messages), and **Settings** at the foot, lit on the screens under it (Melvin, 2026-11-13: "the settings button and the coaching tabs should show up on the left side navigation"). One control, one destination: on desktop, Today hides its gear (`desk:hidden`) and its program chip stops being a button (it still says the program and day), Progress hides All tools, Settings › Training hides its Program row, and Settings hides its Coaching row while the sidebar has Coaching. Gyms, Account, Log a past workout and Feedback stay where they are (rarely used). Phones keep the five tabs, the gear and the row. It sets `--nav-h` to 0 and `--side-w` to its width; bars pinned to the bottom of a screen (Today's Start workout, the stretch picker) and toasts start after it (`.after-side`, `.toast-wrap`).
  - Pages are wider (`max-w-3xl`) with more padding.
  - Every `Sheet` opens as a centred dialog, without the drag handle, and closes by ✕, clicking outside and Escape (no dragging).
  - **The live workout is a focused panel** (Melvin, 2026-11-12): full-screen screens (the workout, done, setup, feedback, Pro) have no sidebar and stay phone-width in the middle of the window.
- **Not on desktop:** the phone-only parts stay as they are on the web (camera and motion labs, haptics, the lock-screen notification, watch apps, Health Connect and Apple Health, the one-handed dock), and the hold-to-repeat and swipe rules don't change.
- **Tests:** `DesktopLayout.test.tsx` (sidebar, no second nav, the workout panel, phones and native apps keep the tab bar); the device sweep runs `Desktop Chrome` and `Desktop Safari` and checks the sidebar never covers the page; the accessibility run scans the main screens on desktop in dark and light, a sheet as a dialog, and the sidebar from the keyboard.
- **Stage 2, `/me/` retired into the app** (Melvin, 2026-11-12): Log a past workout is an app screen (`#/log`) on every platform, `/me/` redirects into the app, and the footer's "Your dashboard" link is gone. Details in [Web dashboard](web-dashboard.md).
- **Stage 3, wide screens** (Melvin, 2026-11-12):
  - Screens with a layout of their own get the full width on desktop (`WIDE` in `App.tsx`: Today, Body, Progress, Friends, Awards, the exercise page); every other screen keeps one comfortable column (`max-w-3xl`). Settings stays one column on purpose: it's a list of rows.
  - **Today:** the plan (length, extras, the exercise list) in the wider left column, the call, coach's notes, quote and quests on the right. On a phone the two groups stack in the old order.
  - **Body:** the recovery map on the left stays in view (sticky) while the muscle list scrolls beside it.
  - **Progress, Awards and the exercise page:** cards flow into two columns with `.desk-cols` (CSS columns, `globals.css`), read down the first column and then the second so the phone's order (what to do first) is kept; `.desk-span` runs a part across both (the header, next steps, the totals, the level card). A card never splits.
  - **Friends:** the feed beside challenges and the board (stage 2).
  - **Workout keyboard shortcuts** (`workout/KeyboardLog.tsx`): L logs the set (ending rest first, like the volume button), ↑ and ↓ change the reps, Shift with ↑ or ↓ the weight. Never while typing in a box, while a sheet is open, or with Ctrl, Cmd or Alt held; a held L logs once. The keys are named in one quiet line under Log set, on desktop only (`workout.keys`, six languages).
- **Stage 4, desktop-first workflows** (Melvin, 2026-11-12):
  - **Routine builder:** on desktop the editor opens as a wide dialog (`Sheet wide`, `desk:max-w-4xl`) with the routine on the left and the exercise browser always open on the right, so exercises go in one click each with no "Add exercise" step. Rows drag to a new place (HTML drag and drop, `moveItem` in `reorder.ts`, which keeps supersets linked); the up and down buttons stay for the keyboard. Phones are unchanged.
  - **Trainer mode:** the coach's client list is a grid of cards (two across, three on a wide window), and a client's report flows into two columns (`.desk-cols`), what to do first. Trainer is on `WIDE`.
