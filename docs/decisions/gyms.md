# Gyms: official gyms, the gym finder, gym pages and posters (Melvin, 2026-11-12)

Part of the decision record. [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) has the rules that apply to every change and the list of topics. Record decisions about this topic here, in the same change as the code.

Melvin, 2026-11-12, as part of following Hevy's growth playbook (organic growth, little spend, customer delight): gyms are customers too. Gyms register their location, we send them a poster or plastic stand with our name, logo, website and a QR code that says "Add this gym to your Liftsmith", gyms claim their gym in the app, and the QR code picks that gym and all its equipment. There are official (claimed, with a check mark) and unofficial gyms, a gym finder for the area where claimed gyms are more prominent, and a page per gym (lifters who train there, hours, map, address).

This builds on [Gym sharing](gym-sharing.md): lifters' own shared and listed **setups** (equipment and weights, about 1 km) stay as they are. This topic adds the **gyms themselves**. Built in four steps:

1. Gym directory and finder (built, below).
2. Gym pages in the app and on the website, plus city pages (built, below).
3. Claiming: the owner proves the gym is theirs and edits its details; the check mark (built, below).
4. Per-gym QR links, a free printable poster for every gym, and table stands mailed to claimed gyms with 20+ lifters from an admin queue (built, below). Shipping is Melvin's job.

## Step 1: the gym directory (built)

- **Real gyms** live in `gym_places` (`src/server/gymPlaces.ts`): name, address, city, region (provinces, US states and Australian states shortened, "BC", from Nominatim's `ISO3166-2-lvl4` code when it has one, else `SHORT_REGION` in `src/lib/regions.ts`; a place saved before 2026-11-12 with the long name, "Langley, British Columbia", is renamed with its address by a one-time update in `db.ts`, unless another city already has the short address), country, map position, opening hours, website, phone, where it came from, and later the owner. These are businesses on a public map, not lifter data, so the position is kept as the map has it (rounded to about 1 m).
- **Seeded from OpenStreetMap, not Google.** Google Maps' terms don't allow copying places out; OpenStreetMap's licence (ODbL) does, with credit. Every screen or page that shows OSM details says "Gym details © OpenStreetMap contributors". Test on 2026-11-12: Langley, BC gave 23 named places, 14 lifting gyms and 9 studios; about half had no street address and three had hours, and the city rec centres are tagged differently and were missed. So OSM is a starting point, and claiming is how a gym gets complete details.
- **Admin › Gyms** (`GymPlaces.tsx`, `/api/admin/gym-places/`): type a city ("Langley, BC") and Import. The server finds the area with Nominatim, then pulls every `leisure=fitness_centre` inside it from Overpass (inside the city's boundary when OSM has one, else its box), trying three Overpass servers in turn. Unnamed and private places are skipped. Running it again is safe (see Importing gyms below): gyms are matched by their OpenStreetMap id, only what changed is written, and a claimed gym is never touched. At most 5,000 places per import. The list pages 50 gyms at a time (a country can have thousands; search by name, city or province), official first, then the most lifters, and each gym's name opens its web page in a new tab (Melvin, 2026-11-12). It shows each gym's lifters and listed setups; staff can mark a place a gym or a studio, hide it, or (by API) delete it. Admins only; audited.
- **Importing gyms: speed, progress and the debug log** (Melvin, 2026-11-14: "importing gyms take a long time … which city is loading, estimated time left, any errors, additional debug information … idempotent … not overwriting any information on claimed gyms"). The import lives in `src/server/gymImport.ts`; the admin side in `src/components/admin/GymImport.tsx`.
  - *Why it was slow:* nearly all of an import is waiting on Overpass, and a busy Overpass server (they often answer 504 or hang) held a request for up to the route's five minutes before the next server was tried, one city at a time with a two-second gap. Overpass also sometimes answers 200 with a `remark` ("runtime error: Query timed out"), which was saved as a city with no gyms.
  - *Now:* each Overpass server gets 100 seconds (and the query tells Overpass to give up at 90 on its own), then the next is tried, all inside a 250-second budget; a `remark` error counts as a failure; the area Nominatim found is kept on the import row (`gym_imports.scope`), so a re-import skips that lookup (`fresh: true` looks it up again); and the admin page runs **two cities at a time**, each starting on a different Overpass server (`server`, rotated), the second a moment later so Nominatim (one request a second) isn't asked twice at once. Database reads run in parallel and writes go in batches of 500.
  - *Idempotent:* gyms are keyed on the map's own id (`source_ref`, `osm:<type>/<id>`, a unique index), so a re-import never makes a second copy. A row whose map data hasn't changed isn't written at all (`unchanged`), only changed fields are refreshed, and the update itself says `AND claimed_by = ''`, so a **claimed gym is never changed** by an import (we take the owner's word over the map's; counted as `kept`). A gym near a border falls inside two cities' areas: when the map gives it no city, a refresh keeps the city it already has instead of flipping it to whichever city was imported last (`guessed`). Addresses (city and gym slugs) never change on a re-import.
  - *Busy map servers* (Melvin, 2026-11-14, after the first "every country" run: "check the gym import and improve it"). That run did 65 cities in an hour and 22 failed: overpass-api.de answered in 24 to 43 seconds when it answered but was often at capacity (502, 429), maps.mail.ru answered 504 within a second two times in three, and overpass.private.coffee hung for the full 100 seconds 39 times out of 44, which alone cost about an hour of waiting. Now every request to a map server is kept with its outcome (`gym_imports.attempts`: ok, busy, timeout or error), and each import ranks the servers by how they did in the last 30 minutes: the one that answered most goes first, and one that mostly hangs ("slow") is only tried after a busy round (`planServers`). When every server still in the running says it's busy (429, 502, 503, 504), the import waits 8 seconds, then 16, 32 and 64, and asks again, up to five rounds inside its time limit, instead of failing; a server that hung or failed outright isn't asked again in that import. The log starts with the order and each server's recent record. A city that still fails because the servers were busy goes back in line, after 3 minutes and then 10 (`RETRY_AFTER_MS`, now on the server), before it counts as failed ("2 busy, trying again soon"); a place Nominatim doesn't know isn't retried. The debug log keeps those earlier failed tries (`triedAgain`).
  - *Imports run on the server* (Melvin, 2026-11-15: "get gym imports to run on the server"). A country's or every country's import is a job on the server (`src/server/gymImportJobs.ts`), so Admin › Gyms can be closed while it runs; a single city still imports right on the page. Starting one (`{action: "importJob", country, skipRecent, notify}`) writes the job (`gym_import_jobs`) and a row per city (`gym_import_items`: pending, running, waiting to retry, ok or failed, with its result), and the page only reads it (`?job=1`, every 4 seconds while it runs), so reopening the tab shows the live progress where it is. One job at a time (a second gets "Another import is running"). The work is done by `/api/cron/gym-imports/` (800 seconds a run): a worker takes the job's lease (`lease_until`, one worker per job, so calling it twice does nothing), imports two cities at a time with the same `importArea` as before (idempotent, claimed gyms never changed), writes each city as it lands, and starts no city that couldn't finish in its time; then it starts a fresh run of itself (`kickWorker`, a POST with `CRON_SECRET`, sent to the public site because vercel.app addresses sit behind Vercel's sign-in) and stops. The `gym-imports` Vercel Schedule also calls it every five minutes, so a hand-over that never arrived, a worker that crashed (its half-done cities go back in line) or a city waiting to retry is picked up within minutes; a tick with nothing to do is one query. A city whose map servers were busy goes back in line after 3 and then 10 minutes (`RETRY_AFTER_MS`), and a worker waits for one that comes due in its own time. **Stop** lets the cities being imported finish and starts nothing new; **Retry failed** starts a new job of the cities that failed or weren't reached; **Close** hides a finished job; **Download debug log** (`?job=<id>&log=1`) has every city with its result, its earlier busy tries and its step-by-step log from `gym_imports`. **Email me when it's done** (on by default) sends the admin who started it one email when the job finishes: cities done, gyms found, new and changed, and the failed cities with why. Jobs are kept 90 days, like import rows.
  - *Progress:* the cities being imported now, done of total with a bar, time left (the average so far spread over the cities left), gyms found, new and changed, and every error with the reason (which server, what it said). **Skip cities imported in the last 7 days** (on by default) lets a stopped or failed run carry on where it left off, and **Retry failed** re-runs only the cities that failed. **Download debug log** saves the whole run as JSON.
  - *Recent imports:* every import (failed ones too) leaves a row in `gym_imports` with a step-by-step log: Nominatim or the remembered area, each Overpass server tried with its time and answer, what was left out and why (unnamed, private, not a gym, repeated), and database read and write times with how many rows were new, changed, unchanged or claimed and left alone. Admin › Gyms lists the last hundred, each opens to its log, and Download all saves them. Rows are kept 90 days; they hold public map data and timings, nothing about a lifter.
- **Rec and community centres** (2026-11-12): OpenStreetMap tags most of them `leisure=sports_centre` or `amenity=community_centre`, which the first Langley import missed. Imports now ask for those too and keep one only when its name says there's a gym ("Recreation", "Rec Centre", "Leisure", "Fitness", "YMCA", "Wellness", "Athletic", "Gym") or its sport tag is fitness or weightlifting, so arenas and pools alone stay out. Gymnastics clubs are studios.
- **Major cities in one go** (Melvin, 2026-11-12: "add all gyms from all major cities in Canada", then "the same for the US"). Admin › Gyms has **Import Canada's major cities** (about 90: every city of about 100,000 or more plus each capital) and **Import the US's major cities** (about 200: the biggest cities plus some smaller capitals), listed in `src/server/majorCities.ts`. **Every Google Play country** (Melvin, 2026-11-14: "add every country's major city that the Google Play Store is connected to"): the other 94 Play countries (the ones in our six languages, `docs/decisions/google-play-review.md`) plus Cuba (Melvin, 2026-11-14: "I love them"; the app still isn't offered there, because our US-based service providers can't serve it, but its gyms are on the map and the website) have their biggest cities and capital in `src/server/worldCities.ts`, about twenty for the biggest countries and the capital alone for the smallest, written "City, Country" so Nominatim finds the right one; 97 countries and about 860 cities in all. Admin › Gyms picks a country from a list (with its city count) or imports every country in one go; at two cities at a time that's several hours, run on the server so the page can be closed (see Imports run on the server below). The admin page runs two city imports at a time (see Importing gyms below), shows progress and can be stopped; running it again refreshes. An import writes in batches (`savePlaces` reads what's there in a few queries), up to 5,000 places, with five minutes per city.
- **More details per gym** (Melvin, 2026-11-14: "add all the information possible about a gym location … gym hours and phone numbers … address information, whatever else is important").
  - *From the map first:* an import keeps everything useful OpenStreetMap has: the unit (`addr:unit`, "4-100 Main St"), postcode, phone (also `contact:mobile`), email, website, hours, Instagram and Facebook pages (a handle or a link, always stored as the site's own canonical address; anything else is dropped) and wheelchair access (yes, limited, no). New `gym_places` columns: `postcode`, `email`, `instagram`, `facebook`, `wheelchair`, `enriched_at`.
  - *Then the gym's own website* (Admin › Gyms › Fill in details from gym websites; `src/server/gymEnrich.ts`, `GymEnrich.tsx`, POST `{action: "enrich"}`). For each unclaimed, visible gym with a website that is missing something, most lifters first, it reads only the home page: its schema.org structured data first (a LocalBusiness or gym: phone, email, street address, postcode, opening hours turned into the map's `Mo-Fr 06:00-22:00` form, social pages from `sameAs`), then `tel:`, `mailto:` and Instagram and Facebook links on the page. An email is taken only on the gym's own domain (never the web designer's), and share buttons aren't social pages.
  - *Rules for reading sites:* only the gym's own site, never Google Maps, Yelp, Facebook or any directory (their terms forbid it); robots.txt is not read (Melvin, 2026-11-14, chose "Ignore robots.txt" over the recommended "keep following it": one public home page a month per gym, so a site that asks crawlers to stay away is still read); we say who we are in the user agent; 24 gyms a batch, 6 at a time, 8 seconds and 1.5 MB per page, at most once a month per gym (`enriched_at`). Only public addresses are fetched (no IP addresses, localhost, `.local` or `.internal`, ports other than 80 and 443), and every redirect is checked again, so an admin can't be tricked into reaching our own network.
  - *The site's icon* (Melvin, 2026-11-14: "download their favicons and we show it on their gym pages"). The same read also takes the website's icon, the sharpest square one it has (see "Sharper icons" below; it used to be the touch icon first, then the largest declared icon, then `/favicon.ico`); never SVG (it can carry script and we serve it from our own origin), at most 150 KB, and only what its first bytes say is PNG, ICO, GIF, JPEG or WebP. It's kept in `gym_photos` as kind `icon`, only for an unclaimed gym with no logo and no icon yet, and stands in as the gym's logo on its web page and in the app until an owner adds a logo (the owner's logo always wins; staff's Remove pictures takes it down too). `/api/gyms/photo/` names the type from the bytes. A gym without an icon counts as missing something, so it's read again after a month.
  - *Never over anything better:* only empty fields are filled (`CASE WHEN field = '' …`), a claimed gym is never touched (its owner's details win), and a later map import never blanks a field the website filled (`KEEP_IF_MAP_EMPTY`): the map wins only when it has a value.
  - *Shown:* gym pages list phone, email, website, Instagram and Facebook, the address with its postcode and wheelchair access, and their structured data carries the postcode, email and `sameAs`; the app's gym preview shows the same. Text in all six languages (`gp.*`).
- **A gym the map doesn't have** (`GymAdd.tsx`, route `gymadd`, "Can't find your gym? Add it" under Find gyms near you in Gyms, and "Gym not listed? Add it in the app" on the website's gym pages). A signed-in lifter or owner gives the name, street address and city; the server finds it with Nominatim (the city's centre, or the lifter's rounded position, when the street isn't on the map), refuses one with a similar name within 300 m, and saves it hidden and `pending` with `source = 'lifter'` (`suggestPlace`, op `suggest`). Five a day per account. Staff see it under **Gyms lifters added** in Admin › Gyms and in the To Do list, and Approve (it shows everywhere) or Reject (deleted). The owner claims it once it shows. Deleting the account deletes a gym of theirs still waiting, and takes their name off the rest.
- **Studios:** yoga, pilates, barre, spin, dance, martial arts, climbing and the like are marked `studio` from their name and sport tag, and the finder leaves them out (`studios=1` brings them back for later use). Staff fix a wrong guess with Mark gym or Mark studio.
- **Find gyms near you** (`NearbyGyms.tsx`, in Add a gym and the setup wizard's gym step) now shows real gyms within 15 km first: **official ones first** with a check mark (`verified` icon, "Official gym" for screen readers), then nearest, with the distance, street and city, "equipment set up" when there's a setup, and how many lifters added it. Lifters' listed setups that aren't tied to a real gym follow under "Setups other lifters listed". `GET /api/gyms/` keeps its `gyms` answer for older apps and adds `places`.
- **Picking a real gym** opens the same preview as a shared gym (`SharedGym.tsx`) with the gym's name, address and check mark. A gym offers one setup: its owner's once claimed, else the most-added setup lifters listed for it. When nobody has set one up it starts as a typical commercial gym ("Nobody has set up this gym's equipment yet…"), so the lifter changes what's different and can list it. The new gym remembers which real gym it is (`Gym.placeId`), so "You already have this gym" means that gym, not a matching kit, and listing it later ties the listed setup to the real gym (`public_gyms.place_id`). A setup tied to a real gym shows under that gym, not on its own.
- **Lifters here:** adding a real gym counts the lifter once (`gym_place_lifters`: account, else the same one-way address code as reports). Only the total is shown. Deleting the account takes the lifter out of the totals. The privacy policy ("Gyms you share or list") and `docs/PRIVACY_LABELS.md` say so.
- Pure web code, so Android, iOS and the web app get it together.

## Step 2: gym pages (built)

- **On the website** (`src/server/gymPage.ts`, `/api/gyms/page/`, rewritten from `/gyms/…` in `next.config.ts`; server build only): `/gyms/` lists every city with gyms, `/gyms/<city>/` ("Gyms in Langley, BC") lists its gyms (official first, then the most lifters, then by name) with studios in their own section, and `/gyms/<city>/<gym>/` is one gym. Addresses use the city and region (`langley-bc`) and the gym's name, unique within its city, and never change on a re-import, so links and posters keep working.
- **A gym's page** shows the facts first: name and check mark, address, Directions (Google Maps) and Open in OpenStreetMap links, the website and phone, opening hours by weekday (`src/lib/openingHours.ts` reads OSM's common forms; anything else is shown as written), the equipment its setup has, and "N Liftsmith lifters train here". Then **Add this gym to your Liftsmith**, which opens the app on the gym (`#/gymplace/<id>`), a "claim it" card on unofficial gyms (step 3), and up to eight more gyms in the city.
- Like public profiles, they're **plain HTML with no scripts** in the site's header and footer (`siteFrame.ts`, store links tagged `gym_page`), under `SHARE_PAGE_CSP`, in the reader's language from Accept-Language (text in `gp.*`, six languages), cached for 1 minute in the browser and 2 at the edge (it was 5 and 15, which left /gyms/ empty for a while after the first import; 2026-11-12). **Linked from** the site footer (Product › Find a gym, both the Next pages and the server-drawn ones) and the home page's first How it works step; the app reaches gyms from Today's gym chip and Settings › Gyms and equipment. Each carries `ExerciseGym` (or `ItemList`) structured data. A studio's page and an unknown address are `noindex`.
- **`/sitemap-gyms.xml`** (listed in `robots.ts`) has `/gyms/`, every city and every gym that isn't hidden or a studio. **Since 2026-11-16 only gyms with something of their own** (`gymIndexable` and `INDEXABLE_SQL` in `gymPlaces.ts`: an owner, a website, phone or opening hours, a lifter who added it, or an equipment setup). About half the imported gyms had only a name and an address; Search Console left thousands of those "Crawled" or "Discovered - currently not indexed". Their pages stay up, `noindex`, and join the sitemap by themselves once the gym gets any of those ([Public pages and SEO](public-pages-and-seo.md) "Search Console indexing").
- **Map picture: Mapbox** (Melvin chose it, 2026-11-12). The gym page and the app's gym view show a static street map with a pin, from Mapbox's Static Images API (dark style, 640x320 at 2x). It goes through our own server (`/api/gyms/map/?place=<id>`, `mapImage` in `gymMedia.ts`, cached for a day), so the pages' CSP (images only from our site) holds and the token never reaches a browser. It's off until `MAPBOX_TOKEN` (a public token with the default scopes) is set in Vercel (Melvin set it on 2026-11-12); without it the page shows no map and still links out to directions. The picture keeps Mapbox's logo and the page credits Mapbox and OpenStreetMap, as their terms ask.
- **Logo and photos** (Melvin, 2026-11-12). The owner adds a logo and up to six photos on the owner's page (Settings › Gyms and equipment › the gym). The app turns each into a JPEG first (a logo up to 512 px and 150 KB, a photo up to 1,600 px and 450 KB, on white) and the server checks it's a JPEG, keeps it in the database (`gym_photos`, like share cards) and serves it from `/api/gyms/photo/?id=`, cached for good (a new picture gets a new id). A new logo replaces the old one; 30 additions a day per account. They show on the gym page (logo in the header, photos above the club) and in the app. Staff can take all of a gym's pictures down with Remove pictures in Admin › Gyms; deleting a gym or the owner's account removes them too.
- **In the app** (`GymPlace.tsx`, route `gymplace`): loads the gym (`GET /api/gyms/?place=<id>`) and shows the same preview as the finder, now with the lifters line, opening hours in the app's language and clock, and links to directions, the website and the gym's web page (`PlaceDetails.tsx`). A gym the lifter added from the finder has **See this gym's page** in Gyms. Pure web code, so Android, iOS and the web app get it together.

## Step 3: claiming (built)

Melvin, 2026-11-12, picked **"Website email, else code on stand"**: a code to an email at the gym's own website domain makes the gym official at once; otherwise the claim code on the stand or poster we mail does it; and staff can still approve a claim by hand in admin.

- **Claim this gym** (`GymClaim.tsx`, route `gymclaim`, from "Is this your gym? Claim it" on the gym's preview and its web page). Needs an account. The form asks for name, role (owner, manager or staff), work email, and optionally a phone and a note. Server: `src/server/gymClaims.ts`, through `POST /api/gyms/` ops `claimStart`, `claimVerify`, `claimPoster`, `owned`, `edit` and `release`.
- **Website email:** when the email's domain is the gym's website domain or a subdomain of it (`emailMatchesSite`), we email a six-digit code (30 minutes, five wrong tries, used up in one conditional update, so parallel guesses can't pass the limit). Shared hosts never count (Facebook, Instagram, Linktree, Google sites, Wix, Squarespace and the like, `SHARED_HOSTS`), so a gym whose "website" is a social page can't be claimed by anyone with an address there.
- **Code on the stand:** each poster or stand carries a claim code (`XXXX-XXXX`, letters and digits without look-alikes), stored only as a hash in `gym_places.claim_code` and used up by the first correct claim. Ten wrong tries per account a day. Codes are made in step 4 when a poster is printed (`newPosterCode`).
- **Anything else goes to staff:** the claim is saved as pending and shows at the top of **Admin › Gyms** (Claims to check, with the gym, name, role, email, phone and note), where staff approve or reject it. The lifter sees "Thanks, we'll check". Five claims per account a day, ten gyms per account.
- **Official** means `gym_places.claimed_by` is set: the check mark everywhere, first in the finder and on city pages, and a re-import from OpenStreetMap never touches it. A gym already claimed says so ("This gym already has an owner"); a mistaken owner is fixed through feedback and staff.
- **The owner's page** (`GymEdit.tsx`, route `gymedit`, from "Manage this gym's page" on the gym's preview): opening hours per weekday with a switch and two times (or Open 24 hours), website (https only), phone, a short description (500 characters, through the same word and link check as the rest of the app), and the equipment lifters get when they add the gym, picked from one of the owner's own gyms in Liftsmith. Saved hours are written in OpenStreetMap's form (`formatHours`), so the web page reads them like any other. Saving marks the gym's details as the owner's (`source = 'owner'`).
- **The owner's results** (Melvin, 2026-11-12), at the top of the owner's page: lifters in all, new lifters and poster or stand scans in the last 30 days (`ownedGyms`; scans per day in `gym_scan_days`, kept by `countScan`), then one next step, the first that applies: add opening hours, set the equipment, print the poster (no scans in 30 days), how many more lifters until we mail a stand, else keep the stand where members check in. Numbers with a step to take, per "Action over stats".
- **Letting go:** Stop managing this gym, or deleting the account, makes the gym unofficial again and keeps the details the owner set. The account's claims are deleted with it (`claimDeletes`). The privacy policy and `docs/PRIVACY_LABELS.md` cover claims.
- Pure web code, so Android, iOS and the web app get it together.

## Step 4: posters, table stands and the QR code (built)

Melvin, 2026-11-12, agreeing to item 4 of the [growth plan](growth.md): start with a **free printable poster any gym prints itself**, and mail the plastic table stand (about $15 to $30 each with postage) **only to claimed gyms with 20 or more lifters**, so spend follows proof.

- **The QR code** opens the gym's web page with `?from=qr` (`qrTarget` in `src/server/gymPrint.ts`), not the app directly: the page works for someone without the app (store links, the gym's details) and its **Add this gym to your Liftsmith** button opens the app on the gym, which on Android opens the installed app through App Links (`/app/`). iOS Universal Links are still deferred (open-items.md), so on an iPhone it opens the web app. A visit with `?from=qr` is counted once per address and gym a day (`gym_places.qr_scans`, `countScan`), isn't cached, and shows in Admin › Gyms; the canonical address has no `?from`. The QR code is drawn as inline SVG from the bundled `qrcode-generator` (`qrPath`), black on white.
- **Free poster for every gym:** `/gyms/<city>/<gym>/poster/` (rewritten to the gym page route with `poster=1`; noindex, no scripts, under the gym pages' CSP) prints the poster and two stand cards in the reader's language. Linked from each gym's web page ("Work here? Print a free poster") and from the owner's page in the app. It has no claim code, because anyone can open it.
- **The sheet** (`gymPrint.ts`): the **poster** (one page, Letter in Canada and the US and when the country isn't known, else A4): the Liftsmith mark, "Add this gym to your Liftsmith", the gym's name (with the check mark once official), a 4.2 in QR code, three steps quoting the app's own button, and the website. The **stand cards** are 4 × 6 in, the common acrylic sign holder size, two side by side on a landscape page with dashed cut lines. Text is `gp.print.*` in six languages, in the order's language (admin) or the reader's (public page).
- **Mailed stands:** a gym's owner asks in the app (owner's page › Poster and table stand) once `STAND_MIN_LIFTERS` (20) lifters have added the gym; below that it says how many so far. The owner gives the mailing address (the map's address to start); one order per gym in the works, three asks per account a day, address through the word check. Server: `src/server/gymPosters.ts` (`gym_posters`), `POST /api/gyms/` ops `posters` and `posterStatus`.
- **Admin › Gyms › Posters and stands** (`GymPosters.tsx`, `/api/admin/gym-posters/`): orders to print, then to mail, with the address, language and who asked. Staff can also add an order for any gym (**Send posters** on a gym in the list: poster, stand or both, attention line, address, language), for gyms worth a stand before they reach 20. **Print** (`/api/admin/gym-posters/print/?id=`, admins only, audited) opens the sheet with a **letter** first, its address placed for a #10 window envelope. For a gym nobody has claimed, the letter carries the gym's one-time **claim code** (step 3), made on the first print and kept on the order so a reprint shows the same code, then forgotten when the order is marked shipped (the gym keeps only its hash). The code is never on the poster or stand. Then **Printed** and **Shipped**; Cancel and Reopen for mistakes.

## Clean city names and addresses (2026-11-15)

Some gym pages were filed under a postcode or house number ("6337, CA", "1, ON", "80145, Campania") or under no city at all (`/gyms/gyms/…`), and some addresses had stray commas and repeated parts. The rules are in one pure file, `src/server/gymAddress.ts`, used by the import, the website fill-in and the repair:

- **A city needs a letter and isn't a postcode** (`cityName`): "80145 Napoli" gives "Napoli", "Toronto, ON" gives "Toronto", "80145", "1" and "V2Y 1A1" give nothing. Any script counts ("浜松市").
- **Import:** a gym's city is its map `addr:city` (else `is_in:city`) when that's a real name, else the import's own city. The import's city is Nominatim's city, town or village, then the name it found, then the city typed (`areaCity`); a remembered area whose city was a postcode uses the typed one. A refresh that finds a gym's old city was bad takes the new one even when it was only guessed.
- **Addresses** (`tidyAddress`): commas and spaces fixed, repeated parts dropped, and the city, region, postcode and country left off the end, since the page shows those. A unit or number with no street gives no address.
- **A gym that changes city moves** (`moveToCity`): a new folder and a free slug, with the old address in `gym_moves`; the old gym page and city page answer with a 301 to the new one (`movedGym`, `movedCity`), keeping `?from=qr` so poster scans still count.
- **Fix city names** (Gym list, shown when some cities look wrong; `gymRepair.ts`, action `repair`) walks every unclaimed gym a thousand at a time: the name inside the value, else the city at least two gyms within about 3 km agree on, else OpenStreetMap's reverse lookup for the position (a dozen a step, one a second), and tidies the address. - **The first run is put right** (`revertOldRules`, once, from the worker on its first run after the release and from `startFix`): every correction it made (a field that had a value) goes back to what we had where the field still holds what it wrote, its fills (an Instagram, a postcode, hours where there were none) are kept, city fixes stay, and every gym it stamped gets one go under the strict rules (`ai_fix_at` back to 0). Its job says how many were undone. Jobs carry the rules they ran under (`gym_ai_fix_jobs.rules`, 1 for the first run, 2 now) and whether that's done (`reverted`).
- **Admin › Gyms › Import › AI fix-up** (`GymAiFix.tsx`): **Run the AI fix-up on unclaimed gyms** (admins), then a card with gyms checked of the total and a progress bar, how many got missing details, have suggestions, went to Reports or couldn't be read, the pause note, **Stop** or **Close**, **Suggestions waiting for you** (Apply, Dismiss) and **Latest changes** (the newest 60 filled details, each with **Undo**; a city change has none because it moved the page). Support staff watch only. The card reads the job every 10 seconds while it runs, never while it's paused or the tab is hidden.
- **Which gyms**: every unclaimed, visible, approved gym with a website (`claimed_by = ''`, `hidden = 0`, `pending = 0`, `website <> ''`), whatever the import did with it. A gym without a website is left out: there's nothing for Claude to read, and it never guesses from memory. A gym claimed later is never touched (every write repeats `claimed_by = ''`).
- **Once ever** (`gym_places.ai_fix_at`, partial index `gym_places_ai_fix`): a batch picks its 12 gyms and stamps them in one `UPDATE … RETURNING`, so two workers, a crash, Stop and a new run can never take a gym twice. A gym that AI couldn't be asked about at all (the day's cap, AI switched off, the key, Anthropic busy, unreachable or answering with an error) gets its stamp back and waits; once Claude has answered, or the page couldn't be read, that gym is done for good. A later run takes only gyms that never had their go (gyms new since), and says so when there are none.
- **What it changes** (`fixesFor`, `gymAiFix.ts`, task `gymFix`): Claude sees our entry and the page (both wrapped as data) and gives what the page states for this location, how many locations the business has, and whether it says the gym closed for good or isn't a gym. Every value passes the import AI step's checks (`checkedField` in `gymAutoFill.ts`), then the strict rules above. A city fill moves the page and redirects, as with every city fix. Each write (a fill, or an applied suggestion) happens only if the field still holds what Claude was shown.
- **Closed or not a gym goes to Reports, never hidden**: a report on Admin › Reports › Reported gym pages (reason `closed` or `notGym`, who `ai-fixup`, the note starting "AI fix-up, from the gym's website:"), for staff to hide, merge or dismiss like any other.
- **Undo**: every change is kept with its old value (`gym_ai_fixes`, index on job and time). Undo puts the old value back if the field still holds what the pass wrote and the gym is still unclaimed. Apply writes a suggestion the same way.
- **The cost limit**: every request goes through `askClaude`, so the `aiHelpers` kill switch and `aiDailyCap` stop it, and the worker checks `aiStatus` before each batch. At the cap the job pauses until midnight Pacific (`paused_until`, `nextPacificDay`) with the note "Paused: … It carries on by itself at midnight Pacific."; switched off or without a key it looks again every 15 minutes, and after Anthropic was busy in 2 minutes. It shares the cap with the import's AI step and the other helpers. Haiku reads a 20,000-character page for about a tenth of a cent, so the default $2 cap covers roughly 2,000 gyms a day.
- **Runs on the server**: its own job table (`gym_ai_fix_jobs`, one running at a time, a lease like the import's), worked by `runFixWorker` beside the import worker in `/api/cron/gym-imports/` (every five minutes, and handed over to a fresh invocation when its time is up), so it never blocks an import or the daily refresh. POST actions `fixStart`, `fixStop`, `fixClose`, `fixUndo`, `fixApply`, `fixDismiss` on `/api/admin/gym-places/`, audited as `gyms.aiFixStart`, `gyms.aiFixStop`, `gyms.aiFixUndo`, `gyms.aiFixApply` and `gyms.aiFixDismiss`; GET `?fix=1`.
- **Reads stay small**: the next gyms and the latest changes walk their indexes (`dbIndexes.test.ts`), and the total is counted once, when the pass starts.

## Class schedules (2026-11-17)

Melvin picked "Gym class schedules" from the gap analysis (2026-11-17, thread "Find missing features to build next":
"Class timetable on the gym page: classes and times the owner enters, shown on the public page and in the app").

- **Who posts:** an official gym's owner and its staff (`canManage`, gymStaff.ts), from the gym's page in the app
  (`#/gymedit/<id>`, Class schedule, `OwnerClassesSection` in `GymClasses.tsx`). Anyone reads them. A gym nobody has
  claimed shows no schedule, and releasing or deleting a gym deletes its classes (`classEnds`), because nobody keeps
  them up to date after that. Deleting an account keeps the gym's classes without saying who added them.
- **A class** (`gym_classes`, `src/server/gymClasses.ts`, shared rules in `src/lib/gymClasses.ts`): a name (60), a
  type (strength, HIIT, bootcamp, spin, yoga, Pilates, boxing, dance, mobility, aqua fit, cardio, other), the weekdays
  it runs, a start time, a length (10 to 240 minutes; the editor offers 30, 45, 60, 75 and 90), and optionally the
  instructor (60), the room (40) and a note (160: level, what to bring, how to book). Typed words go through the same
  filter as announcements (no links, no blocked words). Up to 80 classes a gym and 200 changes a day.
- **Little typing:** the type is a chip, and picking one names a class that has no name yet ("Yoga"); days are chips,
  the start is the clock dial (`TimeField`), the length is chips.
- **Every week, with dates off:** classes repeat weekly. Instead of one-off classes, the editor shows the next eight
  dates the class runs as chips; tapping one marks it off (up to 20 dates), and members see that day struck out with
  "Not running this day". Dates more than two days past are dropped when read.
- **Times are the gym's clock.** A class is stored as weekdays plus "18:30", never converted between time zones, so the
  timetable reads like the poster at the front desk wherever the reader is. The app works out "today" and the next
  class on the phone's own calendar, which is right for members near the gym.
- **Members** see the next seven days with classes on the gym's page in the app (`ClassSchedule` in `PlaceDetails.tsx`,
  part of `placeById`, so it's kept and shown offline like the rest of the page). A class opens a sheet with its days,
  time, type, instructor, room, note and coming dates off, and **Add the next one to my calendar** (`addEvent` in
  calendar.ts: the phone's calendar on Android and iOS through the Calendar plugin, a `.ics` file on the web). The
  website's gym page (`classesHtml`, gymPage.ts) shows the whole week Monday first, and a gym with classes counts as
  having something of its own for indexing (`gymIndexable`).
- **No booking** (decided 2026-11-17, after reading coach booking, trainer-mode.md "Booking"). Coach booking is one
  coach's one-on-one times with their own clients. Gym classes need spots, waitlists, memberships and no-shows, which
  gyms already run at the front desk or in their own software, and connecting that software is parked
  (gym-software.md). So the schedule only shows classes and the note says how to book; each card says "Book a spot with
  the gym directly." If gyms ask for it, the next step is a booking link per class, not our own booking.
- Free for gyms and members, the same for under-18s (classes are the gym's, not a lifter's). Pure web code plus the
  existing Calendar plugin, so Android, iOS and the web app get it together. Ten languages (`gp.class*` for what
  members and the website read, `gym.cls.*` for the owner's editor).

## Open now (2026-11-19)

Melvin picked it from ideas borrowed from an open-source sat-nav app (Horizon Maps, GPL-3.0; the idea only, our own code). A gym's hours already came from OSM's `opening_hours` or its owner; nothing said whether it was open.

- **Where:** each real gym's row in Find gyms near you (`NearbyGyms.tsx`, every platform), above the week on a gym's details in the app (`PlaceDetails.tsx`), and each row of the map list on the website's `/gyms/` page (`gyms-near.js`). Lines: "Open now · closes 10:00 PM" (green), "Closes soon · 10:00 PM" in the last hour (amber, `SOON_MIN`), "Closed · opens 6:00 AM" / "opens tomorrow …" / "opens Monday …" (muted), and "Open now · 24 hours". The words always say it, so colour is never the only cue. Text is `gp.*`, shared by the app and the website, in all ten languages.
- **Open now filter:** a chip above the finder's list (and a matching button on `/gyms/`), shown only when at least one gym has readable hours; it narrows the list and the pins to gyms open now, with "None of these gyms is open right now." when none are.
- **One rule, one function:** `openState` and `openNow` in `src/lib/openingHours.ts`. Ranges that end before they start run past midnight, a day ending 24:00 joins the next day's 00:00, and a week that never closes has no closing time. Hours we can't read (anything `parseHours` gives up on) show no line rather than a guess; public holiday rules are skipped, as on the week list.
- **Whose clock:** we don't store a gym's time zone, so it's worked out on the viewer's clock and only for gyms within 300 km (`OPEN_NEAR_KM`), close enough to share it almost everywhere. In the app that's the finder's distance, or for a gym's details the distance from the lifter's last finder search; with neither, no line. On `/gyms/` the script sends the browser's time zone (`&tz=`, an IANA name, checked by `visitorClock`) and the server answers each gym's state, so the rule isn't written twice; the script only words it.
- **Not on the website's own gym pages** (`/gyms/<city>/<gym>/`): they're drawn on the server with no scripts and cached, and the server doesn't know the reader's clock, so they keep the plain week.

## Sharper icons

Melvin, 2026-11-19: "for the gyms we scrape, is it possible find higher resolution icons from the favicon? some websites have iphone / ios / tablet resolution icons". A gym's logo is drawn at 64 px, so a 16 or 32 px favicon looked blurry on a phone. `siteIcon` in `gymEnrich.ts` now looks everywhere a site keeps an icon and keeps the sharpest square one:

- **Candidates** (`iconCandidates`, `manifestIcons`): `<link rel="icon">` and `apple-touch-icon` / `apple-touch-icon-precomposed` with their `sizes` (a touch icon without sizes counts as 180 px), Windows tile images (`msapplication-TileImage` 144 px, `msapplication-square310x310logo` and friends), the web app manifest's icons (`rel="manifest"`, read only when nothing the page names directly is 180 px or more; at most 64 KB; `any` and `maskable` icons, maskable ranked just below a plain one of the same size, never monochrome), `/apple-touch-icon.png` (iPhones ask for it whether or not a page names it), `/favicon.ico`, and **og:image only as a last resort**: tried only when nothing else gave an icon, and kept only if it's square (within 10%) and at least 128 px. Never SVG, never `data:`.
- **Order** (`rankIcons`): declared sizes from 180 to 512 px biggest first, then bigger ones smallest first (they cost more to fetch and serve and look no better at 64 px), then the small ones biggest first, og:image last.
- **Measured, not trusted** (`imageSize`, `iconPx`): each file fetched is measured from its own bytes (PNG, GIF, ICO's biggest picture, JPEG, WebP), must be square within 25%, and the biggest wins. It stops at the first of 180 px or more, after at most five icon files, still within the 150 KB cap. Its size is kept in `gym_photos.px` (the smaller side).
- **Small icons get another look**: an icon under 128 px counts as missing, so the gym is read again when its site is next due (a month, like any other missing field), and a sharper icon replaces it only if it's bigger. The replacement gets a new id, because pictures are cached for good. Claimed gyms and gyms with an owner's logo are never touched (the statement checks both).
- **Backfill of icons kept before this** (no button): every website step (the import job's and each daily refresh's, `enrichBatch`) first measures up to 500 icons with `px = 0` from their first 4 KB only (`substr(image, 1, 4096)`), so Turso's bandwidth never pays for whole images, through the partial index `gym_photos_unmeasured`, which is empty once they're all measured, so it costs nothing after that. An unclaimed gym whose old icon is small goes to the front of the website step's line (`enriched_at = 1`, which still counts as read for the AI step), so the next daily refresh looks for a sharper one. One that can't be measured counts as 1 px.

## Pictures in Vercel Blob

Melvin, 2026-11-19 (from the open items' Later list): gym logos, photos and website icons move out of Turso. They were blobs in `gym_photos.image` (owner photos up to 450 KB each), so every one sat in the database and each CDN miss read its bytes from Turso.

- **Where they live** (`src/server/gymBlob.ts`): each picture is a public file in Vercel Blob at `gyms/<id>`, cached a year, and `gym_photos` keeps only its address in `url` (with `image` left empty). Owners' uploads (`addMedia`) and website icons (`keepIcon` in `gymEnrich.ts`) go to Blob first and then write the row.
- **Nothing outside the server changes**: pages and the apps still load `/api/gyms/photo/?id=`, which reads one indexed row for the address, fetches the file and answers with the same one-year immutable cache. So the gym pages' image policy (our origin only) stays, ids stay, and the copies the apps and browsers already cached keep working. A moved picture's bytes are never read from Turso again.
- **Removing**: a new logo, the owner's Remove, staff's Remove pictures, a deleted gym and a replaced website icon delete the old files after the rows (best effort; a file left behind is never shown). A new file whose row couldn't be written is taken back out.
- **Moving the old ones** (no button): while the gym import worker runs every five minutes (`/api/cron/gym-imports/`, `runMover`), it first measures old website icons (`measureIcons`, which reads their first bytes from the database), then moves pictures in batches of 100, eight uploads at once, for up to four minutes, through the partial index `gym_photos_unmoved`, which is empty once they've all moved, so after that it costs one empty read. A failed upload leaves that picture in the database and it's tried again next run.
- **Off until Blob is set up**: without `BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN` (Vercel sets it when a Blob store is connected to the project) everything stays in the database as before, and an upload that fails falls back to the database too. It's on the admin System tab's Settings card.
- Server only, so Android, iOS and the web get it together.

## Gym chains (2026-11-19)

Melvin, 2026-11-19: "Logos and 'all locations' pages for gym chains" (one of the sat-nav ideas). Chains used to wait until a chain asked for them; Melvin asked for them now, so they're built for every chain the map knows, with no sign-up from the chain. Pure web code plus the server, so Android, iOS and the web get it together.

- **Which gyms are a chain** (`src/server/gymChainNames.ts`, `gym_places.chain`, a `gym_chains` slug):
  1. The map's `brand` tag (or `brand:en`; the first of a `;` list), set when the gym is imported (`savePlaces`). A new brand gets its `gym_chains` row there, with the brand as the chain's name.
  2. Without a brand, a gym whose name is a known chain's name, or starts with it ("Planet Fitness Langley"), matched on folded words (`chainKey`, `matchChain`), at import and in the chains step.
  3. A name shared by **3 gyms or more in 3 cities or more** (`CHAIN_LIMITS`) becomes a chain of its own (`discoverNameChains`, source `name`), unless every word is a common one (`genericName`: "Fitness Centre", "Gimnasio Municipal", "Hotel Gym", in the app's languages and the map's usual ones).
  A chain the map has once stays when a later import has no brand (`chain` is in `KEEP_IF_MAP_EMPTY`).
- **Claimed gyms are untouched**: the import never writes them (as before), the chains step skips them, and they never show the chain's logo in place of their own. A claimed gym that was already a branch is still listed on its chain's page.
- **The shared logo** (`refreshChains`): the site host most of a chain's branches link to (two at least) is the chain's website, and the sharpest website icon read from that host (`gym_photos.px`, "Sharper icons") is copied into the chain's own picture (`gym_photos` kind `chain`, `place_id` `chain:<slug>`, `gym_chains.logo`, its source in `logo_from`), so it stays when that branch goes, under a new id when the pick changes (pictures are cached a year). An icon already in Vercel Blob ("Pictures in Vercel Blob") is read and stored again as the chain's own file, and the old copy's file goes when the pick changes, so a branch replacing its icon never takes the chain's with it. A franchise branch's own site never gives the chain its logo. Every **unclaimed** branch shows the chain's logo in place of its own icon (`chainOf` in `gymPlaces.ts`), so branches without a website get one too.
- **The import job's last step, `chains`** (`gymImportJobs.ts`, Admin › Gyms › Import shows it as "Gym chains"): the worldwide job finds name chains (one grouped read of the unclaimed gyms) and then matches every unclaimed gym without a chain by name, 2,000 at a time by id (`chainStep`, `chain_after` and `chain_fixed` on the job), then counts each chain's visible branches, cities and countries and picks its logo; the daily refresh does only the counting and logos. That count is what the pages read, so no page counts a chain itself.
- **Pages**: a chain shows (its link and its page) once it has **2 visible branches**, and is indexed and in the gym sitemap from **3** (`CHAIN_LIMITS.indexed`; fewer is a thin page, the same thinking as noindex for bare gym pages). 
  - *Website* (`src/server/gymChainPage.ts`, `/api/gyms/chain/`, rewrites in `next.config.ts`, no scripts, the share pages' CSP): `/gym-chains/` lists every chain, biggest first (linked from `/gyms/`), and `/gym-chains/<slug>/` shows the logo, the chain's website and every branch by country (Intl region names) and city, each linking to its gym page, with an `Organization` and its branches as `subOrganization` in JSON-LD. Up to 2,000 branches. Kept on the server 15 minutes (`keptList`) and an hour at the edge.
  - *Gym pages*: "Part of Planet Fitness · See all 2,431 locations" in the hero (website) and as one button in the gym's details in the app (`PlaceDetails.tsx`).
  - *App* (`GymChain.tsx`, `#/gymchain/<slug>`, `GET /api/gyms/?chain=`): the logo, "N locations", then every branch, **nearest first from the spot the lifter last searched for gyms** (`lastNearby`, never a new location request), 50 at a time with Show more, each opening its gym page; the chain's website at the foot.
- **Reads** (docs/decisions/database-reads.md): pages and counts read only gyms in a chain through the partial index `gym_places_chain` (`WHERE chain <> ''`, repeated in each query); the chains step's name pass is the one full read, once per worldwide import, like the tidy step. Text: `gp.chain*` in `src/locales/text/gp.ts`, ten languages.
