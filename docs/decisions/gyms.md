# Gyms: official gyms, the gym finder, gym pages and posters (Melvin, 2026-11-12)

Part of the decision record. [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) has the rules that apply to every change and the list of topics. Record decisions about this topic here, in the same change as the code.

Melvin, 2026-11-12, as part of following Hevy's growth playbook (organic growth, little spend, customer delight): gyms are customers too. Gyms register their location, we send them a poster or plastic stand with our name, logo, website and a QR code that says "Add this gym to your Liftsmith", gyms claim their gym in the app, and the QR code picks that gym and all its equipment. There are official (claimed, with a check mark) and unofficial gyms, a gym finder for the area where claimed gyms are more prominent, and a page per gym (lifters who train there, hours, map, address).

This builds on [Gym sharing](gym-sharing.md): lifters' own shared and listed **setups** (equipment and weights, about 1 km) stay as they are. This topic adds the **gyms themselves**. Built in four steps:

1. Gym directory and finder (built, below).
2. Gym pages in the app and on the website, plus city pages (built, below).
3. Claiming: the owner proves the gym is theirs and edits its details; the check mark (built, below).
4. Per-gym QR links, a free printable poster for every gym, and table stands mailed to claimed gyms with 20+ lifters from an admin queue (built, below). Shipping is Melvin's job.

## Step 1: the gym directory (built)

- **Real gyms** live in `gym_places` (`src/server/gymPlaces.ts`): name, address, city, region (provinces, US states and Australian states shortened, "BC", from Nominatim's `ISO3166-2-lvl4` code when it has one, else `SHORT_REGION` in `src/lib/regions.ts`; a place saved before 2026-11-12 with the long name, "Langley, British Columbia", is renamed with its address by a one-time update in `db.ts`, unless another city already has the short address), country, map position, opening hours, website, phone, where it came from, and later the owner. These are businesses on a public map, not lifter data, so the position is kept as the map has it (rounded to about 1 m).
- **Seeded from OpenStreetMap, not Google.** Google Maps' terms don't allow copying places out; OpenStreetMap's licence (ODbL) does, with credit. Every screen or page that shows OSM details says "Gym details © OpenStreetMap contributors". Test on 2026-11-12: Langley, BC gave 23 named places, 14 lifting gyms and 9 studios; about half had no street address and three had hours, and the city rec centres are tagged differently and were missed. So OSM is a starting point, and claiming is how a gym gets complete details.
- **Admin › Gyms** (`GymPlaces.tsx`, `/api/admin/gym-places/`): type a city ("Langley, BC") and Import. The server finds the area with Nominatim, then pulls every `leisure=fitness_centre` inside it from Overpass (inside the city's boundary when OSM has one, else its box), trying three Overpass servers in turn. Unnamed and private places are skipped. Running it again refreshes unclaimed gyms and never touches a claimed one. At most 5,000 places per import. The list pages 50 gyms at a time (a country can have thousands; search by name, city or province), official first, then the most lifters, and each gym's name opens its web page in a new tab (Melvin, 2026-11-12). It shows each gym's lifters and listed setups; staff can mark a place a gym or a studio, hide it, or (by API) delete it. Admins only; audited.
- **Rec and community centres** (2026-11-12): OpenStreetMap tags most of them `leisure=sports_centre` or `amenity=community_centre`, which the first Langley import missed. Imports now ask for those too and keep one only when its name says there's a gym ("Recreation", "Rec Centre", "Leisure", "Fitness", "YMCA", "Wellness", "Athletic", "Gym") or its sport tag is fitness or weightlifting, so arenas and pools alone stay out. Gymnastics clubs are studios.
- **Major cities in one go** (Melvin, 2026-11-12: "add all gyms from all major cities in Canada", then "the same for the US"). Admin › Gyms has **Import Canada's major cities** (about 90: every city of about 100,000 or more plus each capital) and **Import the US's major cities** (about 200: the biggest cities plus some smaller capitals), listed in `src/server/majorCities.ts`. The admin page runs one city import after another, two seconds apart, so OpenStreetMap's free services aren't hit at once, shows progress and can be stopped; running it again refreshes. An import writes in batches (`savePlaces` reads what's there in a few queries), up to 5,000 places, with five minutes per city.
- **A gym the map doesn't have** (`GymAdd.tsx`, route `gymadd`, "Can't find your gym? Add it" under Find gyms near you in Gyms, and "Gym not listed? Add it in the app" on the website's gym pages). A signed-in lifter or owner gives the name, street address and city; the server finds it with Nominatim (the city's centre, or the lifter's rounded position, when the street isn't on the map), refuses one with a similar name within 300 m, and saves it hidden and `pending` with `source = 'lifter'` (`suggestPlace`, op `suggest`). Five a day per account. Staff see it under **Gyms lifters added** in Admin › Gyms and in the To Do list, and Approve (it shows everywhere) or Reject (deleted). The owner claims it once it shows. Deleting the account deletes a gym of theirs still waiting, and takes their name off the rest.
- **Studios:** yoga, pilates, barre, spin, dance, martial arts, climbing and the like are marked `studio` from their name and sport tag, and the finder leaves them out (`studios=1` brings them back for later use). Staff fix a wrong guess with Mark gym or Mark studio.
- **Find gyms near you** (`NearbyGyms.tsx`, in Add a gym and the setup wizard's gym step) now shows real gyms within 15 km first: **official ones first** with a check mark (`verified` icon, "Official gym" for screen readers), then nearest, with the distance, street and city, "equipment set up" when there's a setup, and how many lifters added it. Lifters' listed setups that aren't tied to a real gym follow under "Setups other lifters listed". `GET /api/gyms/` keeps its `gyms` answer for older apps and adds `places`.
- **Picking a real gym** opens the same preview as a shared gym (`SharedGym.tsx`) with the gym's name, address and check mark. A gym offers one setup: its owner's once claimed, else the most-added setup lifters listed for it. When nobody has set one up it starts as a typical commercial gym ("Nobody has set up this gym's equipment yet…"), so the lifter changes what's different and can list it. The new gym remembers which real gym it is (`Gym.placeId`), so "You already have this gym" means that gym, not a matching kit, and listing it later ties the listed setup to the real gym (`public_gyms.place_id`). A setup tied to a real gym shows under that gym, not on its own.
- **Lifters here:** adding a real gym counts the lifter once (`gym_place_lifters`: account, else the same one-way address code as reports). Only the total is shown. Deleting the account takes the lifter out of the totals. The privacy policy ("Gyms you share or list") and `docs/PRIVACY_LABELS.md` say so.
- Pure web code, so Android, iOS and the web app get it together.

## Step 2: gym pages (built)

- **On the website** (`src/server/gymPage.ts`, `/api/gyms/page/`, rewritten from `/gyms/…` in `next.config.ts`; server build only): `/gyms/` lists every city with gyms, `/gyms/<city>/` ("Gyms in Langley, BC") lists its gyms (official first, then the most lifters, then by name) with studios in their own section, and `/gyms/<city>/<gym>/` is one gym. Addresses use the city and region (`langley-bc`) and the gym's name, unique within its city, and never change on a re-import, so links and posters keep working.
- **A gym's page** shows the facts first: name and check mark, address, Directions (Google Maps) and Open in OpenStreetMap links, the website and phone, opening hours by weekday (`src/lib/openingHours.ts` reads OSM's common forms; anything else is shown as written), the equipment its setup has, and "N Liftsmith lifters train here". Then **Add this gym to your Liftsmith**, which opens the app on the gym (`#/gymplace/<id>`), a "claim it" card on unofficial gyms (step 3), and up to eight more gyms in the city.
- Like public profiles, they're **plain HTML with no scripts** in the site's header and footer (`siteFrame.ts`, store links tagged `gym_page`), under `SHARE_PAGE_CSP`, in the reader's language from Accept-Language (text in `gp.*`, six languages), cached for 1 minute in the browser and 2 at the edge (it was 5 and 15, which left /gyms/ empty for a while after the first import; 2026-11-12). **Linked from** the site footer (Product › Find a gym, both the Next pages and the server-drawn ones) and the home page's first How it works step; the app reaches gyms from Today's gym chip and Settings › Gyms and equipment. Each carries `ExerciseGym` (or `ItemList`) structured data. A studio's page and an unknown address are `noindex`.
- **`/sitemap-gyms.xml`** (listed in `robots.ts`) has `/gyms/`, every city and every gym that isn't hidden or a studio.
- **Map picture: Mapbox** (Melvin chose it, 2026-11-12). The gym page and the app's gym view show a static street map with a pin, from Mapbox's Static Images API (dark style, 640x320 at 2x). It goes through our own server (`/api/gyms/map/?place=<id>`, `mapImage` in `gymMedia.ts`, cached for a day), so the pages' CSP (images only from our site) holds and the token never reaches a browser. It's off until `MAPBOX_TOKEN` (a public token with the default scopes) is set in Vercel (Melvin set it on 2026-11-12); without it the page shows no map and still links out to directions. The picture keeps Mapbox's logo and the page credits Mapbox and OpenStreetMap, as their terms ask.
- **Logo and photos** (Melvin, 2026-11-12). The owner adds a logo and up to six photos on the owner's page (Settings › Gyms and equipment › the gym). The app turns each into a JPEG first (a logo up to 512 px and 150 KB, a photo up to 1,600 px and 450 KB, on white) and the server checks it's a JPEG, keeps it in the database (`gym_photos`, like share cards) and serves it from `/api/gyms/photo/?id=`, cached for good (a new picture gets a new id). A new logo replaces the old one; 30 additions a day per account. They show on the gym page (logo in the header, photos above the club) and in the app. Staff can take all of a gym's pictures down with Remove pictures in Admin › Gyms; deleting a gym or the owner's account removes them too.
- **In the app** (`GymPlace.tsx`, route `gymplace`): loads the gym (`GET /api/gyms/?place=<id>`) and shows the same preview as the finder, now with the lifters line, opening hours in the app's language and clock, and links to directions, the website and the gym's web page (`PlaceDetails.tsx`). A gym the lifter added from the finder has **See this gym's page** in Gyms. Pure web code, so Android, iOS and the web app get it together.

## Step 3: claiming (built)

Melvin, 2026-11-12, picked **"Website email, else code on stand"**: a code to an email at the gym's own website domain makes the gym official at once; otherwise the claim code on the stand or poster we mail does it; and staff can still approve a claim by hand in admin.

- **Claim this gym** (`GymClaim.tsx`, route `gymclaim`, from "Is this your gym? Claim it" on the gym's preview and its web page). Needs an account. The form asks for name, role (owner, manager or staff), work email, and optionally a phone and a note. Server: `src/server/gymClaims.ts`, through `POST /api/gyms/` ops `claimStart`, `claimVerify`, `claimPoster`, `owned`, `edit` and `release`.
- **Website email:** when the email's domain is the gym's website domain or a subdomain of it (`emailMatchesSite`), we email a six-digit code (30 minutes, five wrong tries, used up in one conditional update, so parallel guesses can't pass the limit). Shared hosts never count (Facebook, Instagram, Linktree, Google sites, Wix, Squarespace and the like, `SHARED_HOSTS`), so a gym whose "website" is a social page can't be claimed by anyone with an address there.
- **Code on the stand:** each poster or stand carries a claim code (`XXXX-XXXX`, letters and digits without look-alikes), stored only as a hash in `gym_places.claim_code` and used up by the first correct claim. Ten wrong tries per account a day. Codes are made in step 4 when a poster is printed (`newPosterCode`).
- **Anything else goes to staff:** the claim is saved as pending and shows at the top of **Admin › Gyms** (Claims to check, with the gym, name, role, email, phone and note), where staff approve or reject it. The lifter sees "Thanks, we'll check". Five claims per account a day, ten gyms per account.
- **Official** means `gym_places.claimed_by` is set: the check mark everywhere, first in the finder and on city pages, and a re-import from OpenStreetMap never touches it. A gym already claimed says so ("This gym already has an owner"); a mistaken owner is fixed through feedback and staff.
- **The owner's page** (`GymEdit.tsx`, route `gymedit`, from "Manage this gym's page" on the gym's preview): opening hours per weekday with a switch and two times (or Open 24 hours), website (https only), phone, a short description (500 characters, through the same word and link check as the rest of the app), and the equipment lifters get when they add the gym, picked from one of the owner's own gyms in Liftsmith. Saved hours are written in OpenStreetMap's form (`formatHours`), so the web page reads them like any other. Saving marks the gym's details as the owner's (`source = 'owner'`).
- **The owner's results** (Melvin, 2026-11-12), at the top of the owner's page: lifters in all, new lifters and poster or stand scans in the last 30 days (`ownedGyms`; scans per day in `gym_scan_days`, kept by `countScan`), then one next step, the first that applies: add opening hours, set the equipment, print the poster (no scans in 30 days), how many more lifters until we mail a stand, else keep the stand where members check in. Numbers with a step to take, per "Action over stats".
- **Letting go:** Stop managing this gym, or deleting the account, makes the gym unofficial again and keeps the details the owner set. The account's claims are deleted with it (`claimDeletes`). The privacy policy and `docs/PRIVACY_LABELS.md` cover claims.
- Pure web code, so Android, iOS and the web app get it together.

## Step 4: posters, table stands and the QR code (built)

Melvin, 2026-11-12, agreeing to item 4 of the [growth plan](growth.md): start with a **free printable poster any gym prints itself**, and mail the plastic table stand (about $15 to $30 each with postage) **only to claimed gyms with 20 or more lifters**, so spend follows proof.

- **The QR code** opens the gym's web page with `?from=qr` (`qrTarget` in `src/server/gymPrint.ts`), not the app directly: the page works for someone without the app (store links, the gym's details) and its **Add this gym to your Liftsmith** button opens the app on the gym, which on Android opens the installed app through App Links (`/app/`). iOS Universal Links are still deferred (open-items.md), so on an iPhone it opens the web app. A visit with `?from=qr` is counted once per address and gym a day (`gym_places.qr_scans`, `countScan`), isn't cached, and shows in Admin › Gyms; the canonical address has no `?from`. The QR code is drawn as inline SVG from the bundled `qrcode-generator` (`qrPath`), black on white.
- **Free poster for every gym:** `/gyms/<city>/<gym>/poster/` (rewritten to the gym page route with `poster=1`; noindex, no scripts, under the gym pages' CSP) prints the poster and two stand cards in the reader's language. Linked from each gym's web page ("Work here? Print a free poster") and from the owner's page in the app. It has no claim code, because anyone can open it.
- **The sheet** (`gymPrint.ts`): the **poster** (one page, Letter in Canada and the US and when the country isn't known, else A4): the Liftsmith mark, "Add this gym to your Liftsmith", the gym's name (with the check mark once official), a 4.2 in QR code, three steps quoting the app's own button, and the website. The **stand cards** are 4 × 6 in, the common acrylic sign holder size, two side by side on a landscape page with dashed cut lines. Text is `gp.print.*` in six languages, in the order's language (admin) or the reader's (public page).
- **Mailed stands:** a gym's owner asks in the app (owner's page › Poster and table stand) once `STAND_MIN_LIFTERS` (20) lifters have added the gym; below that it says how many so far. The owner gives the mailing address (the map's address to start); one order per gym in the works, three asks per account a day, address through the word check. Server: `src/server/gymPosters.ts` (`gym_posters`), `POST /api/gyms/` ops `posters` and `posterStatus`.
- **Admin › Gyms › Posters and stands** (`GymPosters.tsx`, `/api/admin/gym-posters/`): orders to print, then to mail, with the address, language and who asked. Staff can also add an order for any gym (**Send posters** on a gym in the list: poster, stand or both, attention line, address, language), for gyms worth a stand before they reach 20. **Print** (`/api/admin/gym-posters/print/?id=`, admins only, audited) opens the sheet with a **letter** first, its address placed for a #10 window envelope. For a gym nobody has claimed, the letter carries the gym's one-time **claim code** (step 3), made on the first print and kept on the order so a reprint shows the same code, then forgotten when the order is marked shipped (the gym keeps only its hash). The code is never on the poster or stand. Then **Printed** and **Shipped**; Cancel and Reopen for mistakes.
- **Admin › To Do** lists gym claims waiting for staff and posters to print or mail (kind "Gyms", also in the morning digest).
- Deleting an account takes its name off its stand orders (the gym's mailing address stays as the mailing record). The privacy policy covers it.
- **The gym's club** (Melvin, 2026-11-12, growth plan): an official gym's owner taps **Start the club** on the owner's page (`gymClubStart` in `social.ts`, op `gymClub`). It's a Friends club named after the gym and tied to it (`clubs.place_id`), so members get the same weekly leaderboard, crew goal and posts as any club, run from Friends, with room for 1,000 people (`gymClubMembers`) instead of 100. One per gym; asking again gives the same code. The gym's preview in the app and its web page then show **Gym club** with **Join the club** (`#/club/<code>`, the usual join screen, which says weekly totals are shared). Joining is opt-in and needs Friends, so the owner is sent to turn on Friends first. When the owner lets the gym go or staff delete it, the club stays with its members as an ordinary club.
- Not built yet: hiding "N lifters train here" below about 10 lifters (Melvin didn't pick it, 2026-11-12). Pure web code, so Android, iOS and the web app get it together.

## Map of the nearest gyms (2026-11-13)

Melvin: "it should show a map of all the nearest gyms with a list beneath it". **Find gyms near you** (the app on
Android, iOS and the web, setup wizard included) shows one map picture above the list with the first 20 gyms as
numbered pins, the same numbers on the list's rows, and the searcher's rounded spot as a cyan dot. The website's **city
pages** (`/gyms/<city>/`) show the same kind of map of the city's gyms above their list (no scripts, so it can't ask
where the reader is). It's one Mapbox static picture through our own server (`areaMapPath`, `areaMapImage` in
`src/server/gymMedia.ts`, `/api/gyms/map/?ids=<id>.<id>…&at=lat,lon`), not an interactive map: no map library to load,
the gym pages' CSP holds, the token stays on the server, and it's cached a day at the edge. The pins go by the ids in
the address, so pin 1 is always the list's first gym. Off with the other maps while `MAPBOX_TOKEN` isn't set. Pure web
code, so Android, iOS and the web app get it together.
