# Integrations roadmap (Melvin agreed, 2026-11-09; research: https://claude.ai/artifact/UoDvYPVgFxt8wwPuJJS7KH)

Part of the decision record. [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) has the rules that apply to every change and the list of topics. Record decisions about this topic here, in the same change as the code.

1. **MVP:** Health Connect + HealthKit reads to prefill the wizard, plus writing finished workouts. Bluetooth 0x180D stays the only live heart rate path (straps, Garmin/Polar/Whoop broadcast, Fitbit Charge 6, Pixel Watch). The wizard's connect step offers Health access and heart rate pairing only.
2. **Readiness (health part built with the health twin, 2026-11-09; the check-in built 2026-11-10, see [Workout data and coaching](workout-data-and-coaching.md)).** An on-device readiness score from sleep, HRV and resting HR against a 30-day baseline, plus a pre-workout check-in (energy, soreness, feeling unwell). It feeds `generator.ts`/`block.ts` as a low/normal/high adjustment the lifter can override. **Readiness auto-adjust is a Pro feature.**
3. **Music (built 2026-11-10 as a playlist chip on Today, see [Connected apps](connected-apps.md)).** Mood playlists follow the day's call, so the check-in picks the mood too (low or unwell gives the easy-day playlist), opened by deep link. **For now, public playlists that we curate** (Melvin, 2026-11-09). The lifter's own saved playlists come later. **No Spotify API** (development mode is capped at 5 users, extended access needs 250k MAU, audio features were removed).
4. **Integrations server (built 2026-11-10 with the Connected apps page; see [Connected apps](connected-apps.md)), right after the MVP and ahead of full sync** (Melvin decided, 2026-11-09). A small server on Vercel with libsql/Turso. Its first job is the OAuth exchange and token storage for **Strava, Oura and Whoop**, so all three ship in the first updates. Apply early for Strava Extended Access, Oura approval (including written consent to use their data in Pro features) and Whoop approval, because each caps us at 10 users until approved.
5. **After that** (full order in the research doc's "How we win" section): Samsung Health Data SDK (dropped 2026-11-11: Samsung Health comes through Health Connect), a bar-speed sensor (replaced 2026-11-11 by camera bar speed, a lab), watch apps (built 2026-11-10, [Watch apps](watch-apps.md)), a Garmin Connect IQ app.
6. **Approved additions (Melvin, 2026-11-09; details in the research doc's "Added to the plan"), built in this order, each on Android and iOS together:**
   - **MVP+1 · Gym by location:** an opt-in geofence on each gym profile picks the active gym on arrival. Approximate location only, kept on the device.
   - **MVP+1 · Cycle-aware readiness:** opt-in, with its own consent screen. Reads menstrual data from Health Connect and HealthKit, or the lifter logs it. It never leaves the device except through the user's own sync, and never goes to analytics.
   - **MVP+1 · Voice and system shortcuts:** iOS App Intents and Shortcuts (log set, start workout, +30 s), Android App Actions, a Quick Settings tile, home-screen widgets, the iPhone Action button and Control Center.
   - **MVP+2 · Calendar:** write planned training days to the device calendar, and read busy times only with permission.
   - **With the server · Coach sharing:** a read-only link or PDF that expires and can be revoked, plus an opt-in weekly email (Resend).
   - **With the server · Cloud integrations** (Strava, Oura, Whoop): connecting one explains that it creates a free account, then uses Google or Apple sign-in. Everything else stays account-free.
   - **Before launch · Legal and consent:** the Health Connect declaration, HealthKit review notes, a privacy policy that covers each integration, Washington MHMDA and GDPR special-category consent, and export and delete that cover linked accounts.
   - **Test devices:** Melvin has a Fitbit Air (check that its "Share heart rate" is seen by our 0x180D reader, since Google lists only some apps as compatible) and an Oura ring (readiness inputs through Health Connect and HealthKit now, the direct API later).
- **Hume:** Melvin says Hume Body Pod data does reach Health Connect (2026-11-09), so it needs no special handling on Android: it arrives through Health Connect, and on iOS through Apple Health. Body weight and composition prefill the wizard through `health.ts`. There is no direct integration, because Hume has no API.
- **Skipped:** live SpO2, the Google Health API, aggregators, and volume-follows-intensity (Android only; iOS can't control other apps' volume. It was skipped under the old strict parity rule and can come back as an Android extra under the 2026-11-09 rule; see Platform deltas).
