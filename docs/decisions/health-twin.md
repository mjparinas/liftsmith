# Health twin (Melvin asked, 2026-11-09; built; mockups: https://claude.ai/artifact/VnajHz63WHMZGuyayc14FS)

Part of the decision record. [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) has the rules that apply to every change and the list of topics. Record decisions about this topic here, in the same change as the code.

- **The Recovery tab is now Body**, with two views of the same hologram figure: **Muscles** (the recovery map, Pro) and **Health** (the twin, free). The view picked is remembered on the device; without Pro it opens on Health. Route stays `#/recovery`. Code: `Recovery.tsx`, `HealthTwin.tsx`, engine `src/lib/twin.ts`.
- **What it shows:** weight trend (% a week over 4 weeks), lean mass and body fat (change over ~6 weeks), BMI (and when to ignore it: lean by body fat), TDEE with cut, lean-gain and protein targets, steps, sleep (last night and the week), HRV, resting heart rate, overnight breathing rate, VO2 max (3-month trend) and **heart rate drop in the first minute of rest** (from our own Bluetooth stream, `restDrops`). Melvin added steps, VO2 max and breathing rate (2026-11-09).
- **Action over stats:** every metric has a status chip in words, a line on what it means and what to do, and, for the ones that feed readiness, what today's plan did ("counted in today's call" or, without Pro, "with Pro, today's plan would take this into account"). Tapping one opens its last 30 days against the lifter's **own normal** (the 30 days before today, mean ± one SD, needs 7 readings), never against other people. The verdict card on top shows today's call; free lifters see what Pro would have called. The figure stands with its heart beating at the resting rate and the main numbers pinned around it; it never changes shape with body fat.
- **Data:** health readings come in as `health` events (`metric`, `day`, `value`; id holds metric, day and value, the newest `at` wins for a day) from `health.ts readDaily` on launch and when connecting (`store.importHealthDaily`, last 45 days). Sleep is the union of asleep stages per night (overlapping sources don't double count; in bed and awake don't count). Steps only for finished days. Everything is derived from events; nothing is stored on the profile. Health data stays on the device and the lifter's own sync: never analytics, feedback reports or share links.
- **Readiness:** `twin.healthExternal` feeds sleep, HRV, resting HR and breathing rate (new: +2 breaths a minute over normal is −8) into `readiness.ts` through `healthSignals`, the one place their points live, only when `can("healthReadiness")` (Pro).
- **TDEE:** with 5+ of the last 7 days of steps, the activity level comes from steps and training together (`withSteps` in `tdee.ts`: the higher of the two, one up when 7,500+ steps and 3+ training days; source `steps`).
- Glossary entries: health twin, HRV, resting HR, breathing rate, VO2 max, lean mass, BMI, rest drop. Not medical advice, said on the screen and every sheet.
- Pure web code on top of the shared health plugin, so Android and iOS get it together; the web differs (Platform deltas).
