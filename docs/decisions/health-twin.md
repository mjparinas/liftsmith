# Health twin (Melvin asked, 2026-11-09; built; mockups: https://claude.ai/artifact/VnajHz63WHMZGuyayc14FS)

Part of the decision record. [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) has the rules that apply to every change and the list of topics. Record decisions about this topic here, in the same change as the code.

- **The Recovery tab is now Body**, with two views of the same hologram figure: **Muscles** (the recovery map, Pro) and **Health** (the twin, free). The view picked is remembered on the device; without Pro it opens on Health. Route stays `#/recovery`. Code: `Recovery.tsx`, `HealthTwin.tsx`, engine `src/lib/twin.ts`.
- **What it shows:** weight trend (% a week over 4 weeks), lean mass and body fat (change over ~6 weeks), BMI (and when to ignore it: lean by body fat), TDEE with cut, lean-gain and protein targets, steps, sleep (last night and the week), HRV, resting heart rate, overnight breathing rate, VO2 max (3-month trend) and **heart rate drop in the first minute of rest** (from our own Bluetooth stream, `restDrops`). Melvin added steps, VO2 max and breathing rate (2026-11-09).
- **Action over stats:** every metric has a status chip in words, a line on what it means and what to do, and, for the ones that feed readiness, what today's plan did ("counted in today's call" or, without Pro, "with Pro, today's plan would take this into account"). Tapping one opens its last 30 days against the lifter's **own normal** (the 30 days before today, mean ± one SD, needs 7 readings), never against other people. The verdict card on top shows today's call; free lifters see what Pro would have called. The figure stands with its heart beating at the resting rate and the main numbers pinned around it; it never changes shape with body fat.
- **Data:** health readings come in as `health` events (`metric`, `day`, `value`; id holds metric, day and value, the newest `at` wins for a day) from `health.ts readDaily` on launch and when connecting (`store.importHealthDaily`, last 45 days). Sleep is the union of asleep stages per night (overlapping sources don't double count; in bed and awake don't count). Steps only for finished days. Everything is derived from events; nothing is stored on the profile. Health data stays on the device and the lifter's own sync: never analytics, feedback reports or share links.
- **Readiness:** `twin.healthExternal` feeds sleep, HRV, resting HR and breathing rate (new: +2 breaths a minute over normal is −8) into `readiness.ts` through `healthSignals`, the one place their points live, only when `can("healthReadiness")` (Pro).
- **TDEE:** with 5+ of the last 7 days of steps, the activity level comes from steps and training together (`withSteps` in `tdee.ts`: the higher of the two, one up when 7,500+ steps and 3+ training days; source `steps`).
- Glossary entries: health twin, HRV, resting HR, breathing rate, VO2 max, lean mass, BMI, rest drop. Not medical advice, said on the screen and every sheet.
- Pure web code on top of the shared health plugin, so Android and iOS get it together; the web differs (Platform deltas).
- **Manual body fat entry** (Melvin approved it, 2026-11-10). Many lifters have no smart scale, so body fat can be logged by hand: an optional "Body fat % (optional)" box under the weigh-in on Progress and on Body and photos (`BodyFatField.tsx`). It's a `BumpField` (− box +, hold to repeat) in 0.1 steps; an empty box's first tap lands on the last reading, or 20% with none, so a scale or calipers number is a few taps away. 3 to 60% (`parseBodyFat` in `tdee.ts`); a typed value outside that is refused with a line saying so. It's saved on the weigh-in event (`bodyFat`), the same field Health Connect and Apple Health fill, so the health twin, TDEE (Katch-McArdle) and coach's notes (body fat rising or falling, three readings over three weeks) read it with no extra code, and it syncs like any weigh-in. Body fat on its own is logged with the last weight, so the weight trend isn't broken. The TDEE screen's body fat box now becomes a weigh-in too (1.5 s after the last change, together with weight), except the typical value filled in when body fat is switched on, which is a guess and stays out of the readings. With no reading the twin's body fat card says "Log body fat" and opens Progress, on every platform. A `bodyFat` glossary entry explains measuring the same way at the same time of day. Pure web code, so Android, iOS and the web app get it in the same change; nothing is written back to Health Connect or Apple Health.
- **Body measurements** (competitor gaps, Melvin, 2026-11-11). Body and photos has a **Measurements** card between the weigh-in and the photos: chips for nine tape sites (waist, hips, chest, shoulders, neck, upper arm, forearm, thigh, calf), a `BumpField` (0.5 cm or 0.25 in steps, starting from that site's last number or a typical one), and Log. Each is a `measure` event (`site`, `cm`), so it syncs, backs up and erases like weigh-ins; lifters who use pounds see and type inches (`src/lib/measure.ts`). Underneath, each measured site shows its latest number and its change since the first. Action over stats: when there are no body fat readings, the waist feeds a coach's note, **Your waist is growing** (up 2 cm+ over 3 to 8 weeks, same food advice as body fat creeping up) or **Waist down, strength holding** (down 2 cm+ with no stalled lifts). Not read from Health Connect (it has no circumference type) or Apple Health, so both phones and the web behave the same. Pure web code, so Android, iOS and the web get it together.

## Status label colours (Melvin, 2026-11-11: "the tiles have a label on the right, it should be colour coded based on what is being displayed and should make sense")

- One calm scale for every label on Body › Health (tiles, the pins on the figure and the detail sheet; `TONE` in `HealthTwin.tsx`), driven by each metric's `status` in `twin.ts`: **good** green (going well: losing slowly, building muscle, well rested, active, above your HRV normal), **steady** cyan (normal for you, holding steady, enough sleep), **watch** amber (worth acting on: dropping or gaining fast, body fat creeping up, short sleep, resting heart rate up), **info** plain (only information: a day's TDEE, building a trend, from setup), **missing** faint with a dashed edge (nothing to judge yet). No red anywhere: these are nudges, not alarms. The words always say the same as the colour.
- BMI's label takes its band's colour from the BMI range bar (see [Tools: TDEE calculator](tools-tdee-calculator.md)), except "Ignore it" for a lean lifter, which stays plain.
- Slow weight loss says "Losing slowly" (it said "On pace", which assumed losing was the aim) and is green unless the goal is building muscle, where it's plain information.
