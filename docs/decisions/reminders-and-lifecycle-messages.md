# Reminders and lifecycle messages (Melvin, 2026-11-09; built)

Part of the decision record. [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) has the rules that apply to every change and the list of topics. Record decisions about this topic here, in the same change as the code.

- **What:** workout reminders, a missed-day nudge, streak alerts, comebacks, milestones and Pro offers, by phone notification and by email, to keep people training and help conversion. One pure planner (`src/lib/messages.ts`) decides what goes out and when for both channels; the phone (`src/lib/reminders.ts`) and the server (`src/server/reminders.ts`) only deliver.
- **Kinds** (each has its own switch in **Settings › Reminders**, separately for push and email, `MessageSettings.tsx`):
  - *Workout reminders:* push on each training day at the workout time (Settings › Training › Schedule's days and time, `trainingDays`; defaults spread the days-a-week goal at 18:00), skipped once there's a workout that day. The first one before any workout reads "Your first workout is ready". By email, one Monday 08:00 note with the week's days and last week's count, not one a day.
  - *Missed workouts:* once per lapse, the morning after the first missed training day (by email, the second), and only if that morning isn't a training day itself (its reminder says it). Then a check-in after 7, 14 and 30 quiet days (counted from setup if there's no workout yet), and nothing more.
  - *Streak alerts:* when the week's goal needs a workout on every day left and there's a streak of at least one week to keep. On a training day it's folded into that day's reminder. Streaks count weeks (game.ts `goalStreak`), so nothing asks anyone to train on a rest day.
  - *Milestones:* email only (the app already celebrates in the moment): the first workout, sent right away when the phone reports it, and streaks of 4, 8, 12, 26 and 52 weeks.
  - *Trial ending* (its own kind since 2026-11-14, push and email, **on by default** because it's about the lifter's own trial, not an offer): two days before a trial ends ("or do nothing and stay on the free plan"). Under-18s get it too, because it's account information, not an offer.
  - *Pro offers:* four offers about what Pro adds, 3, 17, 31 and 59 days after the trial ends or is declined, then none. **Off by default on both channels** (Apple 4.5.4 needs explicit opt-in for promotional pushes; GDPR and CASL need it for marketing email). **None go out until billing is live** (`billingLive()` in `billing.ts`), because until then everyone has Pro and an offer would be misleading. Once it is, trial and Pro state come from the store entitlement (`storePro`).
  - *Getting started tips* (the feature tour; Melvin, 2026-11-14: "a series of emails, with branding, that promote all the features the app has with actual in app screenshots. One per day"): email only, on by default, `src/server/tour.ts`. Eight emails, one a day from the day after the welcome, at 10:00 in the lifter's time zone: the plan, the reasons, one-tap logging, "Why this weight?", the rest timer, recovery, coach's notes, and PRs, levels and badges. Each has a kicker ("Getting started · 3 of 8"), a title, two short lines, a button to that screen and the real screen underneath: the Google Play phone shots in the reader's language, made email-sized by `scripts/email-images.mjs` into `public/email/<lang>/0N.jpg` (re-run it after `play-compose.mjs`). The tour is scheduled by the daily job after everything else: it takes a day with no other email (the welcome included), and a tour email already scheduled for a day steps aside (cancelled, sent again on a later day) when anything else lands on that day, so nobody gets two emails in a day; it doesn't count toward the three-a-week cap. **Never to under-18s**: the phone sends `teen` (by birth year, `isTeen`) with the subscription, and the server sends them neither the tour nor Pro offers. **The Wear OS step** (Melvin, 2026-11-26: "add the fact that we now have support for wear os (android) smart watches, dont forget to add screen shots"): a ninth email, "Your workout on your Wear OS watch", sits right after the rest timer (Getting started · 6 of 9), with the watch app's own screens in the reader's language (`public/email/<lang>/09.jpg`, made by `scripts/email-watch.mjs` from `store/wear/`, which `scripts/wear-shots/` draws from the watch's real layout and strings with Android's renderer, Paparazzi). It waits for the **`wearOnPlay`** admin flag (off by default; turn it on once Google Play approves the Wear OS app, so no email points at an app nobody can install). It's a `late` step: it goes to anyone still on Getting started while the flag is on, and never to someone who finished the other eight without it (they get the weekly "Your workout on your wrist" tip instead, which skips anyone who had the watch step), so nobody gets a "tip 9" weeks after they finished and the weekly tips never pause for it. The kicker counts the steps each reader gets ("of 8" or "of 9").
  - *Weekly tips* (Melvin, 2026-11-15): email only, on by default, `src/server/weekly/`. After Getting started, one email a week about one more thing the app does, with the real screen in the reader's language: Tuesday at 10:00, or the next free weekday up to Saturday, one per calendar week, none in a week with a trial-ending email or after three other emails that week. Like the tour it steps aside for anything else on its day and doesn't count toward the caps. Never to under-18s. Some go only to coaches, physios, school coaches or gym owners and staff. Details in [Weekly tips emails](weekly-emails.md).
  - *Rehab reminders* (`rehab`, 2026-11-15, push only, on by default): the physio's rehab at the workout time on a day without a workout reminder, when it's due (an injury it's for is active, the gap since the last one, and the physio's times a week; `rehabDueOn`). Second in the one-a-day order, after the workout reminder. [Trainer mode](trainer-mode.md) "Rehab that gets done".
  - *Training seasons* (`season`, 2026-11-18, push and email, on by default): the day a season starts, its finale week and three days before it ends, folded into the reminder on a day that has one. Sent while seasons are on and unlocked; teens too, because it isn't an offer. Priority after streak, before missed. [Training seasons](seasons.md).
  - *Welcome:* the email after confirming, always sent (it's the confirmation of the sign-up). Before the first workout it names the workout waiting on Today (`rmd.mail.welcomePlan`: name, exercises, minutes), and so does the first-workout reminder email (`rmd.firstWaitingPlan`).
  - *Lifecycle emails* (Melvin, 2026-11-14: "I like all your email ideas"), all on by default with their own switch, email only, adults and teens alike (none is promotional):
    - *Weekly and monthly recap* (`recap`): Sunday 19:00, the week's workouts, sets and new bests, skipped when the week had no workout; on the 1st at 09:00, last month's totals with a button to the shareable recap (route `recap`).
    - *New personal bests* (`records`): sent when the phone reports a newer best than the server last saw (`record()` in `messages.ts`), at most one a week.
    - *Friends who trained* (`friends`): Thursday 18:00, up to five people the lifter follows who posted a workout that week (not blocked either way, not suspended, posts not hidden; `friendActivity` in `remindersDb.ts`), only for subscriptions tied to an account and only on a day with no other email.
    - *Win-back:* the missed-workout check-ins now come after 7, 14, 21 and 30 quiet days, and from 14 days on they read as a welcome back with an easy first workout (`rmd.winback`).
- **Caps and quiet hours:** at most one message a day per channel; nothing between 21:00 and 08:00 except the reminder at the time the lifter picked (06:00 to 21:00). Email also caps at three a rolling week and one Pro email a fortnight (`CAPS`). Priority on a busy day: reminder, trial ending, recap, streak, season, missed, Pro. The tour, the weekly tips, the friends email and a new best never land on a day that already has an email.
- **Push is local, not FCM/APNs.** Every message can be worked out on the phone from its own data, so the phone schedules the next 14 days with `@capacitor/local-notifications` (Android and iOS) when the app opens, comes back to the foreground, a workout ends or a setting changes. It works offline and needs no account, server, Firebase project or APNs key. Android uses its own "Reminders" channel (mutable on its own) and inexact alarms, so the lifter is never sent to the "Alarms & reminders" screen. On iOS, `WorkoutSessionPlugin.swift` owns the notification delegate and passes everything that isn't the workout's own notification to Capacitor's router, so reminder taps open the app on the right screen. Remote push (FCM/APNs) is only for what the phone can't work out itself: Friends notifications (follows, comments, sent workouts, likes) go through `src/server/push.ts` ([Friends](friends.md) "Push notifications").
- **Language** (2026-11-10): every notification and email is in the app's language (Settings › Language). The words are `rmd.*` in `src/locales/text/rmd.ts`, read through `src/lib/messageText.ts` (it loads only that file, because the server must not import `i18n.ts`). The planner takes `lang` with its other fields; the phone sends it with the email subscription and it's stored with them, and anything missing or unknown reads as English. The confirm and unsubscribe pages opened from an email stay English.
- **The web app has email only** (Platform deltas).
- **Email is one switch** (Melvin, 2026-11-12: "email notifications should just be a toggle, we already know users emails, we don't need that input"). Settings › Reminders › Email has an **Email me** switch and no address box. Its line says what's sent and where ("…go to {email}, the address you signed in with", unsubscribe link, offers off), and switching it on is the consent (`consentAt`). The address is the signed-in account's, which sign-in already verified (Google and Apple give only verified emails, and the emailed code proves it), so `POST /api/reminders/` carries the account's session (`Authorization: Bearer`), the server looks up the account's email (`verifiedAccount` in `reminderHttp.ts`), and a subscription to that exact address is confirmed at once: the welcome email goes out, nothing waits for a link, and the row gets the `account_id`. **Signed out, the switch opens sign-in** (#/account) instead of asking for an address, because there is no address we know and a typed one would need the old confirmation loop; the line under it says so. Switching it off is the old "Stop all email and delete my address": it deletes the row and its history from the server. Shared web code, so Android, iOS and the web get it together.
  - *On by default* (Melvin, 2026-11-14: "Email me setting should be on by default for all users"). A signed-in adult who never turned email off gets it on with the account's verified address, existing accounts included, the next time the app opens (`autoEmailPrefs` and `startDefaultEmail` in `reminders.ts`, called from `App.tsx` before each refresh). It waits for this device's first sync with the account (`useSync.lastSync()`), so a freshly signed-in phone doesn't start a second subscription before the account's settings arrive, and the device that turns it on is the one that keeps the subscription up to date. **Under-18s (by their birth year) start with it off** and can turn it on themselves. Switching it off saves `messages.emailOff` on the profile, which syncs, so it stays off on every device. The sign-in screen says so under the buttons (`account.emailNote`: reminders, the weekly recap and tips, and where to turn them off), and every email keeps its one-click unsubscribe. Pro offers stay off by default. Shared web code, so Android, iOS and the web get it together.
  - *Any other address is still double opt-in:* a subscription whose address isn't the session's own gets the confirmation email and waits for the button on the confirmation page (a POST, so mail scanners that open links can't sign anyone up), with three confirmation emails a day at most per subscription and the routes rate-limited per address. A sync never resubscribes an address that unsubscribed from an email link; only switching email back on in the app does (`resubscribe`).
  - *Older subscriptions* (typed addresses from before 2026-11-12) keep working. A confirmed one keeps its address and shows it under the switch. A pending one, once the lifter is signed in, moves to the account's address on the next refresh (`refreshReminders`) and the server confirms it, so nobody is left waiting on a link to an address they may have mistyped. Signed out, a pending one keeps its address and its Resend confirmation button.
- **What the server keeps** (`reminder_subs` and `reminder_sends`, `src/server/remindersDb.ts`, in the same database as analytics): the address, which kinds are on, when the lifter agreed, and the planner's numbers only: training days and time, time zone, days a week, setup time, last and first workout times, days trained this week, streak weeks and Pro state, plus (since 2026-11-14, for the recaps and bests) the unit, this week's and last month's totals (workouts, sets, new bests), the newest best (exercise name, weight, reps, when) and, before the first workout, the name, exercise count and minutes of the workout waiting. **No set-by-set workouts, body weight, heart rate or health data.** The device keeps a random subscription id and secret in localStorage; the server stores only hashes of it and of the confirmation token. `account_id` is set when the subscription is made or confirmed through the account's session, and stays null for older or signed-out ones.
- **Scheduling:** a Vercel Schedule (`reminders` in `vercel.json`, once a day at 00:05 UTC) runs `GET /api/cron/reminders/` (`CRON_SECRET`), plans each confirmed subscriber's next 26 hours in their own time zone and hands each email to Resend with `scheduled_at` and an idempotency key. When the phone reports a change (say, a workout that makes a missed-day nudge wrong), the server cancels the scheduled emails that no longer apply and schedules any new ones. If the phone is offline after a workout, a scheduled nudge can still go out; that's accepted.
- **Every email** carries a one-click unsubscribe link (an HMAC of the subscription id, so the token is never stored) and the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers (RFC 8058, required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders), says why it was sent and where to change it, and includes the postal address (CAN-SPAM, CASL). The routes refuse to send without `MAIL_POSTAL_ADDRESS`. Unsubscribing stops everything and cancels what's scheduled; the app's syncs can't quietly resubscribe. **Stop all email and delete my address** in Settings, and **Erase this device**, delete the row and its history from the server.
- **Server environment:** `RESEND_API_KEY`, `MAIL_FROM` (a sender on a domain verified in Resend, e.g. `Liftsmith <coach@getliftsmith.com>`), `MAIL_POSTAL_ADDRESS`, `REMINDER_SECRET` (a long random string for unsubscribe links), `CRON_SECRET`, and the database. Without them `/api/reminders/` answers 503 and the app says it couldn't reach the server.
- **Later:** switch the streak alert to the gamification thread's `goalStreak` `atRisk` flag and freezes once that lands; a weekly-quest "almost done" nudge; receipts by email once billing is live.

## How the emails look (Melvin, 2026-11-27: "it's very plain and boring")

- Every lifter email (reminders, lifecycle, Getting started, Weekly tips, the welcome and the confirmation) goes through one layout, `brandedMail` in `src/server/reminderEmails.ts`, drawn like the app: the navy page (`#080b10`), the app icon and the white wordmark, a raised card (`#10151d`, hairline border, a faint blue light at the top) holding the words, and the blue-to-violet primary button with dark ink, full width like the app's. The footer (why it was sent, where to change it, one-click unsubscribe, the postal address) sits under the card, unchanged.
- **Built to survive mail apps:** tables and inline styles only, a `bgcolor` on every coloured cell (Gmail drops `<body>` styles, which is why the old mail showed black text on white), colours also set as a one-colour `linear-gradient` background image so Gmail's dark mode doesn't flip them, a flat blue button in Outlook (no gradients there), and a hidden preview line from the first paragraph. Pictures are PNG (no SVG in mail) and decorative (`alt=""`), so with images blocked the email still reads fully.
- **Each kind has its icon** from the app's own set on a tinted tile (`EMAIL_ICONS`; `public/email/icons/`, made by `node scripts/email-icons.mjs`): calendar for reminders, trophy for bests, chart for recaps, people for friends, medal for milestones, and so on.
- **The streak card** (streak warnings and streak milestones): a glowing flame, the weeks in a big ember number with "week streak", and for a warning this week's workouts against the goal as dots, done ones mint with a tick and the rest ringed in ember, with "1 of 4 workouts done this week" under them. The planner passes the numbers on the message (`Message.streak` in `messages.ts`). Words in `rmd.mail.streakLabel` and `rmd.mail.weekDone`, all ten languages. Plain text gets "week streak: 1" instead.
- Teens, caps and switches are unchanged: this is only how an email looks.

## The daily job and the bundler (2026-11-15)

The 00:05 UTC run on 2026-11-15 failed with "ReferenceError: addDays is not defined": Turbopack bundled the tour's `for (…; …; day = addDays(…))` loop in `src/server/tour.ts` with the imported `addDays` left as a bare name, so no reminder or tour email was scheduled that night. The loop is now a `while` loop, and `scripts/bundle-check.mjs` runs after every `npm run build` and fails the build if any server chunk calls a function it never defines in a for loop's update (`bundleCheck.test.ts`). Re-running the job is safe: every email is sent with an idempotency key and already-scheduled sends are skipped.

**Weeks follow the lifter's first day** (Melvin, 2026-11-15, number audit): the plan input carries `firstDay` (0 Sunday, 1 Monday; older apps send none and keep Monday). The streak warning, the weekly email (the first morning of the week), the weekly recap (the last evening of the week: Sunday, or Saturday for Sunday weeks) and the week's totals all use it, so an email counts the same week as the app. Weights in emails and on public profile pages round like the app (`weightIn` in `unitMath.ts`: up to two decimals, the exact pound), so a 100 kg set reads 220.46 lb everywhere.

## App icon badge (2026-11-19)

Melvin, 2026-11-19: "add app icon notifications", then "on the today page … make it clear where the notifications are coming from. Like a red dot or something. With messages counts".

- **One count.** The icon shows what's waiting in **Friends** (unseen activity plus follow requests, the Friends tab's own number) plus **unread coaching messages**, capped at 99 (`badgeCount` in `src/lib/badgeCount.ts`). Reminders, streaks and other local notifications never add to it: they ask for a workout, not for something to read.
- **Red counts say where.** The same numbers show in red (`CountDot` in `ui.tsx`, `--color-bad` with `--color-bad-ink`, 4.5:1 in both themes and the colour-blind set, checked by `a11y.test.ts`): on the Friends tab, on Today's coach badge (unread messages) and on the desktop sidebar's Friends and Coaching. They replace the old blue dots. The control's label says the count to screen readers; the red number is `aria-hidden`.
- **While the app is closed** each push carries the number, worked out on the server per recipient (`badgeFor` in `src/server/badge.ts`, the same two queries the app's counts come from): APNs `aps.badge` on iOS, FCM `notification_count` on Android. **While it's open** `useIconBadge` (`src/lib/iconBadge.ts`, mounted in `App`) keeps the icon equal to the app's count, so reading everything clears it: the native `AppBadge` plugin (iOS sets the number; Android clears the seen pushes on the `friends` channel, which takes the launcher's badge away and leaves the workout notification), and the Badging API on the web.
- **One switch**, Settings › Reminders › **Number on the app icon** (`messages.badge`, on unless turned off, syncs with the profile). Off clears the icon at once and the phone tells the server again (`resendPushToken`), stored per phone as `push_tokens.badge`, so its pushes stop carrying a number. The red counts in the app stay: they're where things are, not an alert. The switch shows only where the icon can carry a number (the apps, and an installed web app).
- Free, every platform; differences in [Platform deltas](platform-deltas.md).
