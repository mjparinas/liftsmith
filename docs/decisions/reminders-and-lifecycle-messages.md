# Reminders and lifecycle messages (Melvin, 2026-11-09; built)

Part of the decision record. [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) has the rules that apply to every change and the list of topics. Record decisions about this topic here, in the same change as the code.

- **What:** workout reminders, a missed-day nudge, streak alerts, comebacks, milestones and Pro offers, by phone notification and by email, to keep people training and help conversion. One pure planner (`src/lib/messages.ts`) decides what goes out and when for both channels; the phone (`src/lib/reminders.ts`) and the server (`src/server/reminders.ts`) only deliver.
- **Kinds** (each has its own switch in **Settings › Reminders**, separately for push and email, `MessageSettings.tsx`):
  - *Workout reminders:* push on each training day at the workout time (Settings › Calendar's days and time; defaults spread the days-a-week goal at 18:00), skipped once there's a workout that day. The first one before any workout reads "Your first workout is ready". By email, one Monday 08:00 note with the week's days and last week's count, not one a day.
  - *Missed workouts:* once per lapse, the morning after the first missed training day (by email, the second), and only if that morning isn't a training day itself (its reminder says it). Then a check-in after 7, 14 and 30 quiet days (counted from setup if there's no workout yet), and nothing more.
  - *Streak alerts:* when the week's goal needs a workout on every day left and there's a streak of at least one week to keep. On a training day it's folded into that day's reminder. Streaks count weeks (game.ts `goalStreak`), so nothing asks anyone to train on a rest day.
  - *Milestones:* email only (the app already celebrates in the moment): the first workout, sent right away when the phone reports it, and streaks of 4, 8, 12, 26 and 52 weeks.
  - *Pro trial and offers:* two days before a trial ends ("or do nothing and stay on the free plan"), then four offers about what Pro adds, 3, 17, 31 and 59 days after the trial ends or is declined, then none. **Off by default on both channels** (Apple 4.5.4 needs explicit opt-in for promotional pushes; GDPR and CASL need it for marketing email). **None go out until billing is live** (`billingLive()` in `billing.ts`), because until then everyone has Pro and an offer would be misleading. Once it is, trial and Pro state come from the store entitlement (`storePro`).
  - *Welcome:* the email after confirming, always sent (it's the confirmation of the sign-up).
- **Caps and quiet hours:** at most one message a day per channel; nothing between 21:00 and 08:00 except the reminder at the time the lifter picked (06:00 to 21:00). Email also caps at three a rolling week and one Pro email a fortnight (`CAPS`). Priority on a busy day: reminder, streak, missed, Pro.
- **Push is local, not FCM/APNs.** Every message can be worked out on the phone from its own data, so the phone schedules the next 14 days with `@capacitor/local-notifications` (Android and iOS) when the app opens, comes back to the foreground, a workout ends or a setting changes. It works offline and needs no account, server, Firebase project or APNs key. Android uses its own "Reminders" channel (mutable on its own) and inexact alarms, so the lifter is never sent to the "Alarms & reminders" screen. On iOS, `WorkoutSessionPlugin.swift` owns the notification delegate and passes everything that isn't the workout's own notification to Capacitor's router, so reminder taps open the app on the right screen. Remote push (FCM/APNs) waits for a message the phone can't compute (friends, coach sharing); it will need a Firebase project and an APNs key.
- **The web app has email only** (Platform deltas).
- **Email is double opt-in.** Settings asks for the address and a consent tick (offers stay off), then `POST /api/reminders/` stores the subscription as pending and sends a confirmation email. Only the button on the confirmation page (a POST, so mail scanners that open links can't sign anyone up) confirms it and sends the welcome. Three confirmation emails a day at most per subscription, and the routes rate-limit per address. Changing the address or turning email back on after unsubscribing asks again.
- **What the server keeps** (`reminder_subs` and `reminder_sends`, `src/server/remindersDb.ts`, in the same database as analytics): the address, which kinds are on, when the lifter agreed, and the planner's numbers only: training days and time, time zone, days a week, setup time, last and first workout times, days trained this week, streak weeks and Pro state. **No workouts, weights, heart rate or health data.** The device keeps a random subscription id and secret in localStorage; the server stores only hashes of it and of the confirmation token. `account_id` is nullable so accounts can claim the subscription later and send the same fields from sync.
- **Scheduling:** a Vercel cron (`vercel.json`, once a day at 00:05 UTC, which works on every Vercel plan) runs `GET /api/cron/reminders/` (`CRON_SECRET`), plans each confirmed subscriber's next 26 hours in their own time zone and hands each email to Resend with `scheduled_at` and an idempotency key. When the phone reports a change (say, a workout that makes a missed-day nudge wrong), the server cancels the scheduled emails that no longer apply and schedules any new ones. If the phone is offline after a workout, a scheduled nudge can still go out; that's accepted.
- **Every email** carries a one-click unsubscribe link (an HMAC of the subscription id, so the token is never stored) and the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers (RFC 8058, required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders), says why it was sent and where to change it, and includes the postal address (CAN-SPAM, CASL). The routes refuse to send without `MAIL_POSTAL_ADDRESS`. Unsubscribing stops everything and cancels what's scheduled; the app's syncs can't quietly resubscribe. **Stop all email and delete my address** in Settings, and **Erase this device**, delete the row and its history from the server.
- **Server environment:** `RESEND_API_KEY`, `MAIL_FROM` (a sender on a domain verified in Resend, e.g. `Liftsmith <coach@getliftsmith.com>`), `MAIL_POSTAL_ADDRESS`, `REMINDER_SECRET` (a long random string for unsubscribe links), `CRON_SECRET`, and the database. Without them `/api/reminders/` answers 503 and the app says it couldn't reach the server.
- **Later:** switch the streak alert to the gamification thread's `goalStreak` `atRisk` flag and freezes once that lands; a weekly-quest "almost done" nudge; win-back and receipts by email once accounts and billing exist.
