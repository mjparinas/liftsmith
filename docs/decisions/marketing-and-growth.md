# Marketing and growth (Melvin, 2026-11-09; plan: https://claude.ai/artifact/HaXeCsBXt37yWQH9ptm3ub)

Part of the decision record. [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) has the rules that apply to every change and the list of topics. Record decisions about this topic here, in the same change as the code.

- **No paid advertising for now.** Growth is organic only: short faceless videos (TikTok, Reels, Shorts), honest founder answers on Reddit, the SEO and comparison pages, store listing work, and a creator program. Launch week adds Product Hunt and Show HN.
- **Faceless.** Clips show hands, plates, the phone screen, the 3D wireframe demos and on-screen text; no face or voice.
- **Handles:** `@liftsmith` everywhere, `@liftsmithapp` where `@liftsmith` is taken.
- **1 to 2 hours a week at most** of Melvin's time. Claude drafts scripts, posts, replies, outreach and emails; filming is one batch a month.
- **No open numbers.** Users and revenue are never shared publicly.
- **Creator program (Melvin approved 2026-11-09; built):** creators get Pro free and 30% of net revenue (after the store's 15% cut) from each subscriber who used their code, for that subscriber's first 12 months from their first payment, paid monthly once they're owed $25, on revenue past the 30-day refund window; a refund takes its share back. Their followers get a 30-day Pro trial instead of 14 days. Posts must be labelled as sponsored; we never ask for store reviews or scripted praise.
  - *Codes:* 3 to 24 letters, digits or dashes, uppercase (`LIFT-ALEX`; `normalizeCode` in `src/lib/creatorCode.ts`, shared by app and server). The link is `https://www.getliftsmith.com/c/<code>/` (`src/app/c/page.tsx`, `CreatorLanding.tsx`; one page, reached through a rewrite in the server build). It says who sent it, saves the code for the web app (localStorage `liftsmith.creator`, only once the server knows the code), and its Google Play badge carries the code as the install referrer (`utm_source=creator&utm_campaign=<code>`, `playLinkWithCode`).
  - *In the app* (`src/lib/creator.ts`, `CreatorCode.tsx`): on open, a code comes from the link (web) or the Play install referrer (Android, through `InstallInfo`); on iPhone it's typed under "Have a code?" below the plans (paywall and setup's trial step) or in Settings › Redeem a code. A typed code replaces one from a link; a link never replaces a typed one; "Remove code" stops a link or referrer bringing it back. **Once a trial or subscription exists, the code is fixed** (in the app and on the server), because it belongs to that subscription.
  - *Attribution:* the app posts the code and the random install id to `POST /api/creator/` (`src/server/creators.ts`, table `creator_installs`), whatever the usage-stats switch says, because that's how the creator gets paid (`/privacy/` and `docs/PRIVACY_LABELS.md` say so). It also sets the RevenueCat subscriber attribute `creator_code`, and the RevenueCat webhook records it if the app's claim never arrived. Unknown or paused codes answer 404 and give no longer trial.
  - *Generic wording (Melvin, 2026-11-10):* the app calls it **"Redeem a code"** / "Have a code?", not "creator code", because codes and discounts may later come from us rather than a creator. Only the wording is generic so far: every code is still a creator row (`creators` table, commission, the `creator30` offering). A code of our own (a promo) would need a code kind on that table with no commission, its own trial length or store offer per code (Play promo codes and App Store offer codes for real discounts), and an admin form for it.
  - *Trial:* with a checked code, billing picks the RevenueCat offering `creator30` (`pickOffering` in `billing.ts`), whose packages point at 30-day-trial store products or offers; without that offering it falls back to the normal 14-day one.
  - *Admin:* the **Creators** tab (`src/app/api/admin/creators/route.api.ts`, numbers in `src/lib/creatorMetrics.ts`, pure and tested): add, edit or pause a creator (code, name, contact, commission share, months, note), and per creator sign-ups (all time and in the period), trials, paying subscribers, refunds, store revenue, earned, cleared, paid and owed; **Record payout** (audited) and the month's **payout list CSV** (everyone owed $25+). Test installs are left out. Tables `creators`, `creator_installs`, `creator_payouts`.
- **The rules we hold in marketing:** no fake or incentivised reviews, no sock-puppet accounts, Melvin says he built the app whenever he mentions it, competitor claims only from the dated comparison data, and no fake countdowns or scarcity.
