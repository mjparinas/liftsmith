# Google Play review (Melvin, 2026-11-09; audit: https://claude.ai/artifact/5YAP14XHLSZUfXbcEWD4hS)

Part of the decision record. [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) has the rules that apply to every change and the list of topics. Record decisions about this topic here, in the same change as the code.

- **Ask only for health data a feature uses today.** Play's Health Connect review rejects any permission the app doesn't visibly use. `READ` and `WRITE` in `health.ts` are the list (reads: weight, height, body fat, resting heart rate, and for the health twin sleep, HRV, steps, VO2 max and respiratory rate; writes: active calories, heart rate), and the manifest strips every other permission the health plugin adds (`tools:node="remove"`). The same list drives HealthKit, and the iOS usage strings match it. Sleep, HRV, steps, VO2 max and respiratory rate came back with the health twin (2026-11-09), each visibly used there, and declared in `health.ts`, the manifest, both privacy pages, `docs/PRIVACY_LABELS.md` and the iOS strings. A new type goes in all of them in the same change, with a screen that uses it. `storePolicy.test.ts` fails when the manifest and `health.ts` disagree.
- **Precise location stops at Android 11** (`maxSdkVersion="30"`), where scanning for a Bluetooth heart rate monitor still needs it; Android 12+ scans with `BLUETOOTH_SCAN` (`neverForLocation`) and the app asks only for approximate location.
- **The privacy pages carry the Health Connect Limited Use statement and a contact address** (`CONTACT_EMAIL` in `site.ts`, hello@getliftsmith.com). The policy URL for the store listing and the Health Connect form is https://www.getliftsmith.com/privacy/. `public/privacypolicy.html` is also the in-app Health Connect rationale (loaded from the app's assets), so its links are absolute.
- Melvin's developer account doesn't need Play's 12-tester closed test.
- **Sign in details for reviewers** (2026-11-10): `review@getliftsmith.com` with the fixed code in Vercel's `REVIEW_SIGNIN_CODE`, which has Pro without a purchase (Accounts and sign-in, "Store review account"). The form text is in `docs/PLAY_LISTING.md`. Keep the Play Console password and the Vercel variable the same; change both together.
- **App content declarations** (2026-11-10; Melvin approved the answers). Advertising ID: No. Health apps: Activity and fitness, Nutrition and weight management (TDEE's cut and lean bulk targets), Period tracking (cycle-aware readiness); not Sleep management (sleep is only read for the health twin) and not Physical therapy (injury mode isn't medical). Data safety answers are in `docs/PRIVACY_LABELS.md`, and the delete account and delete data URLs are both `https://www.getliftsmith.com/delete-account/`. Change a declaration when the feature behind it changes.
- **Production countries** (2026-11-10, Melvin: all the countries the translations support): the 88 countries on Play's list where English, Spanish or French is an official or main language (`LANGS` in `src/lib/i18n.ts`). Left out: Cuba, which Play lists but our US-based service providers can't serve, and "Rest of World". Add countries when a language is added. German, Italian and Japanese were added on 2026-11-10 (Melvin: "ensure that all G7 countries are supported"), so the countries to add are the ones where those are an official or main language and not already in: Germany, Austria, Liechtenstein, Italy, San Marino and Japan (Switzerland, Belgium and Luxembourg are already in through French).
