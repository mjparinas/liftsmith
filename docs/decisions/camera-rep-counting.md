# Camera rep counting (Melvin asked, 2026-11-09: "rep counter by camera or watch motion", then "use the camera for workouts… a toggle during that exercise to auto count", after trying the lab)

Part of the decision record. [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) has the rules that apply to every change and the list of topics. Record decisions about this topic here, in the same change as the code.

- **On the workout screen, for exercises the camera can read** (`profileFor` in `profiles.ts` has a profile; holds, carries, shrugs and wrist curls don't). A **Count reps** button at the bottom left of the exercise demo turns it on, and **Stop counting** on the counting stage turns it off (`AutoCount.tsx`). It's optional and never adds a tap during a set: the reps counted fill in the reps box, so Log set stays one tap, and the lifter can still bump the number. Each logged set (or warm-up) starts a fresh count; nothing is counted during rest. If the tracker ends a set on its own after 8 s still, the next reps add to the same count.
- **While it's on, the 3D figure follows the lifter** (`LiftDemo follow`, side view) with the live count in the corner. **The camera sits beside the figure** (Melvin, 2026-11-10: "shift the 3D model left"): the figure takes the left of the stage and the camera preview the right 42%, full height, mirrored for the front camera, with a dot that turns green when the whole body is in view. **The bones the camera reads are drawn over the lifter** on the preview (Melvin, 2026-11-10: "bones drawn on the camera superimposed over my image"): accent-coloured bones and mint joints from the pose model's landmarks (`src/lib/reps/skeleton.ts`: `BONES`, and `coverPoint` to match the preview's `object-fit: cover` crop), leaving out any point the model is under 50% sure of. Drawn on a canvas in memory each frame; nothing is recorded. **Front camera by default**, with a flip button on the preview. On or off, the camera side and the source are remembered on the device (localStorage `liftsmith.autoCount`), so it stays on across exercises and workouts until switched off.
- **A lab** (Melvin, 2026-11-10: "put this behind a feature flag" in a Pro-only Labs section): Count reps shows on the workout screen only while **Settings › Labs › Camera rep counting** is on (the `labRepCount` flag, off by default, `labOn` in `src/lib/labs.ts`). Labs is Pro, so without Pro it's off whatever the flag says, and free lifters see the Labs row on Settings marked Pro (it opens the Pro screen). This replaced the `autoCount` Pro feature and PR #169's Pro-tagged Count reps button. The lab screen of 2026-11-09 (`#/lab`, the `repLab` flag) was removed once counting reached the workout screen.
- **Phone motion** counting was unreliable in Melvin's tests, so it's a second lab, **Phone motion rep counting** (the `repMotion` flag, off), shown in Labs once camera counting is on. When it's on, the stage offers "Use phone motion". (The five quick taps on the Settings title that used to switch it were removed: Labs is the one place for it.)
- Nothing about counting is saved yet beyond the reps the lifter logs.
- **One rep engine for every source** (`src/lib/reps/tracker.ts`): follows the excursion from the rest position (5-sample median, then a One Euro filter), counts an attempt each time the lifter leaves rest and comes back, and grades it when the set ends: **full**, **partial** (under 80% of the set's deepest rep or under the profile's minimum range), **failed** (didn't come back within 6 s, tracking lost for 1.5 s mid-rep, or sank back to the bottom after getting partway up). **Grinders** are full reps 50%+ slower than the set's fastest, or with a stall (under 15% of the rep's peak speed for 250 ms+). **Set length** runs from the first movement to the end of the last attempt; the set ends itself after 8 s idle. **Bar speed** is mean concentric velocity: m/s when the scale is known (camera: lifter's height and leg or arm length; motion: integrated), otherwise degrees a second. **Reps in reserve** is a rough estimate from velocity loss (after Sánchez-Medina and González-Badillo 2011).
- **Camera:** MediaPipe Pose Landmarker Lite (33 points, Apache 2.0) in the web view, GPU delegate with a CPU fallback, loaded on first use from **our own copies** (2026-11-10, security audit): `scripts/mediapipe.mjs` copies the runtime from `node_modules` (pinned by the lockfile) into `public/mediapipe/tasks-vision-<version>/` before every dev, build and static build, and the model is committed as `public/mediapipe/pose_landmarker_lite-f16-v1.task` with its SHA-256 checked in `mediapipe.test.ts`. The apps carry both inside (about 20 MB, so counting works offline); the website serves them from its own origin and the service worker caches them. No CDN. Joint angles per exercise come from `profiles.ts` (knee for squats, hip for hinges, elbow for presses, rows and curls, shoulder for raises); a side view is the setup we ask for. Frames are read in memory and dropped: **video never leaves the device and is never recorded.**
- **Motion:** `imu.ts` prefers the platform's fused data (vertical linear acceleration plus rotation rate; DeviceMotionEvent now, CMDeviceMotion on watchOS and Wear OS linear-acceleration sensors later) and falls back to raw |a| - g. Zero-velocity resets at still moments and a snap back to rest at lockout keep double-integration drift from building up. Lifts where the hands don't move (pull-ups, dips, push-ups) get no motion profile.
- **Accuracy is measured on simulated sets** (`simulate.ts`, `evaluate.ts`; pass marks in `reps.test.ts`; full report with `REP_ACCURACY=1 npx vitest run src/lib/reps`). Real recordings with hand-counted truth must replace them before this ships.
