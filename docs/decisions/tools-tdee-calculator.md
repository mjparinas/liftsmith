# Tools: TDEE calculator (Melvin, 2026-11-09)

Part of the decision record. [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) has the rules that apply to every change and the list of topics. Record decisions about this topic here, in the same change as the code.

- **Tools** (`#/tools`, from Progress and Settings) holds calculators that use what the app already knows. First is **TDEE** (`#/tools/tdee`, `src/lib/tdee.ts`, `src/components/app/Tools.tsx`). The same engine powers a public page, `/tools/tdee-calculator/` (with `/tools/` as the index), for search.
- **Formulas:** Mifflin-St Jeor for BMR, or Katch-McArdle when a body fat reading is known (lean mass suits lifters). TDEE = BMR × the standard activity factor (1.2, 1.375, 1.55, 1.725, 1.9). Results also show BMI, a cut (TDEE − 500, never below BMR), a lean bulk (+250), protein at 1.6 to 2.2 g/kg, and the TDEE at every activity level.
- **Automatic, as far as possible.** Sex, age and height come from [Your stats](your-stats.md) (Settings › Body and units), shown here read only with one link there, or a "This needs your …" note and link when missing (Melvin, 2026-11-10; the boxes on this screen were removed). Weight is the latest weigh-in; body fat stays on this screen as a choice about the formula. On native, weight, height and body fat are read from Health Connect / Apple Health on every open (`readBody` and `readBodyStats` in `health.ts`), and on iOS sex and birth year too. A newer health weight becomes a weigh-in whose id is its measurement time, so repeated reads never duplicate it.
- **Activity level from real training:** training days a week (distinct days, so doubles don't inflate it) over the last 4 weeks: 0 sedentary, 1–2 light, 3–5 moderate, 6–7 heavy, 10+ sessions a week athlete. Until there are two weeks of history it uses the days-a-week goal. The lifter can pick a level by hand; Auto goes back.
- **Tracked over time:** a `tdee` event is recorded whenever weight (0.5 kg+), age, height, sex, body fat or activity level changes, with what changed. `App.tsx` checks 2 s after any change to events or profile. The screen charts one point a day (step line to today) and lists every change. The current value is always computed fresh; the events are the history.
- **Minimal typing (Melvin's rule):** activity and body fat on/off are chips; numbers use the shared `BumpField` (hold to repeat), in the app, in Your stats and on the public page. An empty box starts from a typical value (30 years, 177 cm for men, 163 cm for women). Body fat saves one weigh-in 1.5 s after the last change, so holding + doesn't log dozens.
