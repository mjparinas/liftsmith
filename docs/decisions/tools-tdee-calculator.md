# Tools: TDEE calculator and the other tools (Melvin, 2026-11-09)

Part of the decision record. [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) has the rules that apply to every change and the list of topics. Record decisions about this topic here, in the same change as the code.

- **Tools** (`#/tools`, from Progress and Settings) holds calculators that use what the app already knows. First is **TDEE** (`#/tools/tdee`, `src/lib/tdee.ts`, `src/components/app/Tools.tsx`). The same engine powers a public page, `/tools/tdee-calculator/` (with `/tools/` as the index), for search.
- **Formulas:** Mifflin-St Jeor for BMR, or Katch-McArdle when a body fat reading is known (lean mass suits lifters). TDEE = BMR × the standard activity factor (1.2, 1.375, 1.55, 1.725, 1.9). Results also show BMI, a cut (TDEE − 500, never below BMR), a lean bulk (+250), protein at 1.6 to 2.2 g/kg, and the TDEE at every activity level.
- **Automatic, as far as possible.** Sex, age and height come from [Your stats](your-stats.md) (Settings › Body and units), shown here read only with one link there, or a "This needs your …" note and link when missing (Melvin, 2026-11-10; the boxes on this screen were removed). Weight is the latest weigh-in; body fat stays on this screen as a choice about the formula. On native, weight, height and body fat are read from Health Connect / Apple Health on every open (`readBody` and `readBodyStats` in `health.ts`), and on iOS sex and birth year too. A newer health weight becomes a weigh-in whose id is its measurement time, so repeated reads never duplicate it.
- **Activity level from real training:** training days a week (distinct days, so doubles don't inflate it) over the last 4 weeks: 0 sedentary, 1–2 light, 3–5 moderate, 6–7 heavy, 10+ sessions a week athlete. Until there are two weeks of history it uses the days-a-week goal. The lifter can pick a level by hand; Auto goes back.
- **Tracked over time:** a `tdee` event is recorded whenever weight (0.5 kg+), age, height, sex, body fat or activity level changes, with what changed. `App.tsx` checks 2 s after any change to events or profile. The screen charts one point a day (step line to today) and lists every change. The current value is always computed fresh; the events are the history.
- **Minimal typing (Melvin's rule):** activity and body fat on/off are chips; numbers use the shared `BumpField` (hold to repeat), in the app, in Your stats and on the public page. An empty box starts from a typical value (30 years, 177 cm for men, 163 cm for women). Body fat saves one weigh-in 1.5 s after the last change, so holding + doesn't log dozens.
- **Plate calculator** (`#/tools/plates`, `tools/PlateTool.tsx`; Melvin, 2026-11-11, table stakes): a weight in the active gym's own unit (a `BumpField`, 2.5 kg or 5 lb steps), the gym's bars as chips when it has more than one, and the answer as the plate graphic plus "Each side: …", from the same solver the workout uses (`loadBar`, the fewest plates). When the gym's plates can't make the weight exactly it gives the nearest loads below and above (`nearestLoads`). A gym with no plates gets an empty state that opens Gyms. The website's plate calculator page stays as it was.
- **Warm-up calculator** (`#/tools/warmup`, `tools/WarmupTool.tsx`; same day): a barbell working weight, rounded to one the gym can load, and the warm-up sets the workout itself would plan for a first barbell lift (`warmupFor` with no muscles warm yet: the empty bar, then about 50, 70 and 85% for heavy work), each with its plates, and the rest between them. Barbell only, because that's where a ramp needs working out; dumbbell and machine warm-ups stay in the workout.
- Not yet: adaptive TDEE from weight trend plus logged food (needs calorie intake), and steps from Health as an activity input.

## BMI range bar (Melvin, 2026-11-11: "for BMI can we show a graph of where people are? Or at least colour code the label")

- BMI shows on a range bar (`src/components/BmiBar.tsx`) with the standard adult bands (under 18.5, 18.5 to 25, 25 to 30, 30 and over; `BMI_CUTS` and `bmiCategory` in `tdee.ts`) and a marker on the lifter's value; the bar runs 15 to 40 and values past either end sit at the end.
- The band's name is written in its colour. Colours run cool to warm and never red, because BMI is a rough guide, not an alarm: under cyan, healthy `--color-good`, over `--color-warn`, obese ember. All are tokens, so light mode, colour-blind safe colours and more contrast follow. Colour is never the only cue: the name, the cut-offs under the bar and a screen-reader label (`bmi.where`) carry it too.
- Where: the TDEE results (app Tools › TDEE and the public calculator; BMI takes the full width, with a one-line note that BMI can't tell muscle from fat, `bmi.note`) and the BMI sheet on Body › Health, where the chip already names the band so the bar leaves its label out. The BMI chip on Body › Health takes its band's colour too, except when it says to ignore BMI for a lean lifter.
- Shared web code, so Android, iOS and the web get it together.

## Seven more tools (Melvin, 2026-11-11: picked all eight from the ranked list)

- **The hub is one list** (`TOOLS` in `Tools.tsx`): each entry has its `#/tools/<arg>`, icon, tint, title and a line that shows the lifter's own number when there is one (TDEE, how many lifts have a max, DOTS and band, FFMI, max heart rate). The same list routes the screens, so a new tool is one entry plus a file in `src/components/app/tools/`. Order: TDEE, Macros, One-rep max, Strength score, Body fat, FFMI, Heart rate zones, Interval timer, Stretching. The maths is in `src/lib/calculators.ts` and `src/lib/intervals.ts` (both under `noUncheckedIndexedAccess`), shared with the public pages; app text is `calc.*` in all six languages. Every tool is free. Shared web code, so Android, iOS and the web app get them together; no native code.
- **One-rep max** (`#/tools/onerm`): the same estimated-max list and sheet as Progress (`OneRmSection`), plus the set calculator for any weight, reps and RIR. Progress keeps its own; a link from one screen to another is fine under "one control, one destination".
- **Strength score** (`#/tools/strength`): the squat, bench and deadlift total from estimated maxes scored as DOTS, Wilks (1995 coefficients, body weight clamped as OpenPowerlifting does) and IPF GL (2020, classic three-lift; the engine also has equipped and bench-only). The action line says how much to add to the total for the next DOTS level (200/300/400/500, `nextDotsLevel`, `totalFor`), and which of the three lifts is furthest behind lifters of the same size, age and sex (`liftStanding`'s percentile), because that's usually the cheapest place to find it. Numbers can be changed with steppers; "Use my data" goes back. Glossary `liftScores`.
- **Macros** (`#/tools/macros`): goal chips (Lose fat, Maintain, Build muscle) saved as `profile.tdee.goal`; calories from the live TDEE (cut 500 under but never below BMR, lean bulk +250, the same as the TDEE results). Protein first: 2.2 g/kg on a cut, 1.8 to maintain or build. Fat a quarter of calories, never under 0.6 g/kg. Carbs the rest. Each number has its reason under it, and the advice line splits protein into meals of 30 to 40 g. Links to TDEE for how calories are worked out.
- **Body fat** (`#/tools/bodyfat`): the US Navy tape method (Hodgdon and Beckett 1984) from the waist and neck, plus hips for women, prefilled from the latest tape measurements on Body and photos, and height from Your stats. One primary button saves the estimate as today's body fat on a weigh-in (`logBodyweight`) and logs any changed measurement, so TDEE (Katch-McArdle), FFMI, the twin and coach's notes pick it up.
- **FFMI** (`#/tools/ffmi`): lean mass over height squared and normalized to 1.8 m (Kouri 1995), from the latest weigh-in, body fat and height; bands below 18, 18, 20, 22, 23, 25+ for men and 4 lower for women ("Rare without drugs" at the top, after Kouri's finding). The action line is the lean mass to the next band. Without a body fat reading it shows an empty state whose one button opens Body fat. Glossary `ffmi`.
- **Heart rate zones** (`#/tools/hrzones`): maximum heart rate from age (Tanaka, 208 − 0.7 × age) unless the lifter enters their own (`profile.hrMax`, 120 to 230; "Use the estimate" clears it). With a resting heart rate from Health Connect or Apple Health the zones switch to heart rate reserve (Karvonen); chips switch back to % of max. Zones 50–60–70–80–90–100%, the same cut-offs as `hrZone()`, in the `--hr-*` colours with what each is for. The action line: between heavy sets, start the next once back under the top of zone 2. Glossary `hrZones`. `hrZone()` itself has no callers yet; using `hrMax` in live heart rate displays is a follow-up.
- **Interval timer** (`#/tools/intervals`): EMOM (every N seconds, N rounds), Tabata (20/10 × 8) and custom work, rest and rounds, last used plan kept in localStorage (`liftsmith.intervals`, a convenience only). A 5-second count-in. The state is worked out from the start time each tick (`stateAt`), so a locked phone never drifts; it ticks every 200 ms only while running, and keeps the screen awake while running. Sounds and haptics from the shared set: soft counts and `select` in the last 3 seconds of each phase, the rest-over chime and `thud` when work starts, the log pair and `tap` when rest starts, the fanfare and `celebrate` at the end. A polite live region says each change. Pause, Resume, Reset.
