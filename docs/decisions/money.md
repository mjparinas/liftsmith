# Money

Part of the decision record. [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) has the rules that apply to every change and the list of topics. Record decisions about this topic here, in the same change as the code.

- **No ads, ever** (Melvin, 2026-11-09). No ad SDKs, sponsored placements, interstitials or rewarded videos, in either app or on the site. The subscription is the only revenue.
- **Prices (confirmed by Melvin, 2026-11-09):** Pro is $6.99 a month or $39.99 a year. The annual plan is pushed as "about $3.33 a month, less than half of Fitbod". **No lifetime plan.** (Lifetime Pro *codes* exist as free comps from us, never sold: [Marketing and growth](marketing-and-growth.md), 2026-11-11.)
- **Trial (Melvin, 2026-11-09, revenue forecast; replaces the earlier 7-day trial on the annual plan):** every new user sees a **14-day Pro trial offer as the last step of setup**, annual plan first, then monthly, then "Not now, keep the free plan". Anyone who declines keeps the free logger and 5x5. The offer isn't shown again on "Run setup again". The length comes from the `trialDays` flag (`src/lib/flags.ts`, 7 or 14) so it can be A/B tested. Why: about 82% of trials start on install day, and trials of 17+ days convert at 42.5% against 25.5% for 4 days or fewer (RevenueCat data; forecast doc https://claude.ai/artifact/CiXkRo29nsLCuTzKYkQfx7). The offer's buttons start the store's own trial (`Plans` in `Pro.tsx`), and the choice is also recorded on the profile (`profile.pro`, `trial.ts`). **Until billing is live the offer step is hidden** (`billingLive()` in `src/lib/billing.ts`; Melvin, 2026-11-09), because Play and App Store review reject a priced offer with nothing to buy behind it; setup then ends on the notifications step. Neither plan is pre-selected: each is its own button.
- **Free tier:** the workout logger, plate graphic, rest timer and live notification, "why this weight", remix links, what to buy next and machine settings, "Short on time" during a workout, Health Connect and Apple Health, one adaptive workout a week, plus **every fixed program** (Melvin, 2026-11-09: classic 5×5, novice 3×5, 5×5 plus arms, ramping 5×5, tiered progression, training max waves, bodyweight skills, and dumbbell full body since 2026-11-12; built, see "Programs"). **Pro:** adaptive programming (generator, progression, RIR blocks and deloads), the **adaptive splits** (push/pull/legs, upper/lower; Melvin, 2026-11-09), the recovery map, advanced stats (in the app and on the web dashboard), health signals (sleep, HRV, resting HR, breathing rate) moving today's call (`healthReadiness`; seeing the health twin is free, Melvin, 2026-11-09), working around taken equipment mid-workout, the cloud connections, Labs, coaching clients (`coaching`, out of Labs on 2026-11-12; being a client stays free, [Trainer mode](trainer-mode.md)), the plateau detective and the goal-date planner. The split gate is `adaptiveSplits` in `entitlements.ts`.
- **Free vs Pro rebalance** (Melvin picked "all recommended moves", 2026-11-12, after an audit against Fitbod, Hevy, Strong and StrongLifts: Fitbod has no free tier at $95.99 a year; Hevy caps free at 4 routines and 3 months of history and keeps sets per muscle Pro at $23.99; Strong caps free at 3 routines and keeps every chart Pro at $29.99). Our free tier was the most generous of them, and Pro was mostly "adaptive workouts", while the paywall promised advanced stats that the app gave away. So:
  - *Advanced stats are Pro in the app too* (`advancedStats`): Progress's weekly sets per muscle (a locked card, `#/pro/stats`), the trend line beside each estimated max (the estimate and its calculator stay free), and an exercise page's "Which way it's going" (a locked card, `#/pro/lift`). Personal bests, history, the strength score, tonnage and the 1RM calculator stay free.
  - *The cloud connections are Pro* (`cloudConnections`): connecting Strava, Oura, WHOOP, Withings, Polar or Concept2 opens the paywall (`#/pro/connections`) without Pro; a service already connected pauses (no pulls, no Strava uploads; `connections.ts`) and shows "Paused · Resume with Pro", and Disconnect always works. When Pro comes back the next pull fetches the last 45 days, so nothing is lost. Health Connect, Apple Health, Bluetooth heart rate, the Garmin watch app and music stay free: they're the phone's own data or need no server. Why: integrations are where Liftsmith means to win, and the server keeps their tokens.
  - *One adaptive workout a week is free* (`src/lib/freeAdaptive.ts`): without Pro, Today shows "Try adaptive today · 1 free this week" beside the free program. It swaps today's program day for the adaptive plan (`store.tryAdaptive`, `setTryAdaptive`; the program's next day stays next), and "Back to <program>" undoes it. A week holds one when no adaptive workout was started in it (no program, no routine, not logged by hand), counted from the event log with the lifter's own first day of the week. Once used, the chip is the "Adaptive workouts · Pro" chip again. Health signals still don't move a free lifter's call. Why: the free tier never showed the main thing Pro does.
  - *The free plan fits the gym* (`freeProgramFor` in `programs.ts`, used by `store.applyAccess`): the classic 5×5 where the gym has a barbell, rack and bench, the new free **dumbbell full body** where it has dumbbells, and bodyweight skills otherwise (it used to be the classic 5×5 made of stand-ins everywhere).
  - *"Short on time" is free*: the ⏱ button on the workout screen opens the sheet for everyone; inside it, the "Bench or rack taken?" part is Pro for free lifters (one tagged button to the paywall, `replan`). The feature row on the paywall is now "Work around taken equipment".
  - Not changed: no cap on routines, no limit on history (it would go against offline-first and the event log), injury mode stays free (safety), coach's notes, watch apps and Friends stay free.
  - Pure web code (entitlements, screens, the programs engine and the store), so Android, iOS and the web app get it together; no native code changed. Text in all six languages (`pro.*`, `td.freeAdaptive.*`, `progress.weekSets.lock`, `lift.trend.lock`, `conn.proPaused`, `pgm.dumbbell-full-body.*`). The Play listing's free/Pro line in `docs/PLAY_LISTING.md` follows; the live store listing changes when it's pasted into Play Console.
- **The first two weeks: trial to paid** (Melvin picked it, 2026-11-12, from the focus-area ranking: "when the 14-day trial ends, show what Pro actually did for that lifter, and add a couple of well-timed Pro moments during the trial; make sure the Funnel tab can measure it"). The trial has to prove Pro, so instead of saying what Pro could do, the app shows what it did for this lifter. Code: `src/lib/trialRecap.ts` (the numbers, an engine file), `src/lib/trialNotes.ts` (the sentences and the coach's notes), `src/components/app/TrialMoments.tsx` (`useTrial`, the paywall recap and the end-of-workout line). Text: `trl.*`.
  - *What counts:* from the event log, between the trial's start (recorded on the profile when the trial began: the paywall, setup's last step, the web's return from Stripe) and its end (the store's expiry while it's a trial, else the recorded length): **adaptive workouts** planned (`wasAdaptive`: not a program day, a routine or a workout logged by hand), **weights raised** (a lift's top planned working weight above its time before, warm-ups, bonus sets, injury and comeback sets left out), **days adjusted** to recovery (a "go easy" or "push" call the lifter didn't override), and the **biggest jump** (the lift whose planned weight rose most, as a share, from the time before the trial's first raise to the last time in it). Nothing is stored.
  - *The moments* (only while billing is live, and never once the lifter pays): a **line at the end of each adaptive workout** in the trial ("Pro raised back squat today. 6 days left in your trial."), shown only when Pro changed something; a **coach's note at the halfway point** (what it did so far, one tip that makes it work better: rate each lift between sets; "See everything in Pro"); a note in the **last two days** that, for a trial that will renew, only says when it becomes the subscription and that it can be cancelled in the store (no button, nothing to do), and for a cancelled one says when Pro stops, with **Keep Pro** (the Pro screen, whose Manage opens the store's own page); and for **two weeks after a lapsed trial**, "Your Pro trial has ended" with what Pro did and **Get Pro back** (the paywall). With no adaptive workout yet, the note asks for one instead. Trial notes go first in coach's notes (they last only a few days), and "Got it" hides each one; cancelling brings the last-days note back with its new wording. **The paywall** shows "What Pro did in your trial" (or "so far") at the top, three numbers, the sentence and the biggest jump, before the feature list. No countdowns, no discounts: the same honest rules as the rest of the paywall.
  - *Measured* (the admin **Funnel** tab): each moment sends `trial_recap` once per place, stage and trial (`where`: note, paywall or done; the stage and the three counts; no weights). The tab adds "The first two weeks: trial to paid" (trials started by the period's installs, trained in the trial, three or more workouts in it, paid; trial workouts are `workout_finish` events in the 14 days after the install's first `trial_start`) and "Trials that ended, by what happened in them" (trial-to-paid for none, 1 or 2, and 3+ workouts, and for saw what Pro did against didn't).
  - Pure web code, so Android, iOS and the web app get it together; no native code changed. The trial-ending reminder (`rmd.trialEnds`, two days before) is planned on the server, which has no workout data, so it keeps its plain wording.
- **Family sharing on the annual plan only** (Melvin, 2026-11-09), through Google Play's and the App Store's own family sharing; the monthly plan stays individual. Turn it on for the annual product in both store consoles when billing lands, and make the entitlement check accept a family-shared purchase on both platforms.
- **Billing is native, through RevenueCat** (Melvin chose it over wiring Play Billing and StoreKit directly, 2026-11-09). `@revenuecat/purchases-capacitor` sits on top of Google Play Billing and StoreKit, so the store's own sheet takes the payment, which is what both stores require. RevenueCat checks receipts, tracks renewals, grace periods, refunds and family sharing, and sends one webhook for both stores. It's free up to $2.5k a month in revenue, then 1%. Leaving it later is possible, because the stores own the subscriptions. Code: `src/lib/billing.ts` (SDK, entitlement, offers, buy, restore, manage), `src/components/app/Pro.tsx` (the paywall `#/pro` and the `Plans` buttons reused by setup), and Settings › Liftsmith Pro.
  - *Products:* Play has one subscription `pro` with base plans `monthly` and `annual`, each with a 14-day free-trial offer. The App Store has a subscription group "Pro" with `pro_monthly` and `pro_annual`, each with a 14-day free introductory offer. RevenueCat has an entitlement `pro` on all four and a current offering with `$rc_monthly` and `$rc_annual` packages. The `trialDays` flag at 7 picks an offering named `trial7` when one exists, so the A/B test needs a second set of 7-day offers there. Prices and trial lengths shown are the store's own (`priceString`, the intro price); iOS checks trial eligibility, and Android only offers a trial to people who can still get one.
  - *Live only when built with a key:* `NEXT_PUBLIC_REVENUECAT_ANDROID_KEY` and `NEXT_PUBLIC_REVENUECAT_IOS_KEY` (public SDK keys, from the `REVENUECAT_ANDROID_KEY` and `REVENUECAT_IOS_KEY` secrets in `release.yml`). Without a key (CI, the web app, tests) billing is off: everyone has Pro and no price is shown anywhere in the app, setup included. The release build stops if a key has the wrong prefix (Android `goog_`, the Play Store app's public key; iOS `appl_`), because a wrong key (a secret `sk_` or Test Store `test_` key) builds fine but every store call then fails with "Invalid API Key" (2026-11-10, the first Android test build).
  - *Entitlement:* `isPro()` is `hasPro()`: Pro while billing isn't live, otherwise the store's `pro` entitlement. The last answer is cached in localStorage, so Pro works offline and the first paint is right, with 3 days of grace past the last expiry seen (`OFFLINE_GRACE_MS`). The RevenueCat user id is the analytics install id (random, not an advertising id), so webhook rows join the install. A reinstall or "Erase this device" makes a new id; Restore purchases brings Pro back from the store account. Entitlements move to Liftsmith accounts when sign-in lands (the server's `entitlements` table is ready for it).
  - *Without Pro:* Today follows the free classic 5×5 (`store.applyAccess`, which keeps the lifter's own plan in `profile.proPlan` and restores it when Pro arrives), the recovery map shows a locked state, and Pro programs, splits and coaching tools open the paywall. Fixed programs, routines and the logger stay free.
  - *Shown, never hidden* (Melvin, 2026-11-10: "pro features are not hidden but have a small pro tag on it… we want to let people know what they are missing"). Every Pro entry point stays on screen for free lifters with the shared **`ProTag`** (`ui.tsx`, the small amber "Pro" pill) and opens the paywall at that feature (`#/pro/<from>`) instead of doing nothing or disappearing. The screen reader name says "…, a Pro feature: see Pro" (`pro.locked`). Today: an "Adaptive workouts · Pro" chip beside the free program (`today`). Programs: the adaptive plan and the splits (`programs`, `splits`). Recovery: the locked map (`recovery`). Health twin: "Make today an easy day" and "See Pro" when Pro would call the day differently (`twin`). Workout: "Bench or rack taken?" inside the change-the-plan sheet (`replan`; the button itself and "Short on time" are free since 2026-11-12) and Count reps (`count`, PR #169). Progress: the goal planner section (`goals`), weekly sets per muscle (`stats`) and, on a single stalled lift, coach's note button "Find the cause" (`plateau`). Exercise pages: "Which way it's going" (`lift`). Connected apps: Connect on each cloud service (`connections`). The web dashboard's advanced stats already show a locked panel. No Pro tag ever adds a tap during a set: the buttons were already there for Pro lifters.
  - *The paywall says exactly what's gated.* "What Pro unlocks" lists one row per feature in `PRO_FEATURES` (`entitlements.ts`) with a line on what it does (`pro.ft.<feature>`, `pro.fb.<feature>`), so a new Pro feature can't be left off (`Pro.test.tsx`). The feature the lifter tapped is moved to the top and marked "You tapped this" (`proFrom` maps the route argument to the feature and the screen behind it). Under the list, one line says what stays free. Setup's trial step shows the same list as titles only.
  - *Paywall rules:* annual first, neither plan pre-selected (each is its own button), the trial length and the price after it in the button, the store's renewal and cancellation terms, Restore purchases, our Terms of use (`/terms/`, plus Apple's standard licence agreement on iOS, as Apple expects) and the privacy policy, and Manage subscription opening the store's own page. No countdowns or fake discounts.
  - *Thank-you after buying* (Melvin, 2026-11-10: "when you subscribe and it's successful, there should be confetti, haptic feedback and a congratulations for subscribing and taking your health seriously"): a purchase or trial start that unlocks Pro, from the paywall or setup's last step, runs `celebratePro` (`ProWelcome.tsx`): big confetti, the `celebrate` haptic and the fanfare, then a sheet that thanks the lifter for subscribing and taking their health seriously, lists what Pro unlocked (`ProFeatures`), and for a trial says how many days are free and that it can be cancelled in the store. "Let's train" goes to Today. Restore purchases, a cancelled or failed purchase get none of it. Confetti and the sound follow Lean mode and reduce motion, the buzz follows Settings › Vibration; the sheet always shows. It's rendered by `App.tsx` over every screen (setup included), because a purchase moves on straight away. Web code, so Android and iOS get it together (haptics through `@capacitor/haptics` on both); on the web app it shows when the lifter comes back from Stripe Checkout and the account has Pro (`#/pro/paid`). Text: `pro.welcome.*`.
  - *Pro members show it* (Melvin, 2026-11-11: "If a user is pro there should be a pro badge or something on the main page so they can show off … that should unlock some kind of badge or achievement or reward"). **Who counts:** `proMember()` / `useProMember()` in `billing.ts`, a store subscription or trial (Play or App Store through RevenueCat, creator and promo codes included, since those are store offers) or Pro on the signed-in account (lifetime codes, the store review account, and the web subscription once it sets the account's Pro). Unlike `hasPro()`, a build without billing (the web app today, tests) gives nobody the badge, so it always means a real member. **Today:** a small **Pro badge** beside the title (`ProBadge` in `ui.tsx`: the brand gradient with its own ink, a sparkle, "Pro", read as "Liftsmith Pro member"), deliberately unlike the amber `ProTag` that marks a locked feature. - **Pro on the web, through Stripe** (Melvin, 2026-11-11, from the features brainstorm: "gate Pro on the web app with Stripe subscriptions, sharing entitlement with RevenueCat/Play purchases and creator/lifetime codes"). Code: `src/server/stripe.ts` (Stripe's REST API through `fetch`: no SDK and no Stripe.js, so no outside script on the site), routes under `src/app/api/billing/stripe/` (GET plans, POST checkout, `portal/`, `webhook/`), `src/server/access.ts`, and the web half of `src/lib/billing.ts`.
  - *Same plans, same prices:* one product with a monthly and an annual recurring price in USD, the same $6.99 and $39.99. The paywall shows Stripe's own price in the app's language (`money()`), annual first with "about $3.33 a month", neither pre-selected, the trial and the price after it on the button, "Paid by card through Stripe" terms, our terms of use and privacy links, the same rules as the stores'.
  - *Needs an account.* A web subscription belongs to the Liftsmith account, never to a browser, so it survives cleared site data and unlocks the phones too. Signed out, the paywall shows the plans with a "Sign in or create an account" card, and a plan button says to sign in first. Stripe gets the account's email, account id, install id and code as metadata, nothing else.
  - *Checkout and manage:* a plan button asks the server for a Stripe Checkout page (`checkoutUrl`) and the browser goes there; Stripe returns to `/app/#/pro/paid` (or `#/pro/settings` when cancelled), always our own `SITE_URL`, never an address from the request. `#/pro/paid` waits up to about 12 seconds for the webhook to reach the account, then runs the thank-you (`celebratePro`). Manage subscription opens Stripe's customer portal (plan change, card, cancel, invoices). "Restore purchases" on the web is "Already subscribed? Check again": it asks the account.
  - *Trials:* the trialDays A/B (7 or 14), or a live creator or promo code's own trial (30 days) first, set as the subscription's trial; none for an account that already had a web subscription or a store subscription on a linked phone (`trialEligible`), so nobody gets the trial twice by switching platforms. A card is taken at checkout, as in the stores. Promo codes that give a discount offering in the stores give no discount on the web yet (Stripe coupons can come later); their trial days still apply.
  - *One Pro, any platform (`access.ts` `accessFor`):* the account has Pro from, in order, the store review account, a lifetime code (`pro_grants`), a web subscription (`stripe_subscriptions` in trialing, active or past_due, and not more than 3 days past its period end, like the apps' offline grace), or a Play or App Store subscription on a phone linked to the account. A phone is linked when it syncs while signed in (the sync request carries its install id, which is RevenueCat's user id; `account_installs`), so its `entitlements` row from the RevenueCat webhook counts on the web. Sync and `GET /api/account/` send `pro`, `proVia` and `sub`; `hasPro()` adds the account's Pro on every platform. So a web subscriber who signs in on Android or iPhone has Pro there, and a Play subscriber who signs in on the web has Pro there.
  - *No paying twice:* checkout answers 409 when the account already has Pro from anywhere, and the paywall says so. On a phone, Pro from the account shows where it comes from as plain text ("from a subscription on the Liftsmith website… manage or cancel it there"), with **no link or price** to the website, because Google Play's and Apple's rules don't allow pointing in-app buyers to another way to pay (Platform deltas). On the web, a store subscription says to manage it in that store.
  - *Setup's offer step stays native-only.* The web app's setup ends without the trial offer (it would need a sign-in in the middle of setup); the Pro tags and the paywall sell it there.
  - *The webhook* (`storeStripeEvent`, signature checked with the endpoint secret in constant time within 5 minutes) keeps each subscription's status, plan, period end, trial end and cancel-at-period-end, newest event wins. Live events also write `subscription_events` with store `web` and source `store` (Stripe signed them, so they count for creator commission like the stores' webhook): `trial_start`, `purchase` or `trial_convert` on the first paid invoice, `renew`, `cancel`, `uncancel`, `expire` (reason `billing` when the card failed), `refund`. USD amounts fill `usd_micros`; other currencies keep their own amount only. The creator code from the metadata is claimed for the install (`claimFromStore`), and a new paying customer emails the admins like the stores do. Test-mode events are ignored by a live key and the other way round.
  - *Deleting the account cancels its web subscription in Stripe straight away* (`forgetStripe`) and removes the link rows; the in-app delete confirmation says so when there is one. A store subscription isn't touched (it's the store's).
  - *Off until set up:* with any of `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`, `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, `STRIPE_PRICE_MONTHLY` and `STRIPE_PRICE_ANNUAL` missing, `GET /api/billing/stripe/` says `live: false` and the web app stays fully unlocked, as before. The answer is cached in localStorage so the first paint and offline use are right. The static export (CI, browser tests) has no server, so it never asks.
  - Pure web code for the selling, and the account flag reaches Android and iOS through the shared web layer, so no native code changed.
  - *Set up and live (2026-11-11).* Stripe account "GetLiftsmith" (live) plus a "GetLiftsmith sandbox" for tests. One product, Liftsmith Pro, with tax code `txcd_10103000` (software as a service, personal use) and two USD prices (lookup keys `pro_monthly` and `pro_annual`). The account uses Stripe's **Managed Payments**, on by default: Stripe is the merchant of record and works out, collects and files sales tax and VAT, for a higher fee than plain Stripe. It refuses a checkout for a product with no tax code, so every new product needs one. Customer portal: cancel at the end of the period with a reason, switch between monthly and yearly with proration, card and address updates, invoices. Webhook "Liftsmith Pro on the web" with the five events above. Vercel: Production holds the live key (created as "full access except sensitive operations", so it can't move money out), the live webhook secret and the live price ids; Preview holds the sandbox ones. A test purchase in the sandbox reached the account through the webhook before the live keys went in.
