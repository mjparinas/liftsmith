# Delightful extras (Melvin, 2026-11-09: "build them all")

Part of the decision record. [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) has the rules that apply to every change and the list of topics. Record decisions about this topic here, in the same change as the code.

Small things that make the app a pleasure, all derived from events (never stored), all between sets or optional (never an extra tap during a set), and quiet in lean mode. The badges for tonnage and comebacks belong to the gamification work (`achievements.ts`); these files only celebrate.
- **Lifetime tonnage** (`moments.ts`): a running total on Progress and "you've lifted a ..." milestones (grand piano 500 kg up to the Eiffel Tower's iron 7,300 t), each labelled "about".
- **Anniversaries and comebacks** (`moments.ts`): "1 year since your first workout" on Today (29 February is celebrated on the 28th). **Day one against today** (Melvin, 2026-11-14: "a yearly recap of your first workout versus today"): the anniversary is its own card (`AnniversaryCard` in `Extras.tsx`, from `anniversaryLook`), shown for a week from the day (`ANNIVERSARY_DAYS`, so a lifter who doesn't open the app that day still sees it) until ✕ (dismissed once for the whole week, keyed by the anniversary's day). It puts the first workout beside today in a small table (workouts, sets, total lifted) and the lift from that first workout that has grown most by estimated one-rep max ("Bench press is up 50% since day one"; none when nothing went up), with one Share button for its card (`anniversaryShare`: the years, the date of day one, the growth, totals, and the lift's then and now only when weights are shown). It needs a second workout, so nobody gets an anniversary for one session. a warm welcome back after 14+ days away (readiness already eases the plan after 10), and "comeback complete", workout-count and tonnage moments on the summary.
- **Monthly recap and year in lifting** (`recap.ts`, `#/recap`): workouts, volume, sets and reps, PRs, top lift and muscle, favourite day and time of day, heaviest bar, change against the period before, average heart rate. Last month is offered in a month's first week; the year from 26 December to 7 January. Shared as text for now; it moves to the gamification share card (`shareImage.ts`) once that lands.
- **PR replay** (`PrReplay.tsx`): on the summary, each new best replays rep by rep: the 3D figure (`LiftDemo`) performs the lift that many times, with the lifter's own bar and plates underneath and a rep counter. Reduced motion shows a still pose.
- **Smart plate memory** (`plateMemory.ts`): what's on the bar is the last set or warm-up of the exercise this session; plates go on heaviest first, so the shared inside run stays and only the outside changes ("Take off 2.5, then add 5 kg each side").
- **Ghost mode** (`ghost.ts`): the last session of the exercise races along on the rest card: its weight, reps and rest for this set, and how far ahead or behind the lifter is. On by default.
- **Same as last time** (`ghost.ts`): one tap on Today repeats the last workout's exercises and set counts (weights still come from progression, exercises the gym can't do are dropped); on the set card, one optional tap uses last time's numbers.
- **Voice coach** (`voice.ts`, `VoiceCoachPlugin` on both platforms): off by default. Says the next set ("Set two of four. 100 kilos for 8. two in reserve."), the last set, ten seconds left, a new exercise, a new best and the end of the workout. Music is ducked while it talks. See Platform deltas.
- **Rest extras** (`WorkoutExtras.tsx`): a breathing guide or a plate-math mini-game built from the gym's own plates. Off by default; picked on the rest card or in Settings.
- **The breathing guide is box breathing** (Melvin, 2026-11-12: "make it box breathing? Maybe even customizable"; it was in 4 s, out 6 s). Patterns in `src/lib/breathing.ts`: **box breathing** (in, hold, out, hold, 4 s each; the default), **4-7-8**, the **double sigh** (physiological sigh: in 2, a 1 s sip on top, out 6), **slow breaths** (the old in 4, out 6) and **your own** (each side of the box from a − / + stepper, in and out 1 to 12 s, holds 0 to 12 s, a hold of 0 leaves it out). Picked in Settings › Workout under While you rest once the breathing guide is on (`profile.delight.breath`, `breathCustom`), with the `breathPattern` glossary "i"; the rest card stays simple (one guide, no picker) so nothing is added between the lifter and the next set. The card draws a box: a dot travels round it, up the left side while breathing in, across the top on the hold, down the right breathing out and back along the bottom, one side per step for box breathing (proportional to the cycle for the others), while a square inside grows and shrinks with the breath and the seconds left in the step count down in its middle. Under it: the step ("Breathe in through the nose", "Hold", …), the pattern and its counts ("Box breathing · 4-4-4-4") and one line on why it helps. **Each change of step gives a soft cue**, so it can be followed with eyes closed: a `select` haptic and `sound.breath` (a quiet rising pair to breathe in, falling to breathe out, one note to hold, in the app's pentatonic set), both following the Sounds and Vibration switches. Pure web code, so Android, iOS and the web get it together.
- **General warm-up**: "Warm up first" on Today opens the day's warm-up stretches (#/stretch/warmup). It replaced the old checklist sheet (`warmupRoutine.ts`, 2026-11-11); the drills now live in the stretch library. See [Stretching](stretching.md). It complements the per-exercise warm-up sets in `warmup.ts`.
- **Strength score and strength twins** (`strength.ts`): each lift's best estimated max from the last 6 months against a typical intermediate of the same body weight (`Exercise.start`), optionally adjusted for age (masters-style coefficients, on by default when a birth year is known) and sex. 50 is typical, 75 twice as strong. Twins shows "stronger than about N%" on the big lifts. **It's an estimate from standards, not a ranking of real users**, and the app says so; real rankings need accounts.
- **Earned themes and app icons** (`themes.ts`): full palettes unlocked by **badges** (Melvin, 2026-11-11: one set of goals, themes are their rewards; `badge` on each theme, `unlockContext`): Forge from Tonnage bronze (10 t), Glacier from Showing up silver (25 workouts), Volt from Personal bests silver (10 PRs), Aurora from Unbroken silver (8 goal weeks running), Rose from Set collector silver (1,000 sets; anyone with 50 workouts keeps it, its old rule), Abyss from Tonnage silver (100 t), and Royal at the first liftiversary. The locked tile says "Earn the Showing up · Silver badge", and the badge's row on Awards says "Silver unlocks the Glacier theme", plus seasonal Pumpkin (October) and Frost (December) open to everyone. A cosmetic accent from the gamification work wins over a theme's accent. Four themes come with an app icon (Forge, Glacier, Aurora, Royal), drawn by `scripts/make-icons.mjs` with the main icon (see "App icon and favicon"): Android launcher aliases (`AppIconPlugin.java`, `.Icon*` in the manifest; static shortcuts sit on every alias) and iOS alternate icons (`AppIcon-*` sets, `ASSETCATALOG_COMPILER_ALTERNATE_APPICON_NAMES`). A test checks every theme's text contrast (WCAG AA).
- **Body weight and progress photos** (`photos.ts`, `#/photos`): weigh-ins are `bodyweight` events (synced like other data) and update the profile. **Photos never leave the phone**: their own IndexedDB database, outside the event log, so sync, backups, analytics and feedback never see them; shrunk to 1440 px JPEG, which drops location data; erased with "Erase this device". Front, side and back, with a before/after slider. iOS asks for the camera with `NSCameraUsageDescription`.
- **Check in** (`buddy.ts`; replaced the gym buddy share sheet on 2026-11-14, Melvin: "i want this to send a message to friends not coaches. it should just show up as a phone notification, and when they open the app, a permanent but dismisable toast message"): the chip tells mutual friends the lifter is at the gym, as a push and a toast. Details in [Friends](friends.md) "Check in". The buddy names typed on the device are no longer shown or used (`profile.delight.buddies` stays in old profiles, unread).
- **Holiday and seasonal challenges** (`seasonal.ts`): dated windows in local time (fresh start week, spring, solstice, back to it, Halloween "6.66 t", twelve days of lifting, last rep of the year), counted from workouts, sets, days or tonnage, never per muscle. Shown on Today in the window; no XP or badges here (the gamification code may add XP from `seasonalChallenges()`; its own date badges cover 1 Jan, 29 Feb, 14 Mar and 31 Oct).
- Settings: Voice coach, Ghost mode, Smart plate memory, While you rest, Themes, App icon (native). All live in `profile.delight`.
- **Polish pass** (Melvin, 2026-11-10: picked "build it all" from a list of ten; none adds a tap during a set, and Lean mode and reduce motion still cut the motion):
  - *Undo the last set* (`undoable` and `undoLast` in `store/workout.ts`, `UndoChip` in `workout/Polish.tsx`): an Undo pill above the big button after every Log set (working set or warm-up), offered until that rest ends (or 10 s with no rest) or the next set is logged. It deletes the set (and its reps-left rating) and puts the set card back exactly as it was, so the lifter fixes the numbers and logs again; that is also how a wrong set is corrected. Not saved: a reload drops it.
  - *Feel the countdown*: a light haptic tick on each of the last three seconds of rest, with the existing countdown beeps, when the rest-over alert includes vibration. Android's workout service does it natively so it works locked; iOS and the web do it while the app is open (Platform deltas).
  - *Heart-rate-ready rest* (`restReady.ts`, `HrReadyLine`): with a monitor connected, after at least 45 s of rest, once the heart rate is back within 5 bpm of where the last set started (and the set raised it 10+), the rest card says so with one light tick. It never ends the rest by itself (the heart-rate rest timer lab does; Heart rate and health).
  - *What's next* (`nextUp.ts`, `NextUpCard` on Done): the next training day and time from the lifter's schedule (`trainingDays`, `calendar.time`, never later the same day), which of today's muscles will be ready by then, how long the slowest one needs, and which big muscles will be fresh, from the recovery model run forward. Derived, never stored.
  - *One tip at a time* (`tips.ts`, `TipCard`): at most one tip per workout, on the rest card, never on the first rest; each shows once (marked seen when it appears, "Got it" hides it). Tips that don't apply are skipped (voice coach already on, nothing left to reorder, hands-free only in the native apps). Kept on the device in localStorage.
  - *Last set*: on the workout's final working set, Log set reads "Last set" and the burst is bigger.
  - *Plate change, drawn*: plate memory's line on the rest card also shows the plates coming off sliding out (struck through) and the new ones sliding on.
  - *Drag to reorder* (`reorder.ts`, `Reorder.tsx`, `moveExercise`): press and hold an exercise on Today or in Coming up, then drag; holding without dragging (or, on Today, tapping the exercise's number) shows move up, move down and done buttons, the no-drag way that WCAG 2.5.7 asks for; Alt + arrow keys work on Coming up. Moving an exercise takes it out of its superset (its partners stay linked) and dropping it between linked exercises splits them. Mid-workout only exercises still to come move. Today's order and links are kept when the plan is rebuilt with the same exercises (`order` in the plan slice).
  - *Hands-free Log set* (`HandsFree.tsx`, Settings › Workout): a short guide to the shortcuts that already exist: the notification buttons and Quick Settings tile and widget on Android; the notification, Back Tap and the Action button on iOS (they run the Log set App Shortcut); manifest shortcuts on the web.
  - The smarter widget idea was already built (today's call, why, goal and coach's note, #150).
- **Dancing figure easter egg** (Melvin, 2026-11-11: "if you tap on it 5 times, can you get it to do a popular dance"). Five quick taps on any 3D figure (at most 0.9 s apart; the squatting lifter on the welcome screen is the obvious one) and it dances for sixteen seconds, then goes back to its exercise; the routine ends on the squat's first frame so there's no jump. **Zombie-dance homage** (Melvin, 2026-11-14: make it follow the famous zombie music video dance, as a parody on a 3D figure): the signature, widely known moves of that routine in a similar order and feel, reinterpreted for our wireframe lifter rather than copied frame by frame: the rise from a slump with a head snap, the lurching zombie walk forward, head snaps with a shoulder shimmy, the claw walk (claws up by one side of the head, step and drag, then the other way), the claw swipe from low to high, hip thrusts on the beat, and the group pose (one foot forward, leaning in, claws raised). The beat-driven parts run at about 118 beats a minute (`src/lib/demo/dance.ts`, `DANCE_ID`; `dance.test.ts` checks each move where it plays). No music, no footage, and the song's title, the artist's name and likeness never appear in the app or the code. The head tips sideways through the rig's `headTilt` (only the dance uses it); the rig has no trunk twist, so the moves live in the arms, legs, hips, shoulders and head. A `celebrate` haptic plays as it starts (Capacitor Haptics on Android and iOS, `navigator.vibrate` on the web), so all three get it from the same web code. Off in Lean mode and with reduced motion, while the figure follows the lifter (camera counting) or replays reps (PR replay), and on still figures; the public website's demos have it too, without the buzz. On the workout screen the first tap already opens the how-to sheet, so the egg lives in that sheet's figure there.
- **Three more easter eggs** (Melvin, 2026-11-14: "Do midnight lifter, tap logo 7 times, plate math"; `src/lib/eggs.ts`). Checked first that none repeats an existing one: "Before the sun" is a start from 4 to 6 am and "Century" is exactly 100 kg or lb, so neither overlaps. All pure web code, so Android, iOS and the web get them together.
  - *Night owl*: a secret award (`night-owl`, 🦉) for finishing a workout with sets in it between midnight and 4 am local time. It's the finish that counts (the `sessionEnd` event), so a workout started at 11:50 pm that ends at 12:05 am earns it. Midnight to 4 am leaves 4 to 6 am to "Before the sun".
  - *Seven taps on the logo*: the app logo at the foot of Settings (and in the desktop sidebar) opens a "Lifetime, in other units" sheet after seven quick taps (at most 0.9 s apart, `LOGO_TAPS`): the lifetime total, then about how many African elephants, T. rexes, double-decker buses, family cars, grand pianos, golden retrievers and blue whales it comes to (`inUnits`; rough masses, one decimal under 10). Seven taps on the logo, not the figure, so it never clashes with the dance's five taps on the 3D figure. The logo is out of the Tab order and hidden from screen readers, because it's a secret; the sheet is an ordinary sheet. A `celebrate` haptic and the badge sound as it opens.
  - *Plate math*: logging a working set at exactly 420 or 1337 (in kg, or the kg that is exactly that many pounds) shows a one-line joke toast, each joke once per device (`liftsmith.eggs`). **The 420 joke never shows to anyone under 18, or to anyone whose age the app doesn't know** (no birth year); the 1337 one ("leet", hacker speak) is for everyone. Not for a Log set tapped from the lock screen with the app closed (nobody would see it), not for warm-ups. A toast floats over the screen and asks for no tap, so it adds nothing to a set.
  - Kept off the features page and the blog on purpose: they're secrets.
