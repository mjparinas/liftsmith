# Your stats (Melvin, 2026-11-10)

Part of the decision record. [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) has the rules that apply to every change and the list of topics. Record decisions about this topic here, in the same change as the code.

- **One place for sex, age and height** (Melvin, 2026-11-10: "Adding the users age, height, sex on the TDEE calculator seems wrong. There should be a main area that allows the user to add their stats and calculators and other pages that require it should grab it from there"). **Your stats** is the first section of **Settings › Body and units** (`BodyStats.tsx`), reached directly at `#/settings/stats`. It stores `profile.sex`, `profile.birthYear` (from the age typed) and `profile.heightCm`. Weight is the latest weigh-in (logged on Progress, and the Body weight row below Your stats goes there), body fat is on the weigh-in.
- **Screens that need them read the profile and never ask again.** TDEE shows them read only with one "Edit your stats" button; DOTS, fitness age and the age-adjusted strength score do the same. When something is missing the screen says what (`StatsNeeded`: "This needs your sex, age, and height…") with one "Add your stats" button to `#/settings/stats`. The setup wizard still asks age and sex once, writing to the same fields. DOTS no longer has its own formula picker: without a sex it shows no score and the link (was a local Men/Women picker, 2026-11-09).
- **Height follows the units setting**: centimetres with kg; **feet and inches** with lb (two bump boxes, inches carry into feet both ways, so holding + past 11 in goes to the next foot). Stored in cm either way (`ftInToCm`, `cmToFtIn`, `fmtHeight` in `units.ts`).
- **Filled from health data as far as each platform allows.** On every open, and from the "Fill in from Health Connect / Apple Health" button, `readBodyStats` (`health.ts`) reads the newest height, and on iOS sex and date of birth through our `HealthWorkout` plugin's `characteristics` (the shared health plugin can't read characteristics; the button asks for them, the launch read doesn't). `bodyUpdates` (`tdee.ts`) applies them: a health height replaces the profile's unless the lifter typed one after it was measured (`profile.heightAt`); sex and birth year fill only when Your stats has none, so what the lifter set wins. Health Connect has no sex or date-of-birth records, so Android says so under the button and the lifter enters them ([Platform deltas](platform-deltas.md)). Weigh-ins and body fat keep their one import path (`importHealthBody`).
- The public TDEE page on the website keeps its own boxes: visitors have no profile.
