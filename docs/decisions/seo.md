# SEO: ranking and click rate (2026-11-17)

Part of the decision record. [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) has the rules that apply to every change and the list of topics. The pages themselves (which exist, which are indexed, the sitemap) are in [Public pages and SEO](public-pages-and-seo.md); this file is about how they're written to rank and get clicked. Record decisions about this topic here, in the same change as the code.

## Where we started

Melvin pasted the admin Search panel on 2026-11-17 (Oct 18 to Nov 14, about four weeks of data on a new domain): 15,491 times shown, 3 clicks, average position 19.4, most searches at position 50 to 90, 3,680 sitemap pages. The "Worth a better title" list was empty because almost nothing showed on page one. So the problem is ranking first and click rate second. The pages that did rank 6 to 25: `/exercises/box-squat/alternatives/` (11.5), `/exercises/db-row/alternatives/` (25.5), `/exercises/towel-row/` (27.3), `/blog/beginner-gym-workout-plan/` (9.5), `/blog/30-minute-strength-workout/` (6.5). Searches with impressions included "ab wheel rollout", "1 rep max calculator", "2 plates and a 25 on each side", "7 plates on each side" and "one arm db row alternative".

## What changed (2026-11-17)

- **Titles say what people search** (`src/lib/exerciseMeta.ts`). Exercise pages: `<Lift>: how to do it, muscles worked, <n> alternatives` (lifts with a guide: `…, sets and reps`). Alternatives pages: `<n> best <lift> alternatives (home and gym)`. Each title and description is the first of a list of candidates that fits search-result length (`SEO_TITLE_MAX` 60, `SEO_DESCRIPTION_MAX` 158), so long names fall back to a shorter form instead of being cut. `exerciseMeta.test.ts` checks every one of the ~1,000 exercises.
- **Descriptions lead with the answer.** An exercise page's description starts with the muscles it works ("The doorway towel row works the upper back and lats."), and an alternatives page's names the best three swaps ("Can't do the one-arm dumbbell row? Try the …"), so the snippet answers the search before the click.
- **Every exercise page answers questions, with FAQ data.** Lifts with a guide keep their written FAQ; the rest get three answered from their own data (`exerciseFaq`: muscles worked, equipment needed, what to do instead). Alternatives pages get their own (`alternativesFaq` in the page: the best swap and why, a no-equipment swap, a dumbbell, machine or kettlebell swap where one exists, which muscles a swap should train). Both carry `FAQPage` JSON-LD next to the existing `HowTo` and `ItemList`. Google shows FAQ and HowTo rich results only for a few sites now, so the point is the answers on the page, not the rich result.
- **More alternatives pages in search** (Melvin confirmed, 2026-11-17: "pr 575 is fine"). Changed from 2026-11-12 (only the ~50 guided lifts): `indexAlternatives` now also indexes the lifts in pick weights' `STAPLE` and `COMMON` lists with at least three alternatives, 242 pages (from 50), because the pages now differ from each other (named swaps, FAQ) and Search Console showed people searching alternatives to everyday lifts without a guide (the box squat was ranking 11.5 while `noindex`). The niche rest stay `noindex, follow`. Revisit if Search Console's "Crawled, currently not indexed" grows with alternatives pages.
- **Blog posts can have a search title** (`seoTitle` on `Article`): the headline stays on the page and in share cards, the search title carries the words people type. Set for the beginner plan ("Beginner gym workout plan: 3 days a week for 8 weeks"), the 30-minute workout, the dumbbell home workout, the no-equipment workout and lifting on a cut. Use it only when the headline misses the search words; keep it under 60 characters.
- **Calculators aim at their searches.** The one-rep max page is titled "1RM calculator: one rep max calculator for any lift" and answers "What is a one-rep max?". The plate calculator has a **plates per side chart** (1 to 8 plates a side: 45s, 45s and a 25, 45s and a 10, 20 kg and 25 kg plates; `src/lib/plateChart.ts`) and answers "How much is 3 plates?", "2 plates and a 25 on each side?", "7 plates on each side?" and "a plate in kg?" from the same numbers.
- **Internal links to the pages that rank.** The home page (every language) has a Free guides section linking 12 popular exercise guides (`HOME_GUIDES` in `HomeView.tsx`, each must have a guide), the plate and 1RM calculators, programs and muscles. The exercises hub lists the guided lifts first. Exercise pages list the blog posts that link to them (`articlesLinking`), so links go both ways between a post and the lifts it teaches.

## What to watch in Search Console

Give it two to four weeks after the release (Google recrawls a new domain slowly; IndexNow covers Bing).

- Performance › Pages: position and clicks for the five pages above, and for `/tools/plate-calculator/` and `/tools/one-rep-max-calculator/`.
- Performance › Queries: "plates" searches and "<lift> alternative" searches moving up; the admin's "Worth a better title" list filling as pages reach page one (that's the next round: rewrite those).

## Audit, 2026-11-20

Melvin asked to "go through the website and improve seo". A crawl of the live site and its sitemaps found four gaps, fixed together:

- **Gym addresses in other scripts.** `slugify` kept only a to z and 0 to 9, so every city written in Cyrillic, Greek, Korean, Japanese, Persian and the rest fell back to one folder: `/gyms/gyms/` was one city page titled "Gyms in Рубцовск" for 5,050 indexed gyms from Kyiv, Seoul, Sofia, Tokyo and Karaj, most with a bare id for an address. Now `translit.ts` spells Cyrillic, Greek, Armenian, Georgian, Korean Hangul (Revised Romanization) and Japanese kana (Hepburn) in Latin letters first ("Санкт-Петербург" gives `sankt-peterburg`, "서울특별시" `seoulteukbyeolsi`), and a city no table can spell (Chinese characters, Arabic script, Thai) gets a short stable folder of its own, `city-<8 hex>`, never a shared one (`citySlugOf`). Latin names give exactly the address they gave before, so no existing page moves except those that were wrongly filed. **After the release, press Fix city names once** (Admin › Gyms › Gym list): it now counts these gyms and moves each one with a 301 from its old page (`moveToCity`, `gym_moves`), the same path as any other city fix. The page itself still shows the name as written.
- **The gym sitemap was at 46,745 of the 50,000 addresses one sitemap may hold**, and a sitemap over the limit is skipped whole. `/sitemap-gyms.xml` is now a sitemap index (`gymSitemap.ts`): part 1 has `/gyms/`, the cities, chains, trainers, coaches' programs, the community and the routine library, and the parts after it the indexed gym pages, 20,000 each (`SITEMAP_PART`, `/sitemap-gyms-<n>.xml` through `api/gyms/sitemap-part/[part]`). The 45,000 cap on gym pages went up to 200,000. Search Console and robots.txt keep the same address. (A folder named `sitemap/[part]` doesn't work: Next treats a dynamic segment under `sitemap` as its own metadata route and answers 404.)
- **Page language.** The shared root layout writes `<html lang="en">` into every page, and `DocLang` corrects it only once the page's script runs, so the HTML as served said English on all nine translated languages (the content inside is already marked with `<div lang>`). Every page under `/es/`, `/fr/` and the rest now answers with a `Content-Language` header for its language (`next.config.ts`), which Bing reads. A post-build rewrite of the HTML was tried first and dropped: on Vercel the prerendered pages aren't at `.next/server/app/*.html` when `postbuild` runs (its build log says "Script policy added to 0 pages"), so it would have done nothing in production.
- **Gym, city and chain pages carry their breadcrumb trail as `BreadcrumbList`** data (`crumbsLd` in `gymPage.ts`, the same items as the visible trail), so results can show "Gyms › Toronto, ON" instead of the address. A gym's `ExerciseGym` data also names its logo (`image`) and its chain (`brand`, linking the chain page), and robots.txt allows `/api/gyms/photo/` (the one API path search engines may read) so those pictures can be crawled.

## Indexing and the impressions swing, 2026-11-27

Melvin sent the Page indexing export (2026-11-22: 8,079 indexed, 6,448 not: 4,096 "Excluded by noindex", 1,275 "Crawled - currently not indexed", 1,066 "Discovered - currently not indexed", a handful of redirects, 404s and canonicals) and the Performance export for three months (151k impressions, 29 clicks), and asked how to improve the pages Google crawls.

What a crawl of every sitemap address found (3,931 site pages and a 1,500-page sample of the 46,745 gym pages): every one answers 200, is indexable and names itself as canonical. **Nothing in a sitemap is noindex**, so the 4,096 come from links Google follows to pages that are noindex on purpose: each gym page links its poster and "Report a problem" pages (always noindex), city pages link every gym including the bare ones and studios that #539 made noindex, and exercise pages link their alternatives page whether or not it's one of the 242 indexed ones. "Validation failed" on that bucket only means those pages are still noindex, which is right; it isn't an error to fix.

What changed:

- **robots.txt keeps `/gyms/*/*/poster/` and `/gyms/*/*/report/` out of crawling** (`GYM_TOOL_PAGES` in `robots.ts`): two noindex pages per gym, tens of thousands of crawl visits the gym pages need.
- **Links to pages that aren't indexed carry `rel="nofollow"`**: a gym that isn't `gymIndexable` in city lists and "More gyms", and an exercise's alternatives page that isn't `indexAlternatives` (exercise and comparison pages). People still reach them; crawlers spend their visits elsewhere.
- **Every gym page says something of its own** (`gymSummary` in `gymPage.ts`, `gp.sum*` in all ten languages): a plain line under the name built only from the gym's facts ("Crunch Fitness is a gym at 100 Main Street, Niagara Falls. It's one of 450 Crunch Fitness locations. It's open 24 hours a day, every day. The closest other gym is Planet Fitness, 0.8 mi away."). As many of those sentences as fit 158 characters are the page's meta description, so each result reads differently. "More gyms" now lists the city's gyms closest to this one first, each with how far it is (miles in the US, UK, Liberia and Myanmar, km elsewhere). Before, a gym page was about 95% the same template words as every other.

**The impressions swing isn't a ranking drop.** About 85% of the impressions come from queries no person types the same way twice: an exercise plus "for bartenders", "for left handed people", "for back sleepers" or "for graduate students" (136 of the top 1,000 queries), "at home", "workout plan", "how to do X correctly", and pairs like "static stretching vs dancing" or "wall sits vs wall sits". They land on desktop (138.5k of 151k impressions, 0.01% CTR), mostly from the US and India, at positions 5 to 10, and never click. That is the pattern of automated tools (rank trackers and AI answer engines expanding a keyword list), not people. The site has **no page** built for any of those templates (no `/for-<persona>/` pages, and `vsPairs` never pairs a lift with itself), so there's nothing to prune: Google matches them to the plain exercise pages (`/exercises/power-clean/` alone has 27.8k). Their rise and fall (16.8k a day on 2026-11-16, 8.2k on 2026-11-21, 18k on 2026-11-24) is those tools, while real clicks went from 0 or 1 a day to 4, 7 and 11 on 2026-11-22 to 24. Judge progress by clicks and by mobile, not by impressions.

What real searches show: plain lift names ("leg press", "lat pulldown", "preacher curl", "seated row") sit at positions 50 to 80, normal for a domain a few weeks old against long-established sites. "gyms near me" (2,120 impressions) and chain names ("anytime fitness", "snap fitness") already reach positions 6 to 10 on city pages, the strongest real lead.

**After this ships, in Search Console:** don't restart validation on "Excluded by noindex" (those pages are meant to be noindex; the count falls by itself as Google stops crawling them). Press **Validate fix** on "Crawled - currently not indexed" a week after release, once Google has recrawled some gym pages with their new text. "Discovered - currently not indexed" has no validation; it shrinks as crawl visits stop going to noindex pages. For the six "Google chose a different canonical", two 404s and three redirects, open each reason, export its example URLs and send them over: the export screen's examples list is the only place those addresses are named.

## Rules

- A new page template gets a title built for the search (what, then the intent words: how to, muscles, alternatives, chart, calculator) through a candidates list that fits 60 characters, and a description that answers first.
- Blog copy stays English only and never names another app; competitors appear only on compare, alternatives and import pages ([Public pages and SEO](public-pages-and-seo.md)).
- Structured data only describes what's on the page. No fake ratings, no `Product` for the app.
- A web address is made from a name through `slugify`, which spells other scripts in Latin letters; a name it can't spell gets a code of its own, never a shared fallback folder.
- A sitemap that can grow past 50,000 addresses is a sitemap index of parts.
- A link to a page that is noindex on purpose carries `rel="nofollow"`, and a tool page that is always noindex (a poster, a report form) is kept out of crawling in `robots.ts`.
- A bulk page (a gym, a city) opens with a line built from its own facts, never only template words, and that line is its description.
