# Today screen (Melvin, 2026-11-09)

Part of the decision record. [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) has the rules that apply to every change and the list of topics. Record decisions about this topic here, in the same change as the code.

- The greeting uses the lifter's first name when one is set ("Good evening, Melvin"). Setup's first step asks for it (optional), and Settings can change it.
- **Daily quote:** one quote a day on Today, directly below the readiness card (Melvin, 2026-11-11), about effort, health, hard work and discipline, from `src/lib/quotes.ts` (380 quotes, at least 365 so none repeats within a year; bundled, so it works offline). A fixed seeded shuffle picks the day's quote, so every device shows the same one and it changes at local midnight. Every quote has its author and, where known, its source; only documented quotes, with common misattributions left out (a test checks a few). Settings › Daily quote turns it off (`profile.quotes`).
- **Readiness colours** (Melvin, 2026-11-11: "go easy and as planned are both very similar in colour", and every "How do you feel?" chip looked the same). The readiness card speaks like a traffic light: **amber slows down** (Go easy, and Low), **blue is as planned** (As planned, and Okay), **green goes** (Push, and Great); **Sore is red** and **Unwell violet**. So a feel chip has the colour of the call it leans toward, and the two rows never say opposite things (green for easy in one row and for great in the other). Before, Go easy was cyan and As planned brand blue, only 54° of hue apart, and Push was ember. Tokens `--call-easy`, `--call-normal`, `--call-push`, `--feel-sore`, `--feel-unwell` (`CALL_STYLE` and `FEEL_HUE` in `theme.ts`) with dark, light and colour-blind safe values (amber, blue, teal, pink, lilac). Each call button's icon is always in its colour and the picked one has a stronger tint; each feel chip shows a dot in its colour, then its border, fill and tick once picked (`Chip`'s `hue`). `a11y.test.ts` checks the calls stay 75°+ apart in OKLab hue, the colour-blind set stays apart under all three kinds of colour blindness, and every colour is 4.5:1 text on the background and cards.
- **Done for today** (polish pass, Melvin 2026-11-12: "fix them all"). Once a workout has been saved today, Today's title becomes "Done for today" and the readiness card, length slider, plan and Start button give way to a card with what was done (sets, minutes, volume) and the mascot, the next workout and why (`NextUpCard`, the same card as the summary), the cool-down for three hours after, and a **Train again** button that brings the plan back. Picking a plan (a routine, "Same as last time", doing a past workout again) also brings it back: the store keeps `againAt`, and Today shows "Done for today" only for a workout that started after it. Coach's notes, the quote and quests stay.
- **No streak yet reads as progress, not 0** (same pass). Until the first goal week is met, the flame chip in Today's header is hidden and the goal chip says "{n} more this week to start a streak"; on Progress the Week streak tile becomes "This week · 1 of 4". After that they show the streak as before.
- **A calmer top of Today** (same pass). The readiness card has one "i" (on the call; its dialog explains the call and then readiness, through `Info`'s `also`), and the line under the call no longer repeats the headline and score: it says only what the call does to the plan (`callWhy(…, short)`, `why.short.*`); the full sentence stays for the home screen widget. "Something hurts?" moved into the card beside How do you feel? (still one control); while an injury is being trained around, the warning chip stays in the top row, and without the readiness card (fixed programs, done for today) the chip stays there too.
