# Engagement and juice (owner request, 2026-11-09)

Part of the decision record. [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) has the rules that apply to every change and the list of topics. Record decisions about this topic here, in the same change as the code.

- Duolingo-style loops and Gradelings-style juice: XP per set with PR and session bonuses, levels with titles, a training-week streak with freezes, PR celebrations (confetti, heavy haptic, fanfare), "plate club" trophies (2-plate bench, 3-plate squat, 4-plate deadlift, bodyweight bench), a post-workout crate of random cosmetics, weekly quests, customization (accent colours, plate and bar finishes, timer sounds), and opt-in nudge notifications (built: see "Reminders and lifecycle messages").
- **Streaks count weeks that meet the days-per-week goal, not days**, so rest days never break a streak and nobody is pushed to train sore.
- **The line we hold:** no tricks in billing (plain prices, cancel in two taps, no fake countdowns or pre-ticked upgrades), no notifications that pretend to be something else, and no reward for training a muscle marked Fatigued. Juice goes around and between sets, never adds taps during one. All motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- XP, levels, streaks, quests, leagues, crates and achievements are derived from events, never stored (`game.ts`, `achievements.ts`), so every device agrees after sync.
- **Built (2026-11-09, gamification thread):**
  - **Achievements are the centrepiece** (Melvin: "what about achievements"). 52 achievements with up to four tiers (bronze, silver, gold, platinum; a one-step badge is gold), 130+ badges in all, across Consistency, Strength (plate clubs that count 45 lb or 20 kg plates, whichever is kinder; body-weight ratios; the thousand-pound club), Volume, Records, Exploring, Training smart and Secret. Every one shows progress toward its next tier. 13 secret ones show as "Secret achievement" until earned. Each tier reached is worth XP (25/75/150/300). They appear on the Awards tab (`#/achievements`), on Done as they unlock, on Progress (closest three), and on the web dashboard's Achievements tab. Share links carry `<id>.<tier>` and still read old trophy ids.
  - **"Training smart" rewards recovery, not grinding:** easy days taken, full rests, rating exercises, hitting targets. Nothing rewards extra days beyond the weekly goal.
  - **Levels:** 50 on a curve (`levelAt(n) = 50·(n−1)^1.8`), with a title every few levels (Rookie … Mythic). Level-ups get their own banner, sound and big confetti on Done.
  - **Streak freezes:** one earned every 4 goal weeks, at most 2 held, spent automatically on a missed past week; a frozen week keeps the streak and the league but doesn't add to it. `goalStreak` also returns `atRisk` (the goal can't be met in the days left), `freezeReady` and `best`. Today warns when a streak is at risk. We never suggest back-to-back days to save one.
  - **Weekly quests:** three a week. "Train your goal days" is always one (never more than the goal); the other two rotate, picked from the week's date so every device agrees. 60 to 150 XP each.
  - **Leagues:** Bronze, Silver, Gold, Sapphire, Ruby, Emerald, Diamond, moved by weekly XP against your own league's bar (promote at 300 + 150·i, drop below 100 + 50·i; a frozen week never drops you). **No made-up rivals**: it would pretend to be something it isn't. Friend leaderboards wait for accounts and sync.
  - **Crates:** one per finished workout, opened by a tap on Done (never during a set). The prize is picked from the session id: accent colours, rest-timer sounds (classic, chime, arcade, gong), plate finishes (matte black, chrome, neon; the weight colour stays as a rim so plates stay readable). Rarity weights are 60/28/10/2 and shown plainly; a repeat turns into 25 XP. They're equipped in the Locker on Awards and saved as `profile.cosmetics`.
  - **During a workout, with no added taps:** a PR banner with a heavy haptic, PR sound and small confetti the moment a record set is logged (not on a first-ever session of an exercise), and an "almost there" line during rest when the planned reps at that weight are within two of a PR.
  - **Share cards:** workout, PR, badge, level and lift progress images, shared through one share sheet with a preview and privacy switches; see [Sharing](sharing.md).
  - **Lean mode** (`profile.lean`, runtime `juice.lean`, Settings › Celebrations): no confetti, celebration sounds, count-ups or pop-up motion. Rest-timer sounds and haptics stay. XP, streaks and badges keep counting.
  - **Haptics** go through `@capacitor/haptics` on Android and iOS and `navigator.vibrate` on the web, with a two-beat "celebrate" pattern for PRs, level-ups and rare crates. Sounds are synthesized with Web Audio on all three.
  - Reminders (streak at risk, quest almost done) belong to the reminders work and read `goalStreak` and `questsFor` from `game.ts`.
