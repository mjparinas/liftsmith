# Food from Health (Melvin picked it from the missing-features brainstorm, 2026-11-11)

Part of the decision record. [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) has the rules that apply to every change and the list of topics. Record decisions about this topic here, in the same change as the code.

- **No food logging in Liftsmith.** Food apps (MyFitnessPal, Cronometer, MacroFactor, Lose It! and friends) already write each meal's calories and protein to Health Connect and Apple Health. Liftsmith reads each finished day's totals and turns them into coach's notes, so a lifter who already logs food gets coaching on it for free, and nobody else sees anything new.
- **What is read:** calories eaten and grams of protein per local day, for the last three weeks of finished days (today is still being logged, so it's left out, like steps). Our own plugin does it on both phones (`HealthWorkout.nutrition`), because the health plugin reads food energy but not protein: Android adds up `NutritionRecord` energy and protein per day with Health Connect's aggregate (`HealthWorkoutPlugin.kt`), and iOS runs a daily statistics query for dietary energy and protein (`HealthWorkoutPlugin.swift`). Both dedupe across apps the store's own way. They arrive on launch with the other daily readings (`readDaily` → `readNutrition`, `nutritionReadings` in `health.ts`) as `health` events with metric `kcalIn` and `protein`, so they sync, back up and erase like every other health reading, and never reach analytics, feedback or share links.
- **Permission:** Android asks for `READ_NUTRITION` in the same Health Connect sheet as everything else (`dietaryEnergyConsumed` in `READ`; one Health Connect permission covers energy and protein). iOS asks for dietary energy and protein from our plugin right after the main Apple Health sheet (`askHealth`). Lifters who connected before this change are asked again the next time they tap Connect; until then nothing is read. Connected apps lists it as "Food: calories and protein". The web app reads nothing, as with all health data.
- **A day counts only when it looks fully logged:** 1,000 kcal or more (`LOGGED_KCAL` in `src/lib/nutrition.ts`). A half-logged day would otherwise read as a huge deficit or a protein miss.
- **Coach's notes** (`insights.ts`, free like every note):
  - **Protein short N days running:** the last 3+ logged days in a row were each under 85% of 1.6 g per kg of body weight (the low end of TDEE's protein range), the newest within three days. Says the average and the target, how many grams a day to add (rounded to 5 g) with everyday foods, and links to the TDEE tool's targets. A near miss breaks the run, so it doesn't nag.
  - **Eating too little to build muscle:** goal "build muscle", 4+ logged days in the last week averaging 300+ kcal under TDEE, and weight not already rising (0.25%+ a week). Advice: about TDEE's lean-gain number.
  - **A very big calorie deficit:** any other goal, the week's average more than 250 kcal under TDEE's cut number. Advice: the cut number to keep losing fat, and maintenance to hold.
  - **When a body note already tells the story,** the food numbers join it instead of a second note: "weight dropping fast" gets the week's average and how far under maintenance it is (300+ under), and "body fat creeping up" or "your waist is growing" gets how far over (150+ over). One finding, one note.
  - Calorie notes need a TDEE (sex, age and height in Your stats); without one only the protein note can show.
- **Play:** `READ_NUTRITION` is a new Health Connect permission, so the Health Connect declaration in Play Console needs it added (food totals for coach's notes), and the privacy policy (`public/privacypolicy.html`, `/privacy/`) names calories and protein. `storePolicy.test.ts` checks the manifest and the policy page.
- Pure web logic on top of one native method per phone, so Android and iOS get it in the same change.
- **A coach's targets win** (2026-11-13, [Trainer mode](trainer-mode.md) "Nutrition targets"): when a lifter's coach sets daily protein or calories, the protein note goes by the coach's grams and the calorie notes judge the week against the coach's number alone.
