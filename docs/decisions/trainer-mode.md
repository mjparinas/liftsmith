## Out of Labs (built 2026-11-12)

- **A page for coaches on the website,** `/coaches/` (`CoachesView.tsx`), in all six site languages (`/es/coaches/` and friends, in `LOCALIZED_PATHS`, text under `coaches` in `src/locales/site/<lang>.ts`), linked as "For coaches" in the footer. How it works in four steps, what a coach sees and never sees, what it costs (Pro, clients free), questions, and buttons that open the app on Coaching (`/app/#/trainer`). The pricing table gains two rows: training with a coach (free) and coaching clients (Pro).
- **Invited clients skip most of setup.** A coach's invite link (`/app/#/trainer/<code>`) on a fresh install keeps the code on the device (`coachInvite.ts`; also from the Play install referrer `utm_source=coach&utm_campaign=<code>` after installing from the web app's "Get the Android app" link, and typed in through the welcome screen's "Got a code from a coach?", which covers iOS where the App Store has no referrer). The welcome screen then says "Train with your coach" and asks to sign in first (sharing goes through the account). Setup's coach path (`Onboarding.tsx`, `CoachJoin.tsx`) is three steps instead of six: whose invite it is, the client's name and the same consent as Coaching (Share my workouts), then the gym and units, then days and body weight. The goal, experience and days come from the coach; reminders, heart rate and the analytics question keep their defaults; the save step is covered by signing in; and there is no Pro trial offer (a client doesn't need Pro). "Set up on my own instead" goes back to the usual setup. An account that was already set up and signs in on the welcome screen lands on Coaching with the code filled in.
- **What new clients start with** (Your clients › New clients start with): goal, experience and days a week as chips, saved on each tap (`coach_defaults`); tapping a picked chip again leaves that question to the client. The answers come back with the code's preview and join, and the client can change them later in Settings.
- **A note to each client.** On a client's report, **Your note** (up to 500 characters, plain text, `coach_notes`, one per coach and client; Clear note removes it). The client sees it at the top of Today ("From your coach", `CoachNoteToday`, above the coach's notes) with Open coaching and Got it, which hides it on the device until the coach changes it, and under Coaching › Your coach. Today's copy is fetched on open, on return to the app (at most every 15 minutes) and on sign-in, kept in localStorage, and forgotten on sign-out. Notes go when the pair unlinks or either account is deleted.
- Analytics adds `feature` `trainer` details `note`, `unnote` and `setup` (finished setup on the coach path). Pure web code, so Android, iOS and the web app get it together; no platform gap.

## Coaches' own way in, and counting coaches (built 2026-11-12)

Melvin asked whether coaches should have a separate sign-up and whether we should track lifter vs coach. He chose **one account for everyone** (many coaches lift, and a client can start coaching later), with coaches getting their own way in and the admin panel counting them:

- **The way in.** The coaches page's buttons open the app at `#/trainer`. Before setup that means "I'm a coach" (`coachSignup` in `coachInvite.ts`, kept in localStorage; on Android the Play link from that welcome screen carries `utm_source=coaches` as the install referrer). The welcome screen says "Coach your clients" and asks to sign in first, since clients find a coach through the account. Setup then asks the usual questions for the coach's own training without "Save your progress" and ends on the coaching screen. "I'm here to train" goes back to the usual setup and ends on Today.
- **Counting.** An account becomes a coach with its first invite (`coach_accounts`: account, time, and `via` = `coaches` when the app still has the coaches-page mark, else `app`). Clients are the accounts in `coach_links`. The Users tab shows the counts and each account's role, and the morning digest has a Coaching card ([Analytics and the admin panel](analytics-and-the-admin-panel.md)). Deleting an account deletes its row.

## How it works (stage 1, built 2026-11-11)

- **One screen, Settings › Coaching** (`#/trainer`, `Trainer.tsx`; one row on Settings under Connected apps). Signed out, it asks to sign in, because everything goes through the account and the client's synced events.
- **Your coach (client side, free):** enter a coach's code (or paste their link; `#/trainer/<code>` opens the screen with it filled in and looked up). Checking a code links nothing: it shows the coach's name and exactly what they will and won't see, then **Share my workouts** or Not now. Each linked coach has **Stop sharing**, which cuts access at once. Up to 3 coaches.
- **Your clients (coach side, Pro):** **Make an invite code** gives an 8-character code (no 0/O or 1/I, shown as ABCD-EFGH) that lasts a week and works for any number of clients until then; **Send invite** shares the link through the phone's share sheet (copies it where there's none), **New code** replaces it and **Turn off code** stops it. Clients are listed by the name on their profile; each opens their report. Up to 30 clients. Without Pro, the section is one row, Pro-tagged, to the paywall.
- **A client's report** (`#/trainer/c.<id>`, `clientReport` in `src/lib/trainer.ts`), action first: **What to do** lists, most urgent first, no workouts yet, gone quiet (7+ days), behind the weekly goal last week, missed reps three workouts running (the fatigue coach's note's test), up to two stalled lifts (`stalls` from `insights.ts`), new bests this month, and "On track" when nothing needs doing, each with one thing to do. Then workouts in each of the last four weeks against their goal, the last 10 workouts (date, minutes, sets, exercises, new bests) and this month's new bests in the coach's own unit. **Remove client** at the bottom.
- **What a coach sees** is decided on the server, field by field (`KEEP` in `src/server/coach.ts`): sets (exercise, weight, reps, targets, reps in reserve, set types, supersets), session start and end (minutes, program and routine, length, work and rest time), feedback, effort ratings, swaps and the client's own exercises (for their names), from the last year. Never sent: heart rate (on sets and sessions too), readiness and the day's call, gyms, the sets' injury share, notes, and every other event type (weigh-ins, body fat, measurements, health readings, cycle, injuries, pain, check-ins, photos). The weekly goal comes from the client's synced profile. `coach.test.ts` checks each of these.
- **The coach's phone checks what it gets** (`cleanEvents`): malformed events from another person's phone are dropped, and the client's own exercises are named from their events without entering the coach's library.
- **Server:** `GET/POST /api/coach/` (`route.api.ts`, signed in only). Tables `coach_invites` (code, coach, expiry; one live code per coach), `coach_links` (coach and client), and since 2026-11-12 `coach_defaults` and `coach_notes`. Looking up or using a code is limited to 20 tries an hour per account, so codes can't be guessed. Deleting an account deletes its invite and every link, either side (`coachCleanup`).
- **Platforms:** pure web code on the server and in the app, so Android, iOS and the web app get it in the same change, with no native code and no platform gap.
- Analytics: `feature` `trainer` with `invite`, `join`, `leave`, `remove`, `assign` and `unassign`. No names or ids.

## Routines from the coach (stage 2, built 2026-11-11)

- **The coach builds with the tools already there.** A routine is made the usual way (save a workout as a routine from its summary, or make one in Routines), then on a client's report **Routines for this client › Assign a routine** opens a sheet of the coach's saved routines; one tap sends it. No second routine editor (one control, one destination).
- **It travels as a routine share code** (`encodeRoutine`, the same `r1~…` code as shared routine links, [Programs](programs.md) and PR #231): exercises in order, set counts and supersets, no weights or reps, because the client's own progression fills those in. The server stores it in `coach_plans` (coach, client, name, code, when), checks it only loosely (`isRoutineCode`; the app decodes it), keeps up to 10 per client, and only for the coach's own clients.
- **The client** sees **From your coach** at the top of Your coach: each routine with who sent it and when. Tapping it opens the shared routine preview (`#/routine/<code>`, `SharedRoutine.tsx`), where one tap saves it to their routines, and they start it like any other. Saving stays the client's choice, and the routine is theirs to change.
- **The coach sees how each is going** (`planProgress`): workouts since it was assigned that have 60%+ of its exercises count as done, shown as "Done 3 times · last Nov 9" or "Not done yet". The client's saved copy has its own id on their phone, so the match is by what was trained. A routine given a week or more ago and not done yet becomes a **What to do** item ("Push day not started yet": check they saved it). The trash icon takes a routine back; unlinking either way removes the pair's routines, and so does deleting either account.

## Chat, body data and alerts (built 2026-11-12)

Melvin picked "Chat, body data, alerts" from the gap list against Hevy Coach and other coaching apps (2026-11-12), then asked for a 3D body of the client from their health data ("users need to approve this").

- **Chat** (`#/trainer/m.<id>`, `CoachChat.tsx`): plain-text messages between a coach and each client, from either side (Message on the client's report and on each coach under Your coach). Newest at the bottom, 100 a page with "Show earlier messages", up to 1,000 characters each, 120 sent an hour per account. The thread loads on open, on return to the app and every 20 seconds while it's on screen (nothing in the background, [Battery](battery.md)). Opening it marks it read; unread counts show on Coaching (on each client row and each coach) and as a "New message from" card on Today. Sending the first message asks for notification permission. Server: `coach_messages` and `coach_reads`; `GET /api/coach/?chat=<id>[&before=<ms>]` and the `send` action.
- **Body data, shared by the client's choice** (Your coach › Share body data with {name}, a switch per coach, off by default; the join consent says body data stays private until it's turned on). It sends sex and height from the profile and weigh-ins (with body fat) and tape measurements from the last year, checked field by field on the server (`bodyOf`); never photos, health readings, cycle or injuries. The coach's report then shows **Body**: a 3D figure shaped from those numbers (the same `bodyShape.ts` as Health's switch, [Health twin](health-twin.md)), the latest weight with its change over the 30 days before it, body fat, height and each measurement with its change, and a line that it's an approximate shape, not a scan. Turning it off hides it at once. Server: `coach_shares` and the `share` action.
- **Alerts to the coach** (Your clients › Client alerts, on by default; `coach_prefs`): a push when a client's phone syncs a workout that ended in the last 6 hours (each told once, `alertTrained` from `/api/sync/`), and once when a client linked for a week or more hasn't trained for 7 days (`alertQuiet` in the daily admin job), again only after they train. They open the client's report.
- **Pushes without Friends** (`notifyCoach` in `push.ts`, `push.coach*` text): the phones also send their push token to `/api/coach/` (`pushToken`), so coaching pushes reach someone who never opened Friends, and Friends' push settings don't apply. The web app has no push ([Platform deltas](platform-deltas.md)).
- Unlinking either way deletes the pair's messages, reads, share and alerts; deleting an account deletes all of them and its alert setting (`coachCleanup`).
- Analytics: `feature` `trainer` gains `message`, `shareBody` and `unshareBody`, `alerts` and `noAlerts`. No text, names or ids.

## Physio support (built 2026-11-13)

Melvin, 2026-11-13, from the list of new kinds of users: "add physios support" (physios and rehab clinicians: assign rehab programs, watch adherence and pain flags), and blog posts for it. Built on the same coach and client link, with no separate account type (one account for everyone, as above), so it stays compatible with other coach types such as school teams.

- **Trainer or physio** (Your clients › I coach as: Trainer, Physio; `coach_prefs.kind`, the `kind` action, `CoachKindPicker` in `CoachRehab.tsx`). A physio's client report puts **Pain and injuries** right after What to do, and the assign sheet opens on rehab programs at 3 times a week. Clients see "Physio" beside that coach's name. Nothing else changes, and it's still the Pro feature `coaching`.
- **Pain and injuries, shared by the client's choice** (Your coach › Share pain and injuries with {name}, a switch per coach, off by default; the join consent says they stay private until it's on; `coach_shares.pain`, the `share` action with `what: "pain"`). The server then adds `injury`, `injuryHealed`, `injuryReturn` and `pain` events to what the coach gets, field by field (`PAIN_KEEP` in `coach.ts`): body part, when it was logged and healed, graded returns, and each 0 to 10 rating with its session and exercise. Never the client's skip or lighter choices, and never health readings, cycle, notes or photos. The coach's phone checks every event again (`cleanEvents`).
- **The report** (`rehabInjuries`, `ClientPain`): each injury, active first, then ones healed in the last 90 days, with the latest rating, a sparkline of the last 14 (the same one as the injury card), the change across them, and the latest rating on each exercise. **What to do** gains, first of all: pain 5 or more in the last week ("Knee: pain 6/10 this week", check in before their next session), pain up 2 or more across the latest ratings (ask what changed), and an injury logged this week. Pain 5 is the same line that stops a graded return ([Injury mode](injury-mode.md)).
- **A push for high pain** (`alertPain`, called from `/api/sync/`): a rating of 5 or more, rated in the last 6 hours, from a client who shares pain with that coach, at most once in 20 hours per pair, following the coach's alerts switch (`push.coachPain`).
- **Rehab programs** (`src/lib/rehab.ts`): six gentle starting points, knee, lower back, shoulder, ankle, hip, and neck and upper back, each 5 bodyweight exercises at 2 or 3 sets that need only a wall or the floor (`rehab.test.ts` checks), picked from the existing library. They're described as starting points to adjust, not a treatment plan. A coach assigns one in a tap from the assign sheet (physios see them first, trainers after their own routines). The stretching library isn't part of them, because stretches aren't exercises and don't travel in a routine code; a physio can still point a client at it.
- **Rehab routines keep their exercises.** A rehab program travels as the usual routine code with `~rehab` at the end (`encodeRoutine`/`decodeRoutine`); older apps read it as an unknown exercise and drop it. Saved from the preview, the routine keeps `rehab: true` (the preview says what that means, `rt.rehabWhy`). Picked for today, injury mode leaves it as written (no skips, swaps or lighter loads, `Plan.rehab` in `store/plan.ts`, and logging an injury mid-workout doesn't change it, `store/injuries.ts`), and the rest card asks for a pain rating after the sets of every exercise, for every active injury (`PainCheck`), so the physio sees pain exercise by exercise. This is a deliberate exception to injury mode's "skip until healed": the program was chosen for that area by the person treating it, and the client chose to save it.
- **Times a week and adherence** (`coach_plans.per_week`, `perWeek` on `assign`, `PerWeekPicker`): any routine can be given with 1 to 7 times a week, or none. After a week, the report shows "Kept to it 33%: 2 of 6 lately", counting workouts that follow it (the 60% match above) in the last two weeks, or since it was given, against what the target asks for in that time. Under half becomes a What to do item ("Knee: 2 of 6 lately"; ask what gets in the way, a shorter plan they'll do beats a long one).
- **No medical claims.** The pain section ends with "Liftsmith doesn't diagnose or treat anything; your own assessment decides what's right for them", the rehab programs say they're starting points, and the website copy says the same. The words are physio and rehab clinician (in French, kinésithérapeute).
- **Website and blog:** a For physios and rehab clinicians line on `/features/`, the coaches page's FAQ gains "Can physios and rehab clinicians use it?" and its body data answer says what can now be shared, and two posts in `src/content/blog/p.ts` (for physios: home program adherence and pain between visits; for clients: keeping up physio exercises at home).
- **Platforms:** pure web code on the server and in the app, so Android, iOS and the web app get it in the same change. The pain push reaches Android and iPhone; the web app has no push ([Platform deltas](platform-deltas.md), as for the other coaching alerts).
- Analytics: `feature` `trainer` gains `physio` and `trainerKind`, `sharePain` and `unsharePain`, and `rehab` (a rehab program assigned). No body parts, scores, names or ids.
- Unlinking either way deletes the pair's shares (pain included) and alerts; deleting an account deletes its coach settings, as before (`coachCleanup`).

## Nutrition targets (built 2026-11-13)

Melvin asked for "nutrition targets for clients" in a round of coach tools (2026-11-13, with payments, booking and check-ins).

- **The coach sets them** on a client's report (Daily nutrition targets): calories a day (800 to 6,000, steps of 50) and grams of protein a day (30 to 400, steps of 5), either may be left empty, Save or Clear (`coach_targets`, the `targets` action, `CoachTargets.tsx`). A number outside the range is stored as empty rather than refused.
- **The client sees them** under Your coach (Daily targets from {name}), with a line that food is logged in their usual food app. Liftsmith still logs no food ([Food from Health](food-from-health.md)).
- **Their coach's notes go by them** (`insights.ts`, `coachTargets` from `useCoachNotes`, kept on the device with the notes): the protein note uses the coach's grams instead of 1.6 g per kg, and with a calorie target the week is judged against it alone (10% under or over, "Eating under/over your coach's target", one tap to Coaching); the maintenance-based food notes stay quiet then, because the coach chose the number on purpose.
- **The coach sees how they're eating** only when the client shares body data, which now also carries the calories and protein from the last 30 days (`food` in `bodyOf`; the switch's text says so). The report shows the last week's average under the targets, and **What to do** flags calories 10% or more off the target or protein under 85% of it, over 4+ logged days (`foodFlags`).
- Unlinking or deleting an account deletes the targets. Analytics: `feature` `trainer` with `targets` and `untargets`.
