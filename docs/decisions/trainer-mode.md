Messages already went both ways (`coach.test.ts` reads one thread from each side).

## What a client shares, item by item (2026-11-13)

Melvin, 2026-11-13, on the coaches page's "You never see" list: "clients should agree to show things in the never see. technically they can share as much or as little as they choose". So everything beyond training is now the client's own switch, one per coach (`CoachShare.tsx`, `SHARE_KINDS` in `src/lib/trainer.ts` and `src/server/coach.ts`):

- **Always shared** (it's what coaching is): workouts from the last year, as before (`KEEP`).
- **A switch each:** heart rate (`hr`: each set's and workout's average and highest, and a workout's calories), workout notes (`notes`: `note` and `sessionNote`), readiness and recovery (`health`: the check-in on Today and Health's sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, steps, VO2 max and breathing; food stays with body data), body data (`body`, as before, with food), pain and injuries (`pain`, as before) and cycle (`cycle`: the days a period started). Each is a column on `coach_shares`, named only from that fixed list, never from the request.
- **Never:** photos, gyms and anything else in the event log.
- **Defaults:** links made before this keep what they had (only body and pain could be on). On the consent screen (Coaching and setup's coach path) the switches show before Share my workouts, with heart rate and notes on, because they're about training, and the rest off; what's on goes with the join (`shares`). Body data and cycle wait for an under-18 until body composition is on (teen.ts), as body data always has.
- **The coach's report** shows what's shared: heart rate on each recent workout, the client's notes under it, and a Readiness and recovery section (the latest check-in, 7-day sleep, HRV and resting heart rate, their cycle day). What to do adds "Feeling unwell" or "Low on energy" (check in, offer an easier day) and "Sleeping {hours} h a night" under 6 hours. The footer says what the client shares.
- **Website:** `/coaches/` "What you see, and what you don't" has three columns (You always see, Only if they switch it on, You never see) and the FAQ says the same, in all six languages; the client post in `blog/m.ts` too. Features page: `coach-sharing`.

## How it works (stage 1, built 2026-11-11)

- **Settings › Coaching** (`#/trainer`, `Trainer.tsx`; one row on Settings under Connected apps; since 2026-11-13 a page for each side, above). Signed out, it asks to sign in, because everything goes through the account and the client's synced events.
- **Your coach (client side, free):** enter a coach's code (or paste their link; `#/trainer/<code>` opens the screen with it filled in and looked up). Checking a code links nothing: it shows the coach's name and exactly what they will and won't see, then **Share my workouts** or Not now. Each linked coach has **Stop sharing**, which cuts access at once. Up to 3 coaches.
- **Your clients (coach side, Pro):** **Make an invite code** gives an 8-character code (no 0/O or 1/I, shown as ABCD-EFGH) that lasts a week and works for any number of clients until then; **Send invite** shares the link through the phone's share sheet (copies it where there's none), **New code** replaces it and **Turn off code** stops it. Clients are listed by the name on their profile; each opens their report. As many clients as the coach's plan allows ([Money](money.md) "Coach plans"). Without Pro, the section is one row, Pro-tagged, to the paywall.
- **A client's report** (`#/trainer/c.<id>`, `clientReport` in `src/lib/trainer.ts`), action first: **What to do** lists, most urgent first, no workouts yet, gone quiet (7+ days), behind the weekly goal last week, missed reps three workouts running (the fatigue coach's note's test), up to two stalled lifts (`stalls` from `insights.ts`), new bests this month, and "On track" when nothing needs doing, each with one thing to do. Then workouts in each of the last four weeks against their goal, the last 10 workouts (date, minutes, sets, exercises, new bests) and this month's new bests in the coach's own unit. **Remove client** at the bottom.
- **What a coach sees** is decided on the server, field by field (`KEEP` in `src/server/coach.ts`): sets (exercise, weight, reps, targets, reps in reserve, set types, supersets), session start and end (minutes, program and routine, length, work and rest time), feedback, effort ratings, swaps and the client's own exercises (for their names), from the last year. Never sent unless the client shares it (see "What a client shares, item by item"): heart rate (on sets and sessions too), readiness, notes, body data, cycle, injuries and pain. Never sent at all: the day's call, gyms, the sets' injury share, check-ins and photos. The weekly goal comes from the client's synced profile. `coach.test.ts` checks each of these.
- **The coach's phone checks what it gets** (`cleanEvents`): malformed events from another person's phone are dropped, and the client's own exercises are named from their events without entering the coach's library.
- **Server:** `GET/POST /api/coach/` (`route.api.ts`, signed in only). Tables `coach_invites` (code, coach, expiry; one live code per coach), `coach_links` (coach and client), and since 2026-11-12 `coach_defaults` and `coach_notes`. Looking up or using a code is limited to 20 tries an hour per account, so codes can't be guessed. Deleting an account deletes its invite and every link, either side (`coachCleanup`).
- **Platforms:** pure web code on the server and in the app, so Android, iOS and the web app get it in the same change, with no native code and no platform gap.
- Analytics: `feature` `trainer` with `invite`, `join`, `leave`, `remove`, `assign` and `unassign`. No names or ids.

## Routines from the coach (stage 2, built 2026-11-11)

- **The coach builds with the tools already there.** A routine is made the usual way (save a workout as a routine from its summary, or make one in Routines), then on a client's report **Routines for this client › Assign a routine** opens a sheet of the coach's saved routines; one tap sends it. No second routine editor (one control, one destination).
- **It travels as a routine share code** (`encodeRoutine`, the same `r1~…` code as shared routine links, [Programs](programs.md) and PR #231): exercises in order, set counts and supersets, no weights or reps, because the client's own progression fills those in. The server stores it in `coach_plans` (coach, client, name, code, when), checks it only loosely (`isRoutineCode`; the app decodes it), keeps up to 10 per client, and only for the coach's own clients.
- **The client** sees **From your coach** at the top of Your coach: each routine with who sent it and when. Tapping it opens the shared routine preview (`#/routine/<code>`, `SharedRoutine.tsx`), where one tap saves it to their routines, and they start it like any other. Saving stays the client's choice, and the routine is theirs to change.
- **The coach sees how each is going** (`planProgress`): workouts since it was assigned that have 60%+ of its exercises count as done, shown as "Done 3 times · last Nov 9" or "Not done yet". The client's saved copy has its own id on their phone, so the match is by what was trained. A routine given a week or more ago and not done yet becomes a **What to do** item ("Push day not started yet": check they saved it). The trash icon takes a routine back; unlinking either way removes the pair's routines, and so does deleting either account.

## Chat, body data and alerts (built 2026-11-12)

Melvin picked "Chat, body data, alerts" from the gap list against Hevy Coach and other coaching apps (2026-11-12), then asked for a 3D body of the client from their health data ("users need to approve this").

- **Chat** (`#/trainer/m.<id>`, `CoachChat.tsx`): plain-text messages between a coach and each client, from either side (Message on the client's report and on each coach under Your coach). Newest at the bottom, 100 a page with "Show earlier messages", up to 1,000 characters each, 120 sent an hour per account. The thread loads on open, on return to the app and every 20 seconds while it's on screen (nothing in the background, [Battery](battery.md)). Opening it marks it read; unread counts show on Coaching (on each client row and each coach) and as a "New message from" card on Today. Sending the first message asks for notification permission. Server: `coach_messages` and `coach_reads`; `GET /api/coach/?chat=<id>[&before=<ms>]` and the `send` action.
- **The send box stays at the bottom on phones** (Melvin, 2026-11-13: "the text input should stay fixed on the bottom and the viewport should scroll"). On a phone or tablet the thread is a pane the size of the visible screen (`.vv-pane` in `globals.css`): the header at the top, the send box above the tab bar, and only the messages scroll between them, kept at the newest unless the reader scrolled up, and "Show earlier messages" keeps the place being read. The pane follows the visual viewport (`useKeyboardViewport` in `src/lib/keyboard.ts`, `--vv-h` and `--vv-top`), so the on-screen keyboard pushes the send box up the same way in the Android app (Capacitor shrinks the web view), the iOS app and Safari (only the visual viewport shrinks) and Chrome; while the keyboard is up `<html data-keyboard>` hides the tab bar so the box sits right on the keyboard. Pure web code, so Android, iOS and the web get it together. On a desktop the thread stays a page with the box stuck to the bottom (and beside the list on the Messages page).
- **Body data, shared by the client's choice** (Your coach › Share body data with {name}, a switch per coach, off by default; the join consent says body data stays private until it's turned on). It sends sex and height from the profile and weigh-ins (with body fat) and tape measurements from the last year, checked field by field on the server (`bodyOf`); never photos, health readings, cycle or injuries. The coach's report then shows **Body**: a 3D figure shaped from those numbers (the same `bodyShape.ts` as Health's switch, [Health twin](health-twin.md)), the latest weight with its change over the 30 days before it, body fat, height and each measurement with its change, and a line that it's an approximate shape, not a scan. Turning it off hides it at once. Server: `coach_shares` and the `share` action.
- **Alerts to the coach** (Your clients › Client alerts, on by default; `coach_prefs`): a push when a client's phone syncs a workout that ended in the last 6 hours (each told once, `alertTrained` from `/api/sync/`), and once when a client linked for a week or more hasn't trained for 7 days (`alertQuiet` in the daily admin job), again only after they train. They open the client's report.
- **Pushes without Friends** (`notifyCoach` in `push.ts`, `push.coach*` text): the phones also send their push token to `/api/coach/` (`pushToken`), so coaching pushes reach someone who never opened Friends, and Friends' push settings don't apply. The web app has no push ([Platform deltas](platform-deltas.md)).
- Unlinking either way deletes the pair's messages, reads, share and alerts; deleting an account deletes all of them and its alert setting (`coachCleanup`).
- Analytics: `feature` `trainer` gains `message`, `shareBody` and `unshareBody`, `alerts` and `noAlerts`. No text, names or ids.

## Chat dock, statuses and stickers (built 2026-11-15)

Melvin asked (2026-11-13): "What do you think of Facebook Messenger / google meets style chats on the bottom right of the screen that is collapsible. This is for desktop. We need audible alerts when messages come through. Trainers should be able to set a status (offline, with a client, on vacation, etc). Ability to add emojis and stickers to chats? Can the stickers be gym / weight lifting themed. What else can we do here to improve delight?" Then (2026-11-15): "start on the messenger style chat. also add an online state, and other possible useful states".

- **The dock** (`ChatDock.tsx`), on the desktop layout only (the web app on a wide window with a mouse; phones, tablets and the native apps have the chat screen and push instead), for anyone with a coach or clients: a "Messages" bar at the bottom right with an unread count. Open, it lists everyone they can message (unread first, then newest), each with an initial, a status dot, and a preview line (typing, "You: …", a sticker's name, or their status), and the lifter's own status button at the top. A row opens a chat window beside it, up to two (`DOCK_WINDOWS`; a third replaces the oldest), each with the person's name and status as its title bar, which folds the window down to that bar, and a ✕. Which windows are open and folded is kept on the device (`ls.chatDock`). Hidden on the workout and on Coaching's own Messages page and chat screen. Pages on desktop leave room for its bar at the bottom.
- **Alerts:** a message that comes in plays the message sound (`sound.message()`, a soft rising fourth in the app's one voice, under the Sounds switch) once, however many places show it (`chimeFor` in `chatLive.ts`), puts the unread count in the tab's title ("(2) Liftsmith"), and opens that person's window folded with its count. Messages already waiting when the dock opens don't ring. The dock asks the server every 20 seconds while its tab is on screen and once a minute while it's hidden, the one exception to [Battery](battery.md)'s "nothing in the background", because an alert you only get when you look isn't one; it's desktop web only, and phones get a push.
- **Statuses** (`src/lib/coachStatus.ts`, picked in a sheet from the status button on the dock and on Messages, on every platform): Automatic (Online while the person has had coaching open in the last 2.5 minutes, then Offline with "Active 5 minutes ago"), With a client, Training, Away, Do not disturb, On vacation and Appear offline (shows Offline with no last active time). With a client, Training, Away and Do not disturb can last 1, 2 or 4 hours or until changed; a vacation takes the day you're back and shows "On vacation, back Fri, Nov 21". When the time is up it's Automatic again. **Do not disturb and On vacation hold chat pushes** to that person (the message waits in the chat), and the other side sees a note above the send box saying so. Anyone in coaching picks one, coach or client (Melvin's "trainers should be able to set a status"; clients going on holiday is just as useful). The dot is never the only cue: its words sit beside it or in its label. Statuses don't go to anyone who isn't linked.
- **Seen and typing:** under your newest message, "Seen 2:14 PM" once they've opened the thread after it (their read mark, `coach_reads`), "Sent" before. "Sam is typing" with three dots shows for 6 seconds after the other side last typed (the app reports at most every 3 seconds while typing; sending ends it). An open thread asks only for newer messages (`after`), every 5 seconds while the other side is online or typing and every 20 otherwise, only while on screen.
- **The message box** (Melvin, 2026-11-15: the box looked squashed) is one rounded field that takes the whole row beside a round send button and grows with what's typed up to about six lines. One smile button inside its end opens a tray with two tabs, Emoji and Stickers, so the words keep the room.
- **Emoji and stickers:** the Emoji tab holds emoji (gym and training first, `EMOJI` in `src/lib/stickers.ts`) that go into the box where the cursor is; the keyboard's own emoji work too. The Stickers tab holds 12 gym stickers (`STICKERS`: New PR!, Let's go!, Light weight!, On fire, So proud!, Morning!, Hmm…, Rest day, Hydrate!, Protein!, Form check?, Deload), our own drawings (`StickerArt.tsx`: the kettlebell mascot in four moods or one of our icons on a tile tinted in a supporting hue, with a caption in the reader's language), sent on their own with one tap and shown without a bubble. The server keeps the list and refuses one it doesn't know; a sticker's push says its emoji.
- **On a desktop, Enter sends** and Shift+Enter starts a new line; on a phone, return is a new line and Send sends.
- **Message buttons open the chat in place** (Melvin, 2026-11-26: "rather than taking you to a completely new screen to chat, it should expand the collapsed messages and open it directly to the coach's chat"; then "when the coach clicks on team chat, it opens it up in the messages"). Every Message <name> and Team chat button is one `MessageButton` (`MessageButton.tsx`): on the desktop layout it calls `openInDock` (`ChatDock.tsx`), which expands the list and opens that chat's window unfolded; on phones, tablets and the apps, with no dock, the same chat opens in a sheet over the page (closed with back, a swipe down or ✕). A team's chat is a dock window of its own (`tc:<team id>`, titled with the team's name, `TeamChat mode="dock"`), shown even when no one is in the list; its red count stays on the team's card. The chat screens (`#/trainer/m.<id>`, `#/trainer/tc.<team>`) are still there for pushes and links.

## Rehab protocols in phases, moved by pain (built 2026-11-17)

Melvin, 2026-11-17, from the gap list: "Physio rehab protocols with pain tracking" (it also closes "more areas and stages" from the 2026-11-15 rehab review). Each of the six rehab programs became a three-phase protocol, with one plain pain rule that says when to move, read from the pain ratings the app already collects plus a new day-after rating.

- **Three phases per area** (`phases` in `src/lib/rehab.ts`): 1 Calm and move (the original program, so codes and routines saved before phases keep their exercises), 2 Build strength, 3 Back to full use (lunges and hops). Every phase is bodyweight, a wall or the floor, 4 to 5 exercises at 1 to 3 sets, and each phase's exercises differ enough that a workout matches one phase only (`sessionPhase`, the same 60% match as an assigned routine; `rehabPhases.test.ts` checks both).
- **The protocol travels in the routine code**: `h:<part>:<times>:<protocol>:<phase>[:a]` before the rehab mark (`routineShare.ts`); older apps split the segment and still read the part and times. `Routine.rehabPlan` keeps `{ id, phase, self?, since }` on the phone; `since` (when this phase started) never travels.
- **The pain rule** (`phaseCheck` in `src/lib/rehabPhases.ts`, numbers as constants): ready to move up after 6 sessions in the phase (`PHASE_MIN_SESSIONS`, about two weeks at three a week) with pain 2 or less (`PAIN_EASY`) after each of the last 3 rated sessions and no flare the next day; back a phase after pain 5 or more (`PAIN_HIGH`, the same 5 that stops a graded return) after 2 of the last 3, or a flare the next day (5 or more, or 2 points above how it felt going in). Otherwise early, unrated or hold, each with a short why line built from the numbers (`phaseWhy`). The last phase says "going well" instead of moving on: the physio decides when they're done. It's a pain-monitoring rule of the kind physios use, shown as plain numbers, never a diagnosis; nothing is stored, it's read from the events every time.
- **The day-after rating** (`NextDayPain` on Today, `nextDayDue`): the day after a rehab workout that had a pain rating, one slider per injury it's for ("How does it feel today?"), saved as a `pain` event with `nextDay` set to that workout's session. It travels to a physio with pain sharing (`PAIN_KEEP` gains `nextDay`; `cleanEvents` keeps it) and counts as the "before" of a workout started within 12 hours, as any rating without a session does.
- **The physio decides by default.** On the client's report each protocol shows its phase and what the rule says (`withPhase`, `PlanPhase`), and ready or back goes into What to do (`rehabReady`, `rehabBack`, after the pain items). **Move to phase N** assigns that phase (same times a week) and takes the old one back, so the client gets the usual push and Today card. The rule needs the client to share pain; without it the row says so. The assign sheet picks the phase to start in (chips) and has **Let them move phases on their own** (off by default): with it on, the client's Today offers Move up or Back (`RehabPhaseToday`, `moveRehabPhase`), and the physio's report follows the phase their latest workout matched. Without it, the client's card says the physio moves them. "Got it" hides the card on the device until the answer changes.
- **A new phase replaces the saved one** (`saveShared` in `store/routines.ts`): a protocol routine with the same protocol keeps its id, so the rehab history, times a week, reminders and Today's offer carry on, and its phase counts from the moment it's saved. It fits even with the routine list full.
- Free for clients; assigning is still the Pro feature `coaching`. Teen-safe as before: pain stays private unless the client turns on sharing. Pure web code, so Android, iOS and the web app get it in the same change; no platform gap.
- Features page: "Rehab protocols in phases, moved by pain" (`rehab-phases`, Pro, clients free); blog post "Rehab in phases, moved by pain" (`blog/rehabPhases.ts`); weekly email W64 for physios (`physio-protocols`). Text in `src/locales/text/rhp.ts`, all ten languages. Tests: `rehabPhases.test.ts`, `trainer.test.ts`, `RehabPhase.test.tsx`, `Trainer.test.tsx`.
- Analytics: `feature` `trainer` gains `rehabPhase1` to `rehabPhase3` (a protocol assigned in that phase), `rehabPhaseMove` (the physio moved it), `rehabPhaseSelf` (the client did), `rehabPhaseSaved` (a new phase replaced the old) and `rehabNextDay`. No body parts or scores.

Watching asks the server for a presigned GET link valid for an hour (`issueSignedToken` plus `presignUrl`), only for the coach and the client of that video. `VIDEO_BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN` comes from connecting a **second, private** Blob store to the project with the prefix `VIDEO_BLOB` (gym pictures use the public store under `BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN`, and a store is public or private as a whole); without it the section stays hidden (`send: "off"`) and nothing breaks. **Connected through OIDC** (2026-11-19: Vercel's Connect Project now sets only `VIDEO_BLOB_STORE_ID`, plus a webhook key we don't use, and no read-write token): the server signs with the function's own OIDC token and that store id, and since a client token can't be signed without a read-write token, "start" hands back no token and the phone's Blob client (`uploadPresigned`) asks our route for a presigned PUT of that one path (`presignVideo`: only the client who started it, only while unfinished, only until its 30 minutes end, same size and type limits). That upload is one request rather than parts, because presigned links don't cover multipart. Either variable turns the feature on.
- **Notes pinned to moments.** Speed chips (0.25×, 0.5×, 1×), a bar under the video with a mark for each note, and **Add a note at 0:07**, which pauses and pins the note to that millisecond (kept inside the clip). Notes list in clip order; tapping one seeks there and pauses. Either side can add notes (the client answers on the same notes) and take back their own; 40 notes a video, 500 characters, 300 notes a day per account. Each note pushes the other side (`coachVideoNote`); a finished video pushes the coach (`coachVideo`).
- **What's waiting.** A video the coach hasn't opened and notes from the other side since you last opened it count with unread coaching messages: on the app icon (`badge.ts`), the coaching overview's `videos`, Today's coach badge and the desktop sidebar (`coachWaiting`), and as red counts on the section and each video. Opening a video clears its count.
- **Keeping it small.** The newest 12 per coach and client are kept (older ones go, file and notes), 10 a day per client, and an upload started but never finished is dropped after an hour. Either side can delete a video. Unlinking (either side, or a school team removing an athlete) and deleting or resetting an account delete every video and file between them.
- **Teen-safe:** like check-in photos, a client under 18 (`isMinor`) can't send a form video: the server refuses (`teen`), the client sees "Form videos are for lifters 18 and over" instead of the button, and the coach's report says clients under 18 can't send them.
- **Plans:** watching and pinning notes is a coaching tool, on every coach plan; sending is free for clients. Indexes: `coach_videos_pair`, `coach_videos_client`, `coach_video_notes_video` (in `dbIndexes.test.ts`).
- **No native code:** the camera is getUserMedia (the permission camera rep counting already uses on Android and iOS), the clip is a file input and playback is a `<video>`, so Android, iOS and the web get it together. Not built: drawing on the video, side-by-side compare of two videos, and filming from the workout screen; ideas for later.

## Payments through Stripe Connect (built 2026-11-13)

Melvin asked for "taking client payments, through Stripe Connect" in the same round.

- **Each coach has their own Stripe account** (a connected account on Liftsmith's platform with the full Stripe dashboard, `coach_stripe`, `src/server/coachPay.ts`, `CoachPay.tsx`). Under Your clients, Set up payments with Stripe makes the account and opens Stripe's setup pages in the browser (an Account Link), where Stripe asks for the coach's country, details and bank account. Liftsmith never sees them. When the coach comes back, Coaching asks Stripe whether the account can take payments yet; once it can, Open Stripe opens dashboard.stripe.com, where they sign in to their own account (payouts, refunds, receipts). The account is made with controller properties, not the legacy `type: express` (2026-11-15: Stripe refuses that for new platforms because it leaves the platform liable for negative balances): Stripe covers losses (`losses.payments: stripe`, Managed Risk), the coach pays Stripe's fees as the merchant (`fees.payer: account`), and Stripe collects the requirements. That rules out the Express dashboard as well (2026-11-16: production refused `stripe_dashboard: express` because Stripe allows it only when the platform collects fees and covers losses), so coaches get the full Stripe dashboard (`stripe_dashboard: full`, the same as a Standard account) and there are no login links. Production's Stripe key is a restricted key, so it needs Write on Accounts and Account Links as well as Checkout Sessions and Subscriptions.
- **Direct to the coach**: Checkout runs on the coach's connected account (`Stripe-Account` header), so the coach is the merchant: the money, receipts, refunds and disputes are theirs. Liftsmith keeps a small share only from coaches on plain Pro (Melvin, 2026-11-15: "a nominal platform fee for the 3 free clients, 2%" and "no cut is only for free ones, not paid ones"): `feeFor` in `coachPay.ts` gives the admin flag `coachPayFee` (default 2%) when coach plans are live and the coach's tier is Pro and isn't a school coach's, else 0. Coach and Coach Plus pay nothing, and nobody pays while the `coachPlans` flag is off. It's sent as `application_fee_amount` on a session and `application_fee_percent` on a monthly plan, and the payments card tells a Pro coach the percentage (`pay.fee`). `STRIPE_CONNECT_FEE_PERCENT` is gone.
- **Prices** are per client on their report: per session or monthly, in the coach's Stripe currency (1 to 10,000). A personal trainer's default is per session and a coach's is monthly (the role).
- **Paying**: the client sees Payments under Your coach and pays on Stripe's Checkout page in the browser, on every platform (`openOutside`). A session is a one-off payment; monthly is a Stripe subscription the client or coach can stop at the end of the paid month. Card details never touch Liftsmith or the app. Store rules: these are real-world personal training services, which both app stores allow to be paid outside in-app purchase; nothing digital in the app is unlocked by them.
- **The Connect webhook** (`/api/coach/stripe/`, its own secret `STRIPE_CONNECT_WEBHOOK_SECRET`) records `checkout.session.completed`, `invoice.paid`, `invoice.payment_failed`, `customer.subscription.updated` and `.deleted`, and `account.updated`. A payment only counts when the event comes from the account of the coach named in its metadata, and each is recorded once (Stripe resends events). The coach gets a push for each payment (`coachPaid`), and **What to do first** says when a monthly payment failed.
- **Ending**: unlinking ends the pair's monthly plan at once and forgets its price and history; deleting an account ends every plan it pays or is paid. Payments already made stay in the coach's Stripe account.
- **Hidden until set up**: with `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` and `STRIPE_CONNECT_WEBHOOK_SECRET` both set, payments show; until then nothing about them shows anywhere. Product names on Stripe's page are in English for now ("Training session with Sam").
- Analytics: `feature` `trainer` with `payOnboard`, `payDashboard`, `price`, `unprice`, `payCheckout` and `payStop`.
- **Programs** (2026-11-15): coaches also sell programs through the same Stripe account, on the website only. See [Coaches' programs](coach-programs.md).

## Assign workouts by date (2026-11-25)

Coaching › Programs starts with a workout calendar. A coach picks a month, an audience and a date, then saves a routine or a rest day. The calendar accepts assignments roughly nine months ahead. An athlete's own assignment takes priority over a team assignment, which takes priority over a coach's assignment to all athletes. A date with no assignment leaves the athlete's usual plan available. Today puts the scheduled workout or rest day before the usual plan. Assigned routines use the same preview, progression and workout logging as other routines. Android, iOS and the web app share the same calendar.

The athlete's report shows four weeks of calendar adherence alongside the existing exercise, lift and consistency history. A workout counts as completed when the athlete logs a completed session from its scheduled routine. Rest-day training uses the coach's local date when grouping sessions for the report.

A lead coach can invite an assistant for the selected calendar audience. The invitation code is single-use and expires after seven days. Assistants join from Calendar access and can schedule routines or rest days on that calendar. The lead can remove access at any time. Calendar access grants no access to athlete reports, messages, or payments.

Athletes › Training activity lists the whole linked roster by recent activity, with four-week workout counts and a link to each athlete's exercise and lift report.
