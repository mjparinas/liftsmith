# App size

Part of the decision record. [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) has the rules that apply to every change and the list of topics. Record decisions about this topic here, in the same change as the code.

- **The Android and iOS apps carry the app, not the website** (Melvin asked why the app doubled in size, 2026-11-11). `npm run build:static` exports everything to `out/`, and Capacitor copied all of it into both shells. By 2026-11-11 that was about 387 MB of files (about 110 MB compressed), and only 27 MB of it was the app: the rest was the public website, mostly the 754 exercise pages and their alternatives pages (275 MB of HTML and Next's page data), the muscle, equipment and blog pages, 25 MB of preview cards (`og/`) and 18 MB of exercise stills (`stills/`). The SEO work and the exercise library batches on 2026-11-11 (#247, #250, #251, #252, #253) added most of it, which is why the bundle jumped in one day. The apps never show those pages: they open straight into `/app/` and link to the website on `SITE_URL` (terms, privacy, share links).
- **How:** `scripts/native-web.mjs` runs after every Capacitor copy (`capacitor:copy:after` in `package.json`, which `npx cap copy` and `npx cap sync` both call, locally, in CI and in `release.yml`) and deletes, from that shell's copy only, every top-level entry of `out/` that isn't on its `KEEP` list: `app/`, `_next/`, `mediapipe/` (camera rep counting's runtime and model), the root `index.html` and `404.html`, the icons, the manifest and `sw.js`. Files Capacitor adds itself (`cordova.js`, `plugins/`) aren't in `out/`, so they stay. `out/` itself is untouched, because the browser tests and the website need all of it. Android and iOS get the same list in the same step. Result: 387 MB down to 27 MB of web files per app (about 11 MB compressed), and the full playthrough passes when served from the trimmed folder.
- **Adding something the app loads by a root path** (a new folder under `public/` that app screens fetch, like `mediapipe/`) means adding it to `KEEP`, or it works on the web and is missing in the apps. Anything new for the website only needs nothing. `src/lib/nativeWeb.test.ts` checks the pruning and the hook; CI's native builds also run when the script changes.
- **The camera engine stays in the apps** (Melvin, 2026-11-11: "Keep the camera engine"). Most of what's left is camera rep counting's MediaPipe runtime (14 MB WebAssembly) and pose model (5.6 MB). It stays bundled rather than downloaded on first use, so the camera labs work offline and from the first tap.
