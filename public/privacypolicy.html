Liftsmith privacy and health data

Liftsmith works offline. Your workouts, gyms and settings are stored on your phone. Your workouts and health data never leave it unless you sign in to turn on sync. Anonymous usage stats (screens and features used, device model, app version), which you can switch off in Settings, are described in the full privacy policy; they never include health data.

What we read from Health Connect or Apple Health

With your permission, Liftsmith reads your weight, height, body fat, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep and heart rate. We use them to suggest starting weights and, later, to adjust a workout to how recovered you are. These readings stay on your phone.

What we write

When you finish a workout, Liftsmith saves its active calories and average heart rate, so they appear alongside your other activity.

What we never do

We never sell health data, use it for advertising (Liftsmith has no ads), or share it with anyone. You can remove access at any time in Health Connect or the Health app, and delete everything Liftsmith stores from Settings.

The full privacy policy is at www.getliftsmith.com/privacy.
